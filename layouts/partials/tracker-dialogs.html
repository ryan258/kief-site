Save the counters you see?

Preserve the unreadable original in browser recovery storage, then replace the active save with your current counters. No rest is performed. Export tracking first for a separate downloadable copy.

Import session backup.

Restore your character sheet from a downloaded JSON file or paste backup JSON. Your current state is automatically preserved in recovery storage before importing.

Session history & snapshots.

Automatic rolling snapshots captured during play. If an accidental rest, double-cast, or errant tap happens at the table, restore any prior moment with one click.

AFTER A COMPLETED LONG REST

Put the kettle back on?

Restore HP, slots, SP, Hit Dice, daily spell uses, and Sorcerous Restoration. Clear concentration and Innate Sorcery; mark your Reaction available. Gold and Mr. Big’s recorded HP/location stay as you left them.

Confirm the rest is complete at your table before resetting. You can undo the reset until your next tracker change.
