8 cantrips · 9 Sorcerer preparations · 6 Draconic extras · 2 origin spells. Each card includes casting, targets, components, duration, resolution, scaling, and an official or licensed 2024 reference checked September 17, 2026.

Kief’s Sorcerer spells use **DC 14 / attack +6**, or **DC 15 / attack advantage** while Innate Sorcery is active. Species and feat spells do not receive that Sorcerer-only benefit. Cantrip damage shown is for character level 5. Reading a card does not cast a spell or spend resources. [Table rulings](/rulings/) records unresolved interactions; campaign equipment is still pending.
