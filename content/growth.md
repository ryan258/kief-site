Current starting point: Sorcerer 5, Candy Land campaign. Local build updated 2026-09-16, premise recorded 2026-09-23; live-sheet sync pending. The previous level 7 improvement plan is in the archive.

## Before play

Complete [D&D Beyond setup](/setup/), confirm starting gear and Mr. Big's form, and tell teammates: Kief brings control, scouting support, mobility, fall rescue, and Counterspell. He does not bring healing magic.

Two things to raise with this DM specifically, because they change what the build is worth: how a curse can be ended at this table, and which travel rules are in use. Neither is a build decision — both are recorded as open items in [campaign checks](/checks/).

## After learning the party

Keep the starting nine preparations through the opening sessions unless the DM permits a rebuild. At an ordinary Sorcerer level-up, replace at most one class preparation and one Sorcerer cantrip. Watch for duplicate party coverage: Detect Magic is the first preparation to reconsider if another character already handles it reliably. Consider a targeted control or support spell for the uncovered need rather than adding another damage spell by default.

## Level 6 — next milestone

- 56 fixed HP, 6 SP, slots 4/3/3, ten class preparations.
- Elemental Affinity arrives; intended choice remains Fire. Only then add fire resistance and +3 to one fire-damage roll of an eligible spell.
- Sorcerous Restoration becomes up to 3 SP per use.
- Choose the tenth spell after actual party experience; Slow is a useful candidate for selective control if charm or fear effects have been poor fits. It calls for a Wisdom save and does not impose either condition, but check each creature's actual saves, resistances, and immunities rather than assuming a creature type decides the matchup.

## Level 7

Sorcery Incarnate, fourth-level slots, and the Draconic Arcane Eye / Charm Monster pair arrive. Reassess the archived higher-level tactics against the actual sheet before restoring them. Dimension Door is a strong candidate for the first fourth-level class preparation because it adds an ally extraction option.

## Level 8

Default ASI recommendation: +2 Charisma, 16 to 18. This improves DC, spell attack, Draconic AC, Persuasion, and Careful's protected-creature count. Revisit only if play establishes a more pressing need.
