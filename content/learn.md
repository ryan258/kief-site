Learn enough to join the scene, take a useful turn, and come back after losing your place. Kief Firelight—a tea merchant with draconic magic and a very small, silent cow—is our worked example.

**Rules baseline:** the revised 2024 fifth-edition rules, called “5.5e” here. **Character baseline:** this repository’s intended level 5 Forest Gnome / Draconic Sorcerer / Sage build, checked against the local records on October 14, 2026. The DM’s campaign ruleset, starting equipment, and live D&D Beyond reconciliation remain pending. Recorded campaign rulings apply where they change the published rules.

This is a learning guide. The [character record](/character/) owns Kief’s build; [Table rulings](/rulings/) owns the recorded campaign decisions. Examples below are invented practice scenes, not events that have happened in the campaign.

## Start small, or return here

For a first read, work through **the conversation, the dice, and one combat turn**. Try the practice scenes when you feel ready. Everything else is a reference to revisit as it comes up.

When it is your turn and you have lost the thread, say:

> “What changed, and what does the party need from Kief?”

Then check four things:

1. **Situation:** Where am I, and what is threatening us?
2. **Concentration:** Am I already keeping a spell running?
3. **Choice:** What one useful thing can I do now?
4. **Update:** What resource did I spend, and what changed?

Keep the [level 5 table card](/rules/) beside this guide during play. You can ask someone to read a spell or track a counter while you make the decision.

## 1. The game starts with a conversation

The Dungeon Master, or **DM**, describes the world and resolves what happens. You decide what Kief tries. Other players decide what their characters try. Together, those characters are the **party**. An **NPC** is a nonplayer character controlled by the DM.

The basic rhythm is: **hear the situation → describe an attempt → resolve it → respond to the result**. Sometimes an attempt simply works; sometimes the DM calls for dice. See the official [rhythm of play](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#RhythmofPlay).

For example:

> **DM:** “The candy seller stops talking when you mention the missing Princess.”
>
> **You:** “Kief offers him some tea and asks what he’s afraid of.”
>
> **DM:** “Make a Persuasion check.”

You have already done the central part of playing: you chose an approach. You do not need an accent, a speech, or the name of a rule. “Kief tries to reassure him” is enough.

A check can influence the conversation. It does not give Kief control of another person’s decisions. Listen to the answer and give another player room to follow up.

## 2. Understand the dice without memorizing the sheet

**d20** means a twenty-sided die. **2d10** means roll two ten-sided dice and add them. A modifier such as **+6** is added to the roll.

For most uncertain attempts, roll **d20 + the relevant bonus**. Meet or beat the target number: a **Difficulty Class (DC)** for a check or save, or **Armor Class (AC)** for an attack. Advantage uses the higher of two d20s; disadvantage uses the lower. They cancel when both apply. See [D20 Tests](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#D20Tests).

### Three rolls that look similar but do different jobs

| Kind | What it answers | Kief example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ability check | Does my attempt work? | Persuasion to reassure the seller: d20 +6 |
| Attack roll | Does my attack hit? | Fire Bolt against an enemy’s AC: d20 +6 |
| Saving throw, or save | Can I resist this effect? | Constitution save to keep concentration: d20 +7 |

**Worked check:** the DM sets Persuasion DC 15. You roll 11, add Kief’s +6, and report **17**. The DM describes the result. Report the total rather than deciding the NPC’s response yourself.

**Worked attack:** you roll 10 for Fire Bolt, add +6, and report **16 to hit**. If the DM confirms a hit, roll **2d10 fire damage**. The +6 belongs to the attack roll; it is not added to damage. Kief has no Elemental Affinity damage bonus at level 5.

**Worked enemy save:** Hypnotic Pattern asks the enemies to save. The DM rolls their Wisdom saves against Kief’s DC 14, or DC 15 with Innate Sorcery active. You do not also roll a spell attack.

Use the printed total bonus. Kief’s +6 spell attack already includes his Charisma modifier and proficiency; adding either again would count it twice.

## 3. Read Kief’s numbers as answers to questions

You can play from these numbers before learning how character creation produced them. They come from the [current character record](/character/#build-and-numbers).

| Question | Current answer |

| How much damage can I take? | Maximum **47 Hit Points (HP)**; track current HP separately |
| How hard am I to hit? | **AC 15**, temporarily **20** with Shield |
| How far can I move? | **30 feet** on an ordinary turn |
| What do I roll for turn order? | **Initiative: d20 +2** |
| What do I add to Fire Bolt or Ray of Frost attacks? | **+6** |
| What number do enemies save against? | **DC 14**, or **15** for Sorcerer spells during Innate Sorcery |

The six abilities describe different strengths. For Kief, Strength is −1, Dexterity +2, Constitution +4, Intelligence −1, Wisdom +0, and Charisma +3. His trained skills include Persuasion +6, Insight +3, Arcana +2, and History +2. The DM chooses the check appropriate to the attempt.

His tea-merchant history supplies ideas and connections. It does not grant Herbalism Kit proficiency or healing powers. Invite a teammate to handle a task they are better equipped to attempt.

## 4. Take one combat turn

**Initiative** establishes turn order. A **turn** is one participant’s opportunity to act; a **round** cycles through everyone. “Once per turn” and “once per round” are different limits.

Think of Kief’s ordinary turn as these available pieces:

| Piece | A useful Kief example |
| --- | --- |
| Movement | Reposition within his 30-foot Speed |
| One Action | Cast an Action spell, Dodge, or work on the objective |
| At most one Bonus Action, when a feature permits it | Activate Innate Sorcery or cast Misty Step |
| One Reaction, when its trigger happens | Shield, Feather Fall, or Counterspell; available again at the start of Kief’s next turn |

Movement can be split around actions. A Bonus Action needs a rule that grants it; unused pieces do not have to be spent. For the general turn structure, see [Combat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#Combat).

Before casting, check the spell’s range, target, area, components, and concentration requirement. Point out affected allies before resolving an area spell. Ask about cover and terrain when positioning is unclear.

### A first turn, with the bookkeeping visible

**Practice situation:** enemies are clustered, a friend is among them, and Kief has all resources available. The group wants time to escape.

1. Say: **“Innate Sorcery, then Careful Hypnotic Pattern. I protect our friend.”**
2. Spend the Bonus Action and one Innate Sorcery use: **2 → 1**.
3. Spend the Action, one third-level slot, and 1 Sorcery Point: third-level slots **2 → 1**, SP **5 → 4**.
4. Place the 30-foot cube within 120 feet, checking who can see the pattern. Sorcerer save DC is now **15**. Careful protects up to three chosen creatures; name them.
5. The DM resolves the other creatures’ Wisdom saves. Record **concentration: Hypnotic Pattern** and identify affected enemies to the party.

Pattern leaves affected creatures Charmed, Incapacitated, and at Speed 0. Damage or another creature’s action to shake them ends the effect on that creature. It does not make them Unconscious. See [Hypnotic Pattern](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions#HypnoticPattern).

Your next turn can be quieter: move toward the exit, attack an unaffected threat with a cantrip, or Dodge while friends get clear. A spell that is already helping the party gives you something worth preserving.

Innate Sorcery also grants advantage on Sorcerer spell attacks: roll two d20s, keep the higher, then add the same **+6**. It does not increase damage or require concentration itself.

If the enemies save, tell the party and adapt. You have still spent the resources; the spell’s failure does not automatically refund them.

## 5. Learn the three spell resources separately

**Character level** measures Kief’s overall progression. **Spell level** measures a spell’s tier. **Slot level** determines which spells that slot can power. A level 5 Sorcerer currently reaches third-level spells.

### Cantrips and spell slots

Cantrips such as Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, and Elementalism use **no spell slot**, though they still use their listed casting time. A prepared spell is available to cast; a slot is a resource spent to cast it. Casting Web does not remove Web from Kief’s preparations.

Kief starts fully rested with four first-level, three second-level, and two third-level slots. A higher slot can power a lower-level spell, but extra benefits depend on that spell’s text. A first-level slot cannot power third-level Fireball. See [spell slots and casting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells).

For this build, remember two combinations from [Table rulings](/rulings/#1-action-economy--spellcasting):

- **Innate Sorcery + Hypnotic Pattern:** legal with the required resources. Innate Sorcery is a feature, so only Pattern spends a slot to cast a spell.
- **Misty Step + Fire Bolt:** legal with the required resources. Misty Step spends a slot; Fire Bolt is a cantrip. Misty Step + a slotted Fireball on that same turn is unavailable.

The 2024 limit is **one spell slot expended to cast a spell per turn**. A reaction on an enemy’s turn occurs on a different turn, but you still need an available Reaction and slot. A reaction on Kief’s own turn shares that turn’s limit. See [one spell with a spell slot per turn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spells#OneSpellwithaSpellSlotperTurn).

### Components and rituals

A spell’s **V / S / M** entries mean spoken words, gestures, and materials. Check what the spell needs before attempting quiet or hands-occupied casting. Kief’s planned component pouch covers ordinary materials; priced or consumed materials still need to be supplied. His starting gear is pending, including the 50+ GP diamond for Chromatic Orb.

A **Ritual** casting adds 10 minutes and uses no spell slot when the spell and your feature allow it. Components still apply. Detect Magic is one of Kief’s ritual options when time permits. See the [character record’s equipment and casting notes](/character/).

### Sorcery Points and Metamagic

Kief’s **5 Sorcery Points (SP)** are a separate pool used to modify spells:

- **Careful, 1 SP:** protect up to three chosen creatures as described in the character record. It is a clear fit for Pattern or Fireball around friends. Careful Web remains a pending interpretation; keep allies out of the webs.
- **Subtle, 1 SP:** remove components except priced or consumed materials. Visible effects can still reveal what happened.

At level 5, Kief cannot combine this pair on one casting. Innate Sorcery and Metamagic are separate features: activating Innate does not make Metamagic free.

Slot conversion can wait until you know the basics. The exact costs are in the [character record](/character/#class-features-and-metamagic). Converting all 5 SP into one third-level slot leaves no SP for Careful or Subtle.

## 6. Concentration means keeping one effect running

Kief can sustain only one concentration effect at a time. Starting another concentration spell ends the first. Damage requires a Constitution save: **DC 10 or half the damage, rounded down, whichever is higher**, capped at DC 30. Becoming Incapacitated or dying also ends concentration. See [Concentration](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#Concentration).

For Kief, roll **d20 +7**:

- Take **8 damage**: the concentration save is **DC 10**.
- Take **26 damage**: the concentration save is **DC 13**.

Track the actual spell name. “Concentrating” alone will not tell you who is flying or which enemies are held.

Kief’s concentration choices include Web, Hypnotic Pattern, Fly, Invisibility, Detect Magic, Fear, Alter Self, and Dragon’s Breath. Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Shield, and Command do not replace concentration. Maintaining concentration itself uses no Action.

Before switching, say the consequence: **“Casting Fly will end my Web. Is that what we need?”** Be especially careful when ending Fly could leave a teammate falling.

## 7. Choose a useful spell by the job

Start with a few familiar choices from the [table card](/rules/#choose-one-useful-move). Add more when you want them.

| The party needs… | Consider… | Remember… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breathing room against a group | Hypnotic Pattern | Concentration; tell allies which targets to leave alone |
| A blocked route | Web | Placement needs support; keep friends outside |
| A simple attack while control stays active | Ray of Frost or Fire Bolt | Roll to hit; no slot spent |
| One enemy’s behavior changed briefly | Command | The target saves; follow the spell’s command options and limits |
| A teammate across a gap | Fly | Agree on a landing; Kief maintains concentration |
| A quieter approach | Invisibility | Coordinate with the recipient; it does not silence footsteps |
| A magical clue examined | Detect Magic | Concentration; detecting magic does not identify every curse or trap |
| A playful way into a scene | Minor Illusion, Elementalism, Shape Water, or Prestidigitation | Describe one effect within the spell’s limits |

**Three emergency reactions compete for the same Reaction:**

- **Shield:** with AC 15, an ordinary attack total of 15–19 can become a miss at AC 20. A total of 20 still hits; the AC increase does not cancel a critical hit. The protection lasts until the start of Kief’s next turn.
- **Feather Fall:** protect up to five falling creatures within 60 feet.
- **Counterspell:** contest qualifying visible casting within 60 feet. In this 2024 build, the enemy makes a Constitution save; interruption is not automatic.

After using one, mark **Reaction spent** until Kief’s next turn starts. See the [table card’s emergencies](/rules/#emergencies-and-reactions) for the quick reference.

## 8. Explore and roleplay with one clear attempt

Try this spoken pattern:

> **“I want [result]. Kief tries [approach].”**

“I want the guard to trust us. Kief offers tea and explains who we’re looking for.” Or: “I want to understand this symbol. Kief compares it with what he remembers from his studies.”

Add a line of character when you want one:

> “Mr. Big has reviewed your proposal. He has concerns.”

The joke can accompany a useful decision. You can also describe Kief in third person or simply state the action. Roleplaying does not require performing a voice.

The DM has encouraged **wit and whimsy**. Shape Water has a recorded campaign extension to liquids, which makes candy-land experimentation especially promising. Try: **“Could Kief reshape a little of this syrup to show the route we took?”** The specific outcome still needs a ruling; the liquid extension grants no automatic damage, creature control, or permanent creation. See the [liquid ruling](/rulings/#shape-water-campaign-liquid-ruling).

Once the DM resolves an attempt, share the result and invite a teammate’s next move. Repeating a clever trick is optional; the scene may offer a better job for another spell or character.

## 9. Mr. Big is a companion with something to lose

Mr. Big’s silent-cow appearance is established character flavor. His detailed mechanics need the campaign ruling kept in view.

**Confirmed by the recorded DM ruling:** he is a physical, intelligent companion who can be summoned and put away. If he dies, he is gone, and Kief suffers an unspecified negative effect. He will not willingly sacrifice himself on command.

**Still pending:** his final stat block, exact death penalty, handling at 0 HP, and which published familiar mechanics carry over. The proposed Cat block has only 2 HP. Telepathy, shared senses, initiative, and touch delivery remain provisional under that ruling.

Give him a bounded job with a safe return: **“Stay beside us and watch that corridor; signal if something comes.”** Let him refuse danger. Avoid making disposable scouting or recasting him the solution to a hazard.

Use [Mr. Big’s guide](/familiar/) for jobs and [the confirmed override](/rulings/#confirmed-campaign-override) for the limits. Learning Kief’s own turn is enough to begin; add the familiar’s unresolved mechanics after the DM settles them.

## 10. Track damage, rest, and what remains

If Kief has 47 HP and takes 9 damage, mark **38 HP**. If concentration is active, resolve its save too. Kief has no healing spell, and his tea restores no HP. Potions, a healer’s kit, and historical magic items require confirmed ownership.

At **0 HP**, alert the table immediately. Unless instantly killed, Kief becomes Unconscious; concentration ends. Death saves normally begin on his turns: 10+ succeeds, three successes stabilize, three failures kill; a natural 1 adds two failures, and a natural 20 restores 1 HP. Further damage is dangerous. Stabilization does not wake him. Follow the DM through the situation using [Dropping to 0 Hit Points](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#Droppingto0HitPoints). Mr. Big’s 0-HP rules remain unresolved separately.

For ordinary recovery:

| Rest | What to update for Kief |
| --- | --- |
| Short Rest: at least 1 hour, subject to rest rules | Spend available Hit Dice for healing: each is **1d6 +4**, up to maximum HP. Sorcerous Restoration can recover up to **2 spent SP**, once per Long Rest. Ordinary slots and Innate Sorcery uses stay spent. |
| Long Rest: at least 8 hours, subject to rest rules | Restore HP and spent Hit Dice; replenish normal slots, SP, Innate Sorcery uses, and other Long Rest features according to their text. |

See [rest rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#LongRest) and [Sorcerous Restoration](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes#Level5SorcerousRestoration). A pause in play does not automatically establish that the characters completed a rest.

## 11. Practice one situation at a time

Answer aloud, or just think through the choice. These are rehearsal prompts with possible answers, not a required sequence.

### The worried witness

**Scene:** a baker knows something about the road but fears the local guards. What does Kief try?

**Possible answer:** offer privacy and tea, then ask one specific question. If the DM calls for Persuasion, use +6. Give a teammate a chance to ask the follow-up. The check does not guarantee the baker will risk everything.

### The escape while Pattern holds

**Scene:** two enemies are affected by Kief’s Hypnotic Pattern. A third is approaching. The party is retreating. Must Kief cast another big spell?

**Possible answer:** maintain Pattern, move toward safety, and consider Ray of Frost against the approaching enemy or Dodge to protect the retreat. Announce which enemies remain affected.

### The emergency escape

**Scene:** Kief uses a slot for Misty Step on his turn. Can he then use a slot for Fireball?

**Answer:** no. He can use an Action cantrip such as Fire Bolt or take an appropriate non-slot action. Check concentration and remaining movement as well.

### The familiar at the suspicious doorway

**Scene:** someone suggests sending Mr. Big into a room because “you can always summon him again.”

**Answer:** explain the campaign’s permanent-death ruling. Suggest a safer way to investigate, such as examining the doorway together or using a spell for a task it actually supports. Mr. Big’s willingness matters.

### The concentration check

**Scene:** Kief takes 26 damage while holding Fly. His Constitution save die shows 6.

**Answer:** DC 13; **6 +7 =13**, so concentration holds. Subtract the damage from current HP separately. If that damage reduced him to 0 HP and he became Unconscious, concentration would end regardless.

## 12. Leave yourself a place to resume

At a break, say or record one compact status. Someone else can write it down for you:

> **“Kief has [current HP] of 47. Slots [first / second / third] remain. SP [number] of 5. Innate uses [number] of 2. Concentration [spell or none]. Reaction [ready or spent]. Mr. Big [location and job]. Next we’re trying to [goal].”**

Add remaining Hit Dice, whether Sorcerous Restoration was used, conditions, and any unresolved ruling when relevant. Record resources **remaining**, consistently, so returning does not require reconstructing which convention you used.

Useful short calls during play:

- **“Recap, please.”** Ask for the changed situation.
- **“Holding Pattern; Ray of Frost on the active threat.”** Name concentration and the new action.
- **“Shield—Reaction spent.”** Make the shared limit visible.
- **“I need the spell read back.”** Get the information needed to choose.

These are suggested phrases; they do not require configuring software or typing commands.
