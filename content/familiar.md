## Confirmed DM ruling — Find Familiar

Recorded 2026-10-07 from Binyled's message relayed by Ryan (message timestamp 11:00 AM; original message date not supplied).

- The familiar is an actual physical creature, not a disposable summoned spirit.
- You can summon it and put it away. Once summoned, that individual is your familiar.
- If it dies, it is gone. Do not plan on restoring it by recasting *Find Familiar*.
- Its bond to you means its death negatively affects you.
- The DM considers it intelligent and able to provide legitimate help. It will not willingly sacrifice itself just because it is asked.

**Still to clarify:** the exact death penalty; what happens at 0 HP, including any death saves or opportunity to heal; the numerical Intelligence score and specific capabilities; creature type and accepted form/stat block; and which remaining casting, dismissal, cargo, telepathy, shared-sense, and touch-delivery mechanics carry over. “How it used to be ran” does not establish a specific older edition or its penalties.

**At the table:** treat Mr. Big as a companion with judgment and self-preservation. Agree on a safe job and an exit; stop or change the plan when he refuses. The 2024 details elsewhere are a provisional reference wherever this ruling has not settled them. Initial summoning and components remain unconfirmed.

## Eight proposed jobs — start beside Kief

Give Mr. Big one job and a clear exit. The movement, checks, telepathy, initiative, and touch-delivery details below are provisional; use them only if the DM retains them. None of these jobs grants immunity or a safe hiding place automatically.

### Beside Kief — the usual starting point

1. **The management representative.** Stare silently during negotiations. Kief interprets, then invites a teammate’s opinion. The Persuasion check and NPC response remain the DM’s call; no free charm effect. “He’s concerned about your process.”
2. **The second pair of eyes.** Watch a visible approach from the party’s position while the others investigate. Make requested checks and report what he actually notices; no automatic surprise immunity.
3. **The safe consultant.** Stay out of the fight or Dodge when appropriate while Kief holds concentration. Kief can announce, “Management approves the exit,” without exposing him to earn a mechanical bonus.
4. **The quiet report.** Share what he perceived through retained telepathy within 100 feet. Keep reports short so the next player can act. Intelligence, senses, range, and understanding still matter; beyond range, follow prior instructions rather than expecting live updates.

### A bounded task — agree on the route back

5. **The window inspection.** Use confirmed size and movement to reach a nearby vantage point with a known return route. Under retained 2024 mechanics, Kief spends a Bonus Action to share senses until the start of his next turn. Stop when the view requires entering unknown danger.
6. **The remote delivery.** Deliver Fly or Invisibility to an ally he can reach without crossing a dangerous area, if touch delivery is retained. Mr. Big uses his Reaction, stays within 100 feet of Kief, and touches the recipient. Kief pays normal costs and holds concentration. If the route is unsafe, Kief or the ally can move for ordinary touch casting instead.

### An exposed task — choose the benefit deliberately

7. **The point cow.** Inspect a short stretch ahead only after considering hazards, the return route, and whether the party has a safer way to look. Stop and report at danger; never send him to trigger a suspected trap. If the proposed Cat block is approved, he has 2 HP. Candy Land does not make him harder to kill.
8. **The well-timed distraction.** Combat Help requires him to be within 5 feet of the enemy on his own turn under retained rules. The qualifying next allied attack gets Advantage. With no Flyby and fragile proposed stats, exposure and withdrawal can be dangerous; he may refuse. Use a different party setup when the plan depends on willing sacrifice.

### A personality beat, then hand the scene back

Optional recurring mannerisms: inspect hospitality, stare at an unanswered question, or disapprove of a rushed plan. Choose one blink or gesture, let Kief translate it, and invite a teammate’s response. These are portrayal prompts, not new powers or established preferences. His opinion is flavor, not a veto over the party.

## Boundaries worth remembering

Mr. Big cannot attack. His cow appearance grants no bovine attacks, hauling ability, or special powers. Object carrying, manipulating tools, and administering potions depend on his form and DM adjudication; don't assume a tiny cat can perform a humanoid's task.

He rolls his own initiative, so coordinate Help and touch delivery with actual timing. Summoning takes an hour, or 70 minutes as a ritual, and consumes 10+ GP incense; a free feat cast still consumes it. Confirm components before session one. Recasting is not a recovery plan if he dies.

His creature type and how locals react to a tiny silent cow remain the DM’s call. Confirm 0 HP/healing rules and the consequences of injury before relying on exposed jobs; the permanent-loss ruling is already recorded.
