Choose the destination before giving an ally the ability to fly.

### When to use it

An ally needs to inspect or reach an elevated location.

### Mechanics

1. Agree on a safe landing and a return cue.
2. Cast *Fly* on the willing creature, using a 3rd-level slot and concentration.
3. Keep sight of the plan, protect your position, and warn before ending concentration.

### At the table

“Where are you landing? Excellent. Now we may discuss the dramatic part.”

### The rule underneath

At level 5 this casting serves one creature for up to 10 minutes. Flight is not permission to exceed carrying capacity or ignore hazards at the destination.
