Give the familiar a social role that does not depend on physical danger.

### When to use it

A tense negotiation benefits from a small, patient presence.

### Mechanics

1. Ask whether Mr. Big is willing to remain beside you.
2. Let his actual behavior supply a cue, then make your own Persuasion attempt when called for.
3. If the room becomes dangerous, prioritize his safety using the confirmed table procedures.

### At the table

“Management would like the payment terms repeated more slowly.”

### The rule underneath

A companion’s presence grants no automatic Help, advantage, or charm. Do not translate every glance into claimed information the DM has not supplied.
