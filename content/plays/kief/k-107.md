Mark three agreed objects to keep a short operation understandable.

### When to use it

Several teammates are handling similar parcels or tools.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Prestidigitation* for a simple mark on each of up to three surfaces.
2. Agree what the marks mean before separating.
3. Track the hour-long duration and do not add a fourth lasting effect without ending one.

### At the table

“This one is ours. This one is theirs. This one is why we are nervous.”

### The rule underneath

Marks convey information but do not authenticate ownership, track objects remotely, or replace a permanent label.
