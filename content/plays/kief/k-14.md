The same cantrip pointed at logistics instead of people.

### When to use it

- Marking routes, cleaning evidence for whoever reads it, managing camp
  without smoke or noise.

### Mechanics

1. Make a small mark or symbol appear on an object or surface for **1
   hour**, up to three effects running. Agree on symbols in advance: arrow
   = route, X = cleared, hoofprint = Mr. Big checked it.
2. Clean the grime off an inscription before the party's scholar reads it;
   chill perishables; light and snuff a campfire without flint.
3. The 1-hour expiry is a feature. The trail betrays nothing to anyone more
   than an hour behind.
4. Kief's Investigation is −1. The kit prepares evidence for better eyes.

### At the table

Mechanically: "At each fork I mark the left post with a small hoofprint,
renewing as we go. If we have to run, we follow the cows home."

Verbally: *"Tiny hoofprints mark the way out, as if a very small, very
organized cow had walked through the dark ahead of them. In a sense, one
had."*

### The rule underneath

Instantaneous and duration effects differ: Prestidigitation's marks
persist for an hour, then vanish. Table conventions (arrow means route) are
fiction agreed once and used without a roll. The three-effect limit means
the fourth mark ends one of the first three, so renew as you go.
