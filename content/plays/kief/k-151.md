Choose a different spell when you cannot honestly account for everyone in the blast.

### When to use it

A cluster looks tempting but bystanders or unseen rooms complicate it.

### Mechanics

1. Check the full 20-foot-radius area of *Fireball* and flammable surroundings.
2. Count which allies could be protected with Careful: at most three for Kief.
3. If important people remain exposed or locations are uncertain, choose a narrower action.

### At the table

“An excellent grouping. Unfortunately, several of them are our responsibility.”

### The rule underneath

Careful protects chosen creatures under its rules, not buildings or every bystander. A large damage number is not the only way to advance the objective.
