Five Sorcery Points that are also spell slots that are also Metamagic. The
accounting is the class.

### When to use it

- Every day. SP are the flexible reserve; slots are the spending plan.

### Mechanics

1. **Slot to SP** (no action): expend a slot for SP equal to its level. The
   maximum is **5 SP**; points over the cap are lost.
2. **SP to slot** (**Bonus Action**): 1st level = **2 SP**, 2nd = **3 SP**,
   3rd = **5 SP**. 3rd is the highest Kief can create at level 5. Created
   slots vanish on a Long Rest.
3. Careful and Subtle each cost **1 SP**. Turning all 5 SP into a 3rd-level
   slot spends the entire Metamagic reserve.
4. Don't convert the last defensive 1st-level slot reflexively. *Shield*
   and *Feather Fall* both need one.
5. Creating a slot is a Bonus Action on Kief's turn. It can't be done in
   the middle of an enemy's turn for a *Counterspell* or *Shield*.
6. Creating a slot doesn't cast a spell. But casting a spell with that slot
   still counts toward the one-slot-per-turn limit.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Quiet turn. Bonus Action: 3 Sorcery Points become a 2nd-level
slot, so Web's back on the table next round. I'm at 2 SP, enough for one
Careful and one Subtle."

Verbally: *"Kief does the arithmetic on his fingers like a man portioning
the last of a good harvest. Enough for one more storm, and seeds held
back."*

### The rule underneath

Font of Magic is a two-way exchange with uneven rates: slots become SP
one-for-one by level, while SP become slots at a premium (a 3rd-level slot
costs 5). Neither direction is a casting. Conversions reward margins
planned in advance: decide which resource a fight should drain before it
starts, not while everything is on fire.
