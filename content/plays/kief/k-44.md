The unglamorous inventory that solves problems a spell slot shouldn't.
Starting gear is **pending**; this is the shopping logic.

### When to use it

- Constantly. Check the pack before checking the spell list.
- Buying starting equipment.

### Mechanics

1. **Priorities** from the character record: an arcane focus or component
   pouch, familiar incense, basic travel equipment, and rope. A 50+ GP
   diamond if the budget allows *Chromatic Orb*.
2. **Rope, 50 feet**: crossings ([play 36](../k-36/)), climbing, binding
   Hypnotic Pattern sleepers ([play 72](../k-72/)).
3. **Crowbar**: Advantage on Strength checks where leverage applies. Kief's
   Strength is −1; he owns it, and the fighter uses it.
4. **Light**: Kief has Darkvision 60 and the [Light cantrip](../k-9/). A
   torch or lantern still matters when he's separated from the party.
5. **Rations**: Mr. Big eats first. Always.
6. **Desired character kit**: the kettle, the blends, the moss jar, and an
   herbalism kit. Kief is not proficient with the herbalism kit; it's
   character, not a bonus.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before anyone spends magic on this wall: fifty feet of
rope, a crowbar, and a cow with a 40-foot climb speed. Let's be primitive
about it."

Verbally: *"Kief's pack produces the rope, the crowbar, and, after a
respectful pause, a ration bar, which goes to the cow."*

### The rule underneath

Darkvision 60 means dim light as bright and darkness as dim light within
60 feet, in shades of grey. Anyone without it needs a light source.
Carrying capacity in 2024 is Strength × 15 pounds (Kief: 120). Rope and
rations solve exploration problems at zero resource cost; spells are the
premium currency.
