One minute, one break or tear. The cantrip that makes friends and undoes
sabotage.

### When to use it

- A villager's broken tool, a guard's snapped strap, the innkeeper's
  cracked kettle. Rapport for free, the partner of [play 13](../k-13/).
- Reversing sabotage: a cut rope, a torn map, a broken chain link.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip (Magic Initiate, Charisma-based), casting time **1 minute**,
   touch: repair a single break or tear in an object, no larger than **1
   foot** in any dimension.
2. It leaves no trace of the former damage. A magic item can be physically
   repaired, but its magic is not restored.
3. Party maintenance: bowstrings, boot soles, scabbard straps, the map
   someone folded wrong.
4. The one-minute cast makes it a downtime tool, never a combat one.
5. It is a touch spell, so Mr. Big can deliver it within 100 feet.

### At the table

Mechanically: "While we talk, I Mend the miller's cracked cup. One minute,
good as new, no charge. Then let's hear about the wagon that came through
on Tuesday."

Verbally: *"Kief runs a thumb along the crack and the clay remembers being
whole. 'Things want to be fixed,' he says. 'Mostly you just have to ask
slow enough.'"*

### The rule underneath

Casting time is part of the price. A 1-minute cantrip is downtime labor:
unlimited, free, and useless mid-fight. Its limits are exact: one break or
tear, no larger than a foot, and no restored magic. A broken axle is too
big; a cracked axle pin might not be. Ask the DM before promising.
