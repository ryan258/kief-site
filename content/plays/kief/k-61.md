The familiar delivers Kief's touch spells, turning a silent cow into a
100-foot extension cord.

### When to use it

- A touch spell needs to reach someone Kief can't safely reach: *Fly* on
  the fighter on the high balcony, *Invisibility* on the scout ahead.
- Utility at a distance: *Light* on the far side of a chasm, *Mending* on a
  gate from the safe side.

### Mechanics

1. When Kief casts a spell with a range of **touch**, Mr. Big can deliver
   it if he is within **100 feet** of Kief. He spends his **Reaction**.
2. He must be able to touch the recipient, so he has to be in position
   before Kief casts. Telepathy coordinates it.
3. The spell stays Kief's: his slot, his DC, his Concentration.
4. Kief's touch spells: *Fly*, *Invisibility*, *Dragon's Breath*, *Light*,
   and *Mending*. *Alter Self* is self-only.
5. Risk: AC 12, 2 HP. Moving him next to a fighter in melee exposes him.
   [Play 63](../k-63/) is the math.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big is already next to Brenna on the balcony. I cast
Fly; Mr. Big uses his Reaction to deliver it. Brenna has a Fly Speed of 60,
and I'm concentrating."

Verbally: *"The fighter feels a small hoof tap her boot and suddenly she
weighs about as much as tea steam. Mr. Big regards her with quiet
professional satisfaction."*

### The rule underneath

*Find Familiar*'s touch clause: when you cast a touch spell, the familiar
can deliver it if it's within 100 feet, using its Reaction. The familiar is
pure range extension. List the spells on your sheet that say "Touch": each
is secretly a 100-foot spell with a delivery point that repositions itself
every round.
