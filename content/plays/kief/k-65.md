The scripted first turn of an important fight.

### When to use it

- Boss fights and set-piece battles the party enters on purpose.

### Mechanics

1. **Bonus Action**: Innate Sorcery ([play 45](../k-45/)). DC 15 and spell
   attack Advantage for a minute.
2. **Action**: the control spell that fits ([play 37](../k-37/)).
   Default: **Careful Hypnotic Pattern** (1 SP), allies protected, DC 15.
   Charm-immune enemies: *Fear* or *Web*. No control option and a real
   cluster: Careful *Fireball* ([play 1](../k-1/)).
3. **Reaction**: held for *Counterspell* if there's a caster
   ([play 30](../k-30/)). If there's a caster, Web is the better opener
   because it keeps both 3rd-level slots.
4. **Tell the party** what changed: "Those three are out. Hit the one on
   the left."
5. **Round 2+**: protect Concentration. *Mind Sliver* sets up an ally's
   save; Dodge from cover is fine. Spend another big slot only for a new
   problem.
6. Cost: one Innate Sorcery use, one 3rd-level slot, 1 SP.

### At the table

Mechanically: "As the doors open: Bonus Action, Innate Sorcery. Action:
Hypnotic Pattern on the honor guard, Careful on Brenna and Tam, one point.
DC 15 Wisdom. Reaction's held for their priest."

Verbally: *"The scales light along Kief's arms, and the throne room's
honor guard slowly forgets what it was guarding. 'We did knock,' he
says."*

### The rule underneath

Opening turns are action-economy arithmetic: Bonus Action buff, Action
spell, Reaction held, three different action types with no conflict. The
Bonus Action buff isn't a slotted spell, so the Action spell is legal
under the one-slot rule. A scripted opener that spends every action type is
the largest upgrade available at any level, and it costs only forethought.
