Not a combat play. It is the reason the party finds the ambush, the
enchanted door, and the gossip first. Mr. Big scouts, *Speak with Animals*
interviews the locals, and ritual *Detect Magic* checks what the eyes
cannot.

### When to use it

- Before committing the party to any unknown space: buildings, ruins,
  camps, negotiations.
- Every day, ambiently. Rituals and the familiar cost time, not slots.

### Mechanics

1. **Mr. Big scouts** once summoned ([play 22](../k-22/)). Cat stat block
   in a toy-cow shape: Speed 40, Climb 40, Stealth +4, Perception +3,
   Darkvision 60. He communicates **telepathically within 100 feet**, and
   Kief can spend a Bonus Action to see and hear through him until the
   start of Kief's next turn.
2. **Speak with Animals**: 3 free casts per Long Rest (Forest Gnome), or as
   a ritual for 10 extra minutes. Beasts report what beasts perceive.
3. **Detect Magic** as a ritual: 10 extra minutes, no slot, Concentration
   up to 10 minutes. Sense magic within 30 feet; a Magic action shows the
   aura and a spell's school.
4. **Interpret**: Kief's Investigation is −1 and his Perception is +0. He
   is the collector, not the analyst. Hand the findings to whoever reads
   evidence best; Kief's contribution is Insight +3 and the network.
5. Mr. Big is not a trap detector and *Detect Magic* does not find mundane
   traps. Say what each tool can and cannot answer.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we go in: Mr. Big takes the wall-top, Stealth plus
four, and reports by telepathy inside a hundred feet. Meanwhile I
ritual-cast Speak with Animals, no slot, and ask the crows who has been
through that gate since dawn."

Verbally: *"Kief sits cross-legged in the grass, conversing gravely with
three crows while a tiny porcelain-looking cow picks its way along the
battlements. The intelligence service is in session."*

State intent and method: where Mr. Big goes, what question the animals
get, what the ritual checks. Never "I scout for danger."

### The rule underneath

Three information engines, three rule systems: a familiar (its own senses,
its own checks, 100-foot telepathy), a ritual detection spell
(Concentration, blocked by thick stone, wood, metal, or lead), and beast
interviews (limited by what beasts notice and understand). The ritual tag
means no slot, 10 minutes longer. Routing the right question to the right
sensor, and the analysis to the party member with the best bonus, is the
skill.
