Animate a witness’s account in a basin so the whole party can question the same picture.

### When to use it

An NPC describes a confusing chase or encounter.

### Mechanics

1. Ask the witness for one movement at a time.
2. Use *Shape Water* on visible liquid within 30 feet to form simple shapes and animate the account; keep the material within a 5-foot cube.
3. Let the witness correct you before a teammate asks the next question.

### At the table

“The tall lump is the duke. Please interrupt if I have made him too flattering.”

### The rule underneath

This illustrates testimony; it does not retrieve memories or establish that the witness is truthful. One lasting shape effect uses one of the spell’s two active slots.
