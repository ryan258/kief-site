Use a fleeting trinket as the opening beat of a proposal.

### When to use it

You need to explain a simple product, token, or symbol quickly.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Prestidigitation* to make a palm-sized nonmagical trinket.
2. Show it immediately and state the idea in one sentence.
3. Let it vanish by the end of your next turn, then discuss a real version.

### At the table

“A prototype with unusually firm return terms.”

### The rule underneath

The trinket is brief and nonmagical. Do not sell it as permanent, use it to evade priced components, or assume it functions as a precision tool.
