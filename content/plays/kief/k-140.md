Make the speaker invisible only after agreeing how the visible conversation will continue.

### When to use it

A willing ally needs to leave a crowded scene quietly while another keeps talking.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Invisibility* at an appropriate moment and range.
2. Have the visible teammate continue a legitimate conversation while the invisible ally moves.
3. Account for sounds, doors, footprints, and any Stealth checks.

### At the table

“I shall ask about the municipal tea tax. Take the opportunity responsibly.”

### The rule underneath

Invisibility does not guarantee unnoticed movement. It ends for the target after an attack roll, damage dealt, or casting a spell; concentration also matters.
