Use Halt to interrupt a particular enemy’s next turn while the party completes something else.

### When to use it

One visible creature within 60 feet is about to interfere with a rescue or task.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Command* with Halt and resolve its Wisdom save.
2. On failure, use the opening during that creature’s next turn to advance the objective.
3. Keep the party aware that this is a short interruption.

### At the table

“Please hold. Your complaint is important to us.”

### The rule underneath

Halt denies movement, Action, and Bonus Action on that turn. It is not a lasting truce, a guaranteed failed save, or a ban on every possible Reaction.
