Kief prevents damage and creates opportunities. He does not heal. A
Healer's Kit is the cheapest way to keep a downed friend alive. **Not owned
yet**: buy it if the allowance permits.

### When to use it

- An ally is at 0 HP, and nobody with healing magic can reach them this
  round.
- After a fight, stabilizing the downed before the party regroups.

### Mechanics

1. Healer's Kit: **10 uses**. As a **Utilize action**, expend one use to
   stabilize an Unconscious creature at 0 HP **without a Wisdom (Medicine)
   check**.
2. A stable creature stops making Death Saving Throws but is still at 0 HP
   and Unconscious. It regains 1 HP after 1d4 hours, or when healed.
3. Without the kit, stabilizing is a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check. Kief's
   is +0.
4. Kief must be within reach of the ally. That means walking into the
   fight. Weigh it against [Command](../k-20/) or [Fear](../k-31/) to clear
   the attacker first.
5. The kit restores no HP. Never imply that tea does either.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Brenna's down. I move to her, Utilize action, Healer's Kit:
she's stable, no roll needed. That's 9 uses left."

Verbally: *"Kief kneels, unrolls a neat bundle of bandages, and ties a knot
his grandmother would approve of. 'You're not leaving yet,' he tells her.
'We haven't had lunch.'"*

### The rule underneath

At 0 HP a creature makes Death Saving Throws: three successes stabilize,
three failures kill, and damage while down counts as a failure (a critical
hit counts as two). Stabilizing ends the countdown but doesn't restore HP.
A support character without healing magic still has a job in that
sequence, and mundane gear is how he does it.
