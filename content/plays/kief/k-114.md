Assign separate people to operate and observe a mechanism.

### When to use it

A visible lever may change something that Kief cannot see from his position.

### Mechanics

1. Agree on an abort signal with the observer.
2. Use *Mage Hand* on an ordinary lever only if the DM judges its required force within the hand’s ability.
3. Pull once and wait for the observer’s report.

### At the table

“One movement. Then we let the building state its opinion.”

### The rule underneath

Range does not guarantee safety: effects may extend beyond 30 feet. Mage Hand cannot activate a magic item, and an unknown lever may be one.
