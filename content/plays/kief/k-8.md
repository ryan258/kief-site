A 60-foot cantrip that slows attackers, helping Kief and his allies keep
their distance.

### When to use it

- A fast melee enemy is approaching Kief or the backline.
- An enemy is fleeing or chasing an ally, and 10 feet decides the chase.
- The target resists fire.

### Mechanics

1. Ranged spell attack **+6**, range 60 feet.
2. Hit: **2d8 cold**, and the target's Speed drops by **10 feet** until
   the start of Kief's next turn.
3. Under Innate Sorcery: +6 with Advantage.
4. It does not stack with itself. Re-apply it each round to keep a chase
   going.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Ray of Frost at the charging guard, plus six to hit. On a
hit, 2d8 cold and his Speed is 10 feet lower until the start of my next
turn."

Verbally: *"A thread of blue-white light chills the guard's knees. 'Mellow
out,' Kief says. 'You're rushing.'"*

### The rule underneath

Speed reduction is a rider on a hit, and duration text is tactics text:
"until the start of your next turn" means it must be re-applied every
round. Separate movement penalties such as difficult terrain and this
reduction both apply, but the same spell's effect does not stack with
itself. Kiting works because you can attack, then move away: a slowed
melee enemy may never arrive.
