Offer a cooling mist to a willing, overheated traveler.

### When to use it

Someone wants a little comfort during a hot stop.

### Mechanics

1. Ask whether they want a light mist.
2. Use *Elementalism* to dampen the small area around them.
3. Offer actual shade, rest, or supplies through the party as appropriate.

### At the table

“A very local forecast. No complaints accepted beyond five feet.”

### The rule underneath

Light dampening is not healing, disease treatment, or automatic relief from Exhaustion. Comfort can support a conversation without inventing a condition cure.
