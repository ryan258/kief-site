Use a small controlled test before proposing a larger candy-river plan.

### When to use it

The party wants to cross or manipulate a magical liquid feature.

### Mechanics

1. From safe ground, choose a visible portion within *Shape Water* range and volume.
2. Try one listed effect and ask what happens; do not start by entering the river.
3. Use that response to plan with the party’s strongest relevant specialist.

### At the table

“Let us interview one puddle before negotiating with the entire river.”

### The rule underneath

Liquid scope is approved; enormous currents, creature bodies, and unusual freezing are not automatically controlled. Consult [the liquid ruling](/rulings/#shape-water-campaign-liquid-ruling).
