Test whether a guard is watching a particular sightline with a harmless object image.

### When to use it

You can observe the guard without committing the party to an approach.

### Mechanics

1. Create a stationary crate image with *Minor Illusion* at a plausible visible spot.
2. Watch whether the guard comments, approaches, or ignores it.
3. Treat the response as one observation, then dismiss or let the image expire.

### At the table

“Inventory has developed a hypothesis.”

### The rule underneath

The image provides no physical cover and interaction exposes it. Ignoring it does not prove the guard is inattentive or that a later approach will succeed.
