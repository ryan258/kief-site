Oil first, fire second. Kief has a fire cantrip; the party's thrower may
have better aim. **Pending purchase.**

### When to use it

- A tough single target worth two turns of setup.
- Pre-fight: an oiled doorway square, ready to be lit.

### Mechanics

1. **Douse**: when taking the Attack action, replace an attack with
   throwing a flask at a creature or object within 20 feet. The target
   makes a Dexterity save (DC 8 + the thrower's Dexterity modifier +
   Proficiency Bonus; Kief's is 13) or is covered in oil for 1 minute. If
   it takes **fire damage** before the oil dries, it takes **5 extra fire
   damage**.
2. **Pour**: a Utilize action covers a 5-foot square within 5 feet. Once
   lit, it burns for 2 rounds, dealing 5 fire damage to a creature that
   enters it or ends its turn there.
3. Kief's follow-ups: [Fire Bolt](../k-6/), a *Fireball*, or a burning
   *Web*. Any ally's fire damage also counts.
4. No Elemental Affinity yet, and no fire resistance. Keep Kief out of the
   burning square.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tam throws the oil at the troll: Dexterity save against
his oil DC. Then me: Fire Bolt. On a hit it's 2d10, plus 5 from the oil."

Verbally: *"Kief watches the oil spread with the small contentment of a man
watching leaves unfurl in hot water. Then he provides the heat."*

### The rule underneath

Damage-type riders don't care whose damage it is: any fire counts, so an
oil flask is a party buff, not a caster trick. Thrown oil uses the Attack
action's slot for one attack, and a spell caster who takes the Attack
action gets one attack. Sometimes the right play for a caster isn't a
spell.
