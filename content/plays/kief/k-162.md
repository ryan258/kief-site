Keep magical repair and expert interpretation in different hands.

### When to use it

A damaged document or device has recoverable pieces.

### Mechanics

1. Ask the knowledgeable teammate which break matters and what should be recorded first.
2. Use *Mending* only on an eligible break, taking a minute.
3. Give the repaired object straight back for their interpretation.

### At the table

“I can fix the paper. You are in charge of what it is trying to start.”

### The rule underneath

Physical repair does not translate, decipher, identify magic, or restore an enchantment. The collaboration preserves the expert’s role.
