The scouting spell for someone else. Kief's job is to cast it, stay safe
enough to keep Concentration, and trust the scout.

### When to use it

- The party's stealthiest member needs to get close: read the ledger, count
  the guards, find the prisoner.
- A negotiation needs an unseen witness in the room.
- Not when Kief's Concentration is already holding the fight together.

### Mechanics

1. 2nd level, Action, **touch**, Concentration up to **1 hour**: a willing
   creature gains the **Invisible** condition.
2. It ends for that creature immediately after it **makes an attack roll,
   deals damage, or casts a spell**.
3. Invisible means unseen, not unheard. Footsteps, tracks, a creaking door,
   and scent still give the scout away. They still roll Stealth when the DM
   asks.
4. Mr. Big can deliver the touch within 100 feet ([play 61](../k-61/)).
5. Upcast at 3rd level to affect a second creature. That uses one of only
   two 3rd-level slots.
6. Agree on a return signal and a time limit before they go. Kief can't see
   them, and losing Concentration ends the spell without telling them.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility on Sable, concentrating, up to an hour. It ends
if you attack, deal damage, or cast. Signal is two knocks. If you're not
back in ten minutes, Mr. Big comes looking."

Verbally: *"Kief pats the scout's arm, and his hand is suddenly resting on
nothing he can see. 'Lovely,' he says. 'I've misplaced you already.'"*

### The rule underneath

The 2024 Invisible condition gives Advantage on Initiative, makes the
creature immune to effects that require seeing it, and imposes Disadvantage
on attacks against it while granting it Advantage on its own attacks. The
end clause is the cost: any attack roll, damage, or spell ends it. A
Concentration buff on an ally is a two-person contract, so brief the other
person.
