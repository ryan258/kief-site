Hand the martials Advantage, then point them at the right target.

### When to use it

- Enemy numbers outweigh the party's, and the party's damage is in its
  weapon attacks.
- The enemies can be caught in a 20-foot cube with allies outside it.

### Mechanics

1. [Web](../k-25/): Restrained enemies have Speed 0, attacks against them
   have **Advantage**, and their own attacks have Disadvantage.
2. **Announce who's Restrained.** The rules don't broadcast it; players do.
3. **Call a kill order**: the most dangerous Restrained enemy first. Focus
   fire removes enemy turns; spread damage removes nothing.
4. Ranged allies benefit immediately. Melee allies entering the webs face
   the same saves, so they fight from the edge.
5. Enemies escape with a Strength (Athletics) check against DC 14, using
   their action. Every escape attempt is an attack that didn't happen.
6. Kief's turn: [Mind Sliver](../k-16/) an enemy that's still free and
   about to enter or start its turn in the webs, so its Dexterity save is
   worse. Escaping uses an Athletics check, not a save, so the penalty
   doesn't help there. Or Dodge to protect Concentration.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Three are stuck in the web: the axe, the spear, and the
dog. Everyone on the axe, you all have Advantage. I'm Mind Slivering the
captain before he follows them in."

Verbally: *"The raiders struggle in the silk, and the party, unhurried and
precise, begins at the dangerous end of the line."*

### The rule underneath

Advantage from a condition applies to every attacker, which makes one
control spell a party-wide accuracy buff. Focus fire is rules-derived:
dropping one enemy removes its turns for the rest of the fight. The web's
difficult terrain and repeated saves also shape where allies can stand, so
plan the geometry before casting.
