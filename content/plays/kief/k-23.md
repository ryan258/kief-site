Thirty feet of instant elsewhere, as a Bonus Action. Kief only; no
passenger.

### When to use it

- Grappled, surrounded, or cornered. Teleportation ignores all of it and
  never provokes Opportunity Attacks.
- Vertical access: the balcony, the ledge, across the gap, any seen spot
  within 30 feet.
- Holding concentration matters more than the 2nd-level slot.

### Mechanics

1. 2nd level, **Bonus Action**, self: teleport up to 30 feet to an
   unoccupied space Kief **can see**.
2. Verbal component only. Gagged? Subtle Spell (1 SP) removes it.
3. One slot per turn: Misty Step plus *Fire Bolt*, *Mind Sliver*, or a
   non-slot action is legal. Misty Step plus *Web* or *Fireball* is not.
4. It needs sight of the destination. Fog, darkness beyond Darkvision, and
   total cover block it. Keep a seen destination in mind.
5. Cheaper option: take the Disengage action and walk away. That keeps the
   slot and the Bonus Action.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The ogre's got me. Bonus Action: Misty Step, 30 feet to the
ledge. The grapple is holding a Kief-shaped volume of air, and I still have
my Action: Ray of Frost, on him."

Verbally: *"The ogre's fist closes and Kief is abruptly a rumor. From the
ledge above: 'No hard feelings. You've got a good grip.'"*

### The rule underneath

The 2024 one-slot rule: on a turn, you can expend only one spell slot to
cast a spell. The limit is slots, not spell count, so a cantrip or a free
feature cast still fits. Teleporting ends a grapple because the grappled
creature leaves the grappler's reach, and teleportation never provokes
Opportunity Attacks, which trigger only on movement out of reach.
