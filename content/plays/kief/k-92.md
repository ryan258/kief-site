Use a chosen scent to make an entrance memorable without claiming a disguise.

### When to use it

You are meeting someone who already associates Kief with tea.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Elementalism* for harmless colored, scented smoke in a small area.
2. Choose one recognizable scent and speak your name plainly.
3. Finish the effect description and make the actual request.

### At the table

“Bergamot, with a faint note of unpaid invoices.”

### The rule underneath

The scent lingers for 1 minute. The smoke is not stated to obscure vision, poison anyone, or defeat a creature’s senses; recognition is a social outcome.
