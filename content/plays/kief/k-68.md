Two unseen observers for one Concentration: an invisible teammate and a
very small cow.

### When to use it

- The party needs a close look at a camp, manor, or lair before choosing an
  approach.
- The scout is willing, and Kief can wait somewhere safe.

### Mechanics

1. Cast [Invisibility](../k-24/) on the scout. Concentration up to an hour;
   it ends if they attack, deal damage, or cast a spell.
2. Mr. Big follows at a distance, staying within **100 feet of Kief** for
   telepathy. He sees what a cat sees; the scout sees what a person
   understands.
3. Kief spends a Bonus Action to share Mr. Big's senses when he needs to
   see something himself.
4. Agree on signals: time limit, a retreat sign, what happens if Kief's
   Concentration drops. Kief can relay through Mr. Big, but Mr. Big can't
   speak to the scout.
5. Kief hides and stays still. Losing Concentration makes the scout visible
   in the worst possible place.
6. Invisible doesn't mean silent. The scout still rolls Stealth.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility on Sable, concentrating. Mr. Big shadows her
from the rooftops, inside my hundred feet. Ten minutes; if Mr. Big comes
back alone, we assume trouble. I'll be behind the woodpile, not moving."

Verbally: *"The scout vanishes. A moment later, a small cow departs in the
same direction, with the air of someone who has been asked to keep an eye
on things."*

### The rule underneath

Different scouts have different information quality: a familiar reports
what it perceives and understands, and a person interprets. A
Concentration spell that keeps a teammate safe makes the caster's own
safety a party priority. Communication plans are established fiction:
agreed in advance, they cost nothing when things go wrong.
