Nobody dies, everybody talks, and the party's reputation precedes it
usefully.

### When to use it

- Enemies worth more alive: deserters, dupes, hired locals, anyone whose
  employer is the real problem.
- Places where bodies cost more than mercy: towns, politics, anywhere the
  Greenvale Tea Consortium might be looking for an excuse.

### Mechanics

1. [Hypnotic Pattern](../k-2/) lands: Charmed, Incapacitated, Speed 0, up to
   a minute. Damage is the off switch, so nobody swings.
2. The party spends the rounds binding sleepers and collecting weapons.
   Ask the DM how long binding takes; a minute is ten rounds.
3. Deal with anyone who passed the save **first**, without area damage near
   the sleepers.
4. When the minute ends (or Kief drops Concentration), they wake bound and
   disarmed, facing a gnome with tea already poured. Insight +3 sorts who's
   worth talking to; Persuasion +6 does the talking.
5. Rope or manacles must be in somebody's pack. Starting gear is pending.
6. Careful Spell (1 SP) protected the party's front line at casting.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Pattern's holding all five, one minute. Rope, weapons in a
pile, and nobody deals a single point of damage or we do this the loud way.
When they wake, I'll have the negotiating blend ready."

Verbally: *"The war party wakes tied in a tidy row, unbruised, disarmed,
and confronted with the smell of something warm. 'Morning,' says the little
gnome pouring the fifth cup. 'Let's talk about your employer.'"*

### The rule underneath

Nonviolent resolutions are rules-supported: a Charmed, Incapacitated enemy
can be bound because the spell ends only on damage or someone shaking it
awake, and rope is neither. The aftermath runs on social rules: attitude,
Influence checks, and the fact that you spared them. Parties that notice
this recruit, ransom, and flip encounters other parties can only kill.
