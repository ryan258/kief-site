Make a magical prop serve the character whose goal is at stake.

### When to use it

Another player’s personal scene offers Kief a chance to contribute.

### Mechanics

1. Ask that player what would help before introducing a flourish.
2. Use *Prestidigitation* or *Elementalism* for one simple requested effect.
3. Return the conversation to their character immediately.

### At the table

“I have supplied the atmosphere. The important words are yours.”

### The rule underneath

Permission to cast is not permission to take over another player’s scene. No roll or bonus is assumed; the play is useful when it supports their chosen approach.

Use the [current spellbook](/spellbook/) and [table rulings](/rulings/) for the spell details and campaign overrides.
