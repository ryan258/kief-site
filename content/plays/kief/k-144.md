Use an agreed disguise for a conversation, then let behavior carry it.

### When to use it

Kief needs a different appearance for a plausible short role.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Alter Self* for appearance and voice while retaining your size and basic body shape.
2. Choose a role you can actually sustain without invented credentials.
3. Coordinate with the party and leave when the role’s purpose is complete.

### At the table

“A traveling tea inspector. A regrettably common profession.”

### The rule underneath

The spell grants neither another creature’s statistics nor knowledge, clothing, or social authority. Impersonation and Deception remain checks or judgments, not spell effects.
