Rehearse a proposed route with a simple moving ribbon of liquid.

### When to use it

The party needs to explain a maneuver without moving miniatures repeatedly.

### Mechanics

1. Place a little water in a basin.
2. Use *Shape Water* to form a simple animated ribbon representing the planned route.
3. Ask the teammate making the crossing to point out the difficult part.

### At the table

“This is us. This bend is the regrettable part.”

### The rule underneath

The ribbon is a diagram, not a prediction. It grants no movement, carries no miniature rider, and substitutes for no terrain check.
