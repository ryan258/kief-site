A 30-foot cone of dropped weapons and running enemies.

### When to use it

- Melee enemies stacked in front of the party, where a cone from Kief's
  space can point away from allies.
- Enemies worth disarming. The **drop** clause is half the value.
- Charm-immune enemies, where *Hypnotic Pattern* would be wasted.

### Mechanics

1. 3rd level (Draconic Sorcery, always prepared), Action, self, **30-foot
   cone**, Concentration up to 1 minute.
2. Each creature in the cone makes a **Wisdom save against DC 14** (15
   under Innate Sorcery). On a failure it **drops whatever it is holding**
   and is **Frightened** for the duration.
3. A creature Frightened this way takes the **Dash** action and moves away
   from Kief by the safest route on each of its turns, unless it has
   nowhere to move.
4. If it ends its turn where it has no line of sight to Kief, it makes
   another Wisdom save and the spell ends for it on a success. Staying
   visible keeps them running.
5. Fleeing enemies may provoke Opportunity Attacks. Tell the party before
   the cast, not after.
6. Allies in the cone? Careful Spell (1 SP) protects up to three.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fear, the cone over all four of them. Wisdom save DC 14 or
they drop their weapons and Dash away from me every turn. Brenna, they'll
run past you. Someone grab those swords."

Verbally: *"For one moment every raider in the hallway sees exactly what
has been living behind the little gnome's mellow eyes. They are suddenly,
unanimously, needed elsewhere."*

### The rule underneath

The Frightened condition by itself imposes Disadvantage on ability checks
and attacks while the source is in sight, and prevents moving closer to it.
*Fear* the spell adds forced Dashing and the dropped items. When spell text
and condition text both apply, read both. The escape save only happens out
of line of sight, so where Kief stands controls how long the rout lasts.
