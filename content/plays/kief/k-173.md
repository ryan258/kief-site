End the scheme by handing its result to the whole party.

### When to use it

A creative plan worked, partly worked, or failed memorably.

### Mechanics

1. State the actual outcome without upgrading an attempt into success.
2. Name the teammate action that made a difference.
3. Save one sentence about the useful ruling or clue, then move into the next scene.

### At the table

“The syrup was mine. The good idea belonged to the committee.”

### The rule underneath

Keep a discovered ruling specific to what the DM decided. A successful stunt once does not establish unlimited spell scope, and a good story need not become a repeated routine.
