Make one crude sand landmark so a teammate can explain the approach.

### When to use it

The party is arguing about the shape of a tower, arch, or bridge.

### Mechanics

1. Gather actual sand or dirt within a 1-foot cube.
2. Use *Elementalism* to sculpt one crude landmark for an hour.
3. Ask the person who saw the site to correct the plan using ordinary gestures or marks.

### At the table

“The tower is to scale. Our confidence is not.”

### The rule underneath

Sculpt Element does not reveal terrain or make a precise architectural model. This is a memory aid; new information still needs scouting or testimony.
