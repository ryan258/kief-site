Keep magical presence separate from accusations about an object’s owner.

### When to use it

A suspicious gift or document might carry magic.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Detect Magic* when it is safe to concentrate and spend the casting time.
2. Take the required Magic action to inspect an aura on a visible eligible target.
3. Report presence and any spell school separately from your theory about its purpose.

### At the table

“I can confirm magic. The accusation still needs ingredients.”

### The rule underneath

Detect Magic is not Identify, truth detection, or proof of a curse. Its barriers and 30-foot reach still apply.
