Offer a liquid model when a witness cannot read a written description.

### When to use it

Someone can recognize a simple object but cannot name it.

### Mechanics

1. Ask permission to show several possibilities.
2. Shape visible water into one crude object at a time with *Shape Water*.
3. Dismiss an old lasting effect before exceeding two; record which feature the witness recognizes.

### At the table

“Was the crown like this, or did it have more points for catching curtains?”

### The rule underneath

A simple shape cannot supply exact portraiture or expose a disguised creature. Recognition belongs to the witness, and leading questions can mislead.
