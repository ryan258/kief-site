Choose which emergency you are covering before the round begins.

### When to use it

Both a dangerous caster and a risky crossing threaten the party.

### Mechanics

1. Tell teammates whether your Reaction is intended for *Counterspell*, *Shield*, or *Feather Fall*.
2. Position for the chosen spell’s trigger and range.
3. Reassess when circumstances change; using one Reaction leaves the others unavailable until it refreshes.

### At the table

“I can catch the spell or the falling person. Let us try not to schedule both.”

### The rule underneath

Having three Reaction spells does not grant three Reactions. A declared priority is a plan, not a readied action or a reservation the rules enforce.

Use the [current spellbook](/spellbook/) and [table rulings](/rulings/) for the spell details and campaign overrides.
