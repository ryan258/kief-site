Ask an animal for landmarks it actually uses, then translate for the party.

### When to use it

A passage network is confusing and local Beasts are available.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Speak with Animals*, using a free cast or ritual as time permits.
2. Ask about one familiar smell, feeding place, or exit rather than street names.
3. Compare its account with the party’s map before choosing a route.

### At the table

“Which tunnel smells like the bakery that never shares?”

### The rule underneath

Conversation does not guarantee accurate cartography or obedience. The animal’s experience supplies clues; navigation and interpretation remain the party’s work.
