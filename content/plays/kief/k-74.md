Nudge a small spill toward a tray before it reaches something valuable.

### When to use it

A tipped pitcher threatens a map or a fragile display.

### Mechanics

1. Identify an existing visible spill and a nearby receiving surface.
2. On your turn, use *Shape Water* to redirect that liquid up to 5 feet.
3. Have a teammate lift the papers while you handle the spill.

### At the table

“The map is not steeping today.”

### The rule underneath

Shape Water takes an Action, not a Reaction. Whether you act before the damage occurs depends on timing; it cannot retroactively save soaked evidence.
