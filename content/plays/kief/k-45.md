Two minutes a day of DC 15 and Advantage on Sorcerer spell attacks. The
difference between a good opening and a great one.

### When to use it

- Round one of an important fight: Bonus Action, then the whole minute is
  upgraded. The same turn can still cast an Action spell.
- Save-spell fights (*Hypnotic Pattern*, *Web*, *Fear*, *Fireball*,
  *Command*) and attack-spell fights (*Fire Bolt*, *Chromatic Orb*) both
  benefit.
- Not in every skirmish. Two uses per Long Rest.

### Mechanics

1. **Bonus Action**, **2 uses per Long Rest**, lasts **1 minute**. No
   Concentration.
2. While active: Sorcerer spells have **save DC 15** (+1) and **Advantage on
   spell attack rolls**. The attack bonus stays **+6**; it is not +1 to
   hit.
3. **Sorcerer spells only**: class preparations, Sorcerer cantrips, and
   Draconic Sorcery spells. *Minor Illusion*, *Speak with Animals*, *Mage
   Hand*, *Mending*, and *Find Familiar* come from other sources and don't
   benefit.
4. There is no way to buy extra uses with SP at level 5. Sorcery Incarnate
   arrives at level 7.
5. The Bonus Action competes with *Misty Step*, *Dragon's Breath*, and Font
   of Magic. Round one usually has nothing else for it.
6. Mark the use on the dashboard, and toggle the effect so the displayed DC
   updates.

### At the table

Mechanically: "That's a real fight forming. Bonus Action: Innate Sorcery,
first of two. For the next minute my Sorcerer save DC is 15 and my spell
attacks have Advantage. Action: Hypnotic Pattern, DC 15."

Verbally: *"The ember-orange shimmer wakes along Kief's forearms, the scales
of some patient ancestor surfacing through the calm like heat through a
cup's wall."*

### The rule underneath

Limited-use features print their recharge. Innate Sorcery's benefits name
"Sorcerer spells," so source tags matter: a spell granted by a species or
feat is not a Sorcerer spell even when Kief casts it with Charisma. A
Bonus Action buff with no Concentration is the safest opener in the game.
Spend the Bonus Action in round one and the minute covers most fights.
