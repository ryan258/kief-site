Prepare a dusty surface as a simple record of later disturbance.

### When to use it

You want to know whether a quiet shelf or threshold was used.

### Mechanics

1. With permission where appropriate, use *Elementalism* to put a thin dust layer on a small surface.
2. Note its present appearance and leave it alone.
3. Return later and ask the DM what visible disturbance remains.

### At the table

“The dust has been appointed night clerk.”

### The rule underneath

Dust is not an alarm or a guaranteed tracker. Wind, cleaning, small animals, and careful intruders can complicate evidence; identification may require a check.
