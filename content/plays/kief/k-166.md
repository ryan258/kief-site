Invite Mr. Big to suggest a safer version of a risky plan.

### When to use it

Someone proposes putting the familiar near an unknown hazard.

### Mechanics

1. Explain the goal rather than issuing a sacrificial command.
2. Ask the DM how the intelligent companion responds and what communication is available under the campaign ruling.
3. Use his refusal or observation to revise the party’s plan.

### At the table

“Management has concerns. I find that management frequently survives by having concerns.”

### The rule underneath

His intelligence and unwillingness to sacrifice himself are confirmed; exact abilities remain pending. His death is permanent loss and harms Kief in an unspecified way.
