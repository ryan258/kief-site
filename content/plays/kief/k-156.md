Give Dragon’s Breath to a willing teammate whose next Action can make good use of it.

### When to use it

A teammate has a safe cone angle and wants the job.

### Mechanics

1. Ask before casting *Dragon’s Breath*; it uses their Magic action to exhale.
2. Cast with a Bonus Action at touch range and maintain concentration.
3. Let the recipient choose whether each later cone is worth their Action and its collateral risk.

### At the table

“Would you like to handle the spicy announcement?”

### The rule underneath

The spell does not grant free attacks or spare Actions. Do not assume Mr. Big will volunteer or that Careful protects allies on later exhalations.
