Select Chromatic Orb only after confirming its real component and the desired damage type.

### When to use it

An opponent resists your ordinary fire or cold option.

### Mechanics

1. Confirm ownership of the 50+ GP diamond required by *Chromatic Orb*.
2. Choose a supported damage type based on actual evidence and make the ranged spell attack.
3. Resolve any leap only when the dice meet its condition and another eligible target exists.

### At the table

“Today’s blend depends on what you are actually resistant to.”

### The rule underneath

The diamond is not consumed but must exist. Subtle does not remove a priced component; the spell is not available merely because it appears on the list.
