Create a cup of temporary water to demonstrate a mundane container.

### When to use it

A seller or artisan needs to show how a spout or catch tray works.

### Mechanics

1. Get permission and an open container.
2. Use *Elementalism* to create one cup of water, then let the owner demonstrate their device.
3. Tell everyone that the water evaporates in 1 minute.

### At the table

“A demonstration batch. Please do not build your reservoir around it.”

### The rule underneath

The short duration makes this unsuitable for stored water or a promised permanent resource. The spell tests no property beyond what you actually observe.
