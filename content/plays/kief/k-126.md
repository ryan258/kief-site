Offer something an animal wants for a specific, voluntary observation.

### When to use it

A local Beast knows the area but has no reason to help.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Speak with Animals* and ask what matters to it.
2. Offer a real, available benefit and ask one bounded question.
3. Accept refusal or a social check rather than repeating the demand.

### At the table

“A fair exchange: one answer, and a snack that actually exists.”

### The rule underneath

The spell permits communication, not control. Do not promise food from unknown inventory or turn a bargain into compelled scouting through danger.
