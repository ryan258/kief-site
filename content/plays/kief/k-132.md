Ask what stopped arriving instead of asking a town to explain its entire curse.

### When to use it

Local people are overwhelmed by a broad mystery.

### Mechanics

1. Ask a merchant about one missing shipment or changed ingredient.
2. Compare the date and route with other concrete reports.
3. Offer the clue to the party’s investigator rather than turning a hunch into a verdict.

### At the table

“When did the cinnamon stop coming?”

### The rule underneath

The merchant background provides a conversational angle, not free knowledge or a new proficiency. The clue matters only if the DM establishes it.
