Tell allies exactly which enemies must remain undamaged while they handle the others.

### When to use it

Hypnotic Pattern has affected some, but not all, opponents.

### Mechanics

1. Name the affected creatures after the DM resolves saves against *Hypnotic Pattern*.
2. Ask allies to direct damage and area effects away from them.
3. Choose a route or target order before the minute expires.

### At the table

“The quiet ones remain quiet. Everyone else gets our attention.”

### The rule underneath

Damage or an Action shaking a target ends its effect. The creatures are Incapacitated, not Unconscious, and allies cannot assume arbitrary actions against them are free.
