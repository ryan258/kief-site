Damage from Fire Bolt or Ray of Frost can force a concentration save after an enemy spell is already running.

### When to use it

- An enemy is maintaining a dangerous spell and Counterspell is too late.
- You can attack the concentrating caster without abandoning a more urgent objective.

### Mechanics

1. Choose [Fire Bolt](../k-6/) for 2d10 fire at 120 feet, or [Ray of Frost](../k-8/) for 2d8 cold at 60 feet and a 10-foot Speed reduction on a hit.
2. Both use a +6 spell attack; Innate Sorcery grants Advantage when active. Neither guarantees damage.
3. Damage forces a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration, normally DC 10 or half the damage taken (rounded down), whichever is higher, capped at DC 30 under the 2024 rules.
4. Separate damage instances can force separate saves, but the chance to hit and amount of damage both matter. Coordinate with the party.
5. Elementalism cannot replace this damage or impose a saving-throw penalty.

### At the table

Mechanically: “Ray of Frost on the caster holding the charm. Plus six to hit; if it deals damage, that also forces a concentration save.”

Verbally: *“Kief gives the priest’s sermon a sudden cold draft.”*

### The rule underneath

Counterspell interrupts casting; damage can break concentration after a spell is running. Neither removes a spell that does not require concentration. The former Mind Sliver timing question is inactive for this build.
