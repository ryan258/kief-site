Insight +3, Passive Insight 13, and a ritual involving a silent cow that
is either a bit or a genuine oracle. Kief has never clarified.

### When to use it

- Reading a counterparty before Persuasion commits to a line.
- A tough social call where the table enjoys a pause: Kief turns to Mr.
  Big, waits, and interprets the blink.

### Mechanics

1. Wisdom (Insight) **+3**, Passive **13**. Good enough for surface reads.
   Compare notes with whoever reads people better.
2. The Cow Consultation: defer to Mr. Big and interpret his blinks. It's
   Kief's own Insight with a dramatic pause. Liars may find it unsettling;
   that's a DM call, not a bonus.
3. Mr. Big has real senses: Perception +3, Passive 13, Darkvision 60. He
   can report what he actually noticed, filtered through a cat's
   understanding.
4. **The flaw, played honestly**: Kief overstates how much Mr. Big
   understands. When lives are at stake, he listens to the party. The
   cow's opinion is flavor, not a veto.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before I answer the baron, I look to Mr. Big. …He blinked
twice, slowly. That's a no. Practically: Insight plus three on the baron.
Does he believe what he's saying?"

Verbally: *"The baron waits, increasingly unnerved, while the gnome and the
tiny cow hold a long silent conference. 'Mr. Big has concerns,' Kief
reports finally. 'I share them.'"*

### The rule underneath

Insight is a read, not a lie detector. It is often opposed by Deception,
and its passive score is what the DM checks when you aren't actively
probing. Character quirks are best when they create play without taking
decisions away from other players. Triangulate a hunch with facts before
betting the party on it.
