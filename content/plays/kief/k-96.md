Give an ally the dramatic entrance instead of taking it yourself.

### When to use it

A teammate is about to make the important appeal or performance.

### Mechanics

1. Ask what simple flourish they would enjoy.
2. Use *Elementalism* to ripple their cloak or nearby banners with a breeze.
3. Stop speaking and let their character take the scene.

### At the table

“I handle weather. They handle diplomacy.”

### The rule underneath

The breeze is modest and local. It does not grant flight, move creatures, or ensure advantage; the ally’s own words and abilities do the work.
