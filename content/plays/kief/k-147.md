Use Kief’s mobility to open a real exit for everyone else.

### When to use it

You can reach a mundane latch or barrier from a safe visible destination.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Misty Step* only if the landing is visible and within 30 feet.
2. Use your Action to operate the accessible latch if the DM judges it feasible.
3. Signal the party to move through under their own power.

### At the table

“I will be on the other side, being useful rather than mysterious.”

### The rule underneath

Teleporting does not bypass a locked mechanism for the group by itself. Heavy, trapped, or magical closures may need more than a free hand and an Action.
