Spend a third-level slot only when making two allies invisible serves one clear plan.

### When to use it

Two willing allies need the same quiet approach.

### Mechanics

1. Check that a 3rd-level slot is available and worth spending instead of major control.
2. Cast *Invisibility* at 3rd level to affect two touched targets.
3. Agree on the same return plan; track each target’s spell-ending actions.

### At the table

“Two absences, one concentration problem.”

### The rule underneath

Upcasting adds a target, not silence or flight. One concentration ending removes the effect from both; do not combine it with Fly as if Kief could maintain both.
