Rehearse a simple audible signal before a noisy operation.

### When to use it

The group needs a recognizable cue that does not require shouted names.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Minor Illusion* to demonstrate the agreed sound at a safe moment.
2. Make sure everyone distinguishes it from ordinary background noise.
3. Use a fresh casting during the operation, within its 30-foot placement range.

### At the table

“Three kettle whistles means leave. One means the kettle.”

### The rule underneath

The sound is audible to enemies too. It is not an unlimited-range message or an automatic response to danger.
