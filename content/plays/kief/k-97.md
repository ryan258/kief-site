Create a smoke shape, then provide its voice yourself.

### When to use it

The party has time for a short story at an existing fire.

### Mechanics

1. Use existing smoke with *Elementalism* to make one crude shape within a 1-foot cube.
2. Speak its dialogue normally; if you need new smoke first, that is a separate casting.
3. Invite a teammate to provide the next line rather than narrating the whole scene.

### At the table

“The dragon was small, poorly funded, and very sure of itself.”

### The rule underneath

The sculpted shape is not animated and has no voice of its own. Two effects do not become a new spell; each casting consumes its own Action.

Use the [current spellbook](/spellbook/) and [table rulings](/rulings/) for the spell details and campaign overrides.
