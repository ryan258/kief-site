Three free casts a day, plus the ritual. The cheapest espionage in the
game, run by a man animals seem to trust on sight.

### When to use it

- Any settlement, wilderness, or ruin with something alive in it: rats,
  crows, dogs, stabled horses.
- Checking testimony: the witness lies; his dog saw who came in.

### Mechanics

1. 1st level, Action, self, **10 minutes**: understand and speak with
   Beasts, and use the Influence action's skill options with them.
2. **3 free casts per Long Rest** (Forest Gnome), or cast it as a **ritual**
   for 10 extra minutes and no slot. Spend the free casts when those
   minutes don't exist.
3. Beasts know what beasts notice: who came through, when, what noise they
   made. Their understanding is limited, so ask simple, concrete questions.
4. A favor may need an Influence check. Kief's Persuasion +6 applies.
5. It does not work on Mr. Big, who is Fey. The telepathy is better, and
   he wasn't going to say anything anyway.
6. Not a Sorcerer spell on this sheet, so Innate Sorcery does nothing for
   it. Track the free uses on the dashboard.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Free cast of Speak with Animals, that's one of three today.
Ten minutes with the tavern's cat: who's been down to the cellar since
last night, and were they carrying anything?"

Verbally: *"The cat talks for a while. Kief nods seriously, refills its
saucer, and comes back with the smuggler's schedule. 'Management has a few
questions for the pigeon, next.'"*

### The rule underneath

The ritual tag means castable with 10 extra minutes and no slot. The
Forest Gnome trait adds free normal casts equal to proficiency bonus per
Long Rest, a common 2024 pattern. Beast-only, 10-minute duration,
comprehension not command: favors are Influence checks. Free, renewable
information channels are the quiet power of caster utility; use them
daily.
