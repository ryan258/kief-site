The baseline round: 2d10 fire at 120 feet, every round, for free.

### When to use it

- Any round without a better play: no cluster for *Fireball*, no control
  worth a slot, and Kief is not already holding a spell that makes Dodge
  the better turn.
- Long days when slots must be rationed.

### Mechanics

1. Ranged spell attack **+6**, range 120 feet, **2d10 fire** at level 5
   (average 11).
2. No damage bonus yet. Elemental Affinity arrives at level 6.
3. Under Innate Sorcery ([play 45](../k-45/)): still +6, but with
   **Advantage**, the accuracy fix for high-AC targets.
4. A miss is a miss ([play 17](../k-17/)). Against targets resistant or
   immune to fire, switch to [Ray of Frost](../k-8/) or
   [Mind Sliver](../k-16/).
5. It ignites flammable objects not worn or carried ([play 7](../k-7/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fire Bolt at the captain, plus six to hit… 18. That's 2d10
fire."

Verbally: *"The bolt leaves Kief's palm the color of a coal that has been
thinking about it all afternoon."*

### The rule underneath

Cantrips scale with character level, not class level or slots: Fire Bolt
is 2d10 at levels 5–10 and 3d10 at 11. Attack-roll cantrips use your spell
attack bonus (proficiency + casting modifier), and misses deal nothing.
Advantage from Innate Sorcery applies because Fire Bolt is a Sorcerer
spell on this sheet.
