Use aquatic adaptation for an inspection, with a mundane route back.

### When to use it

A submerged passage needs a brief look and the party can establish a safe plan.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Alter Self* for aquatic adaptation: water breathing and a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.
2. Arrange a real guide rope or other agreed route if available.
3. Take only the inspection you can safely complete while concentrating.

### At the table

“I am going to ask the basement why it is a lake.”

### The rule underneath

Water breathing is not protection from currents, pressure, poison, or an unknown liquid. Candy-liquid scope for Shape Water does not make every river breathable.
