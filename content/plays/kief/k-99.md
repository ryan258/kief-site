Shape an existing flame into a crude emblem while the storyteller speaks.

### When to use it

A safe hearth provides a backdrop for someone else’s tale.

### Mechanics

1. Confirm there is no nearby flammable decoration at risk.
2. Use *Elementalism* to sculpt a small portion of fire into a crude shape within a 1-foot cube.
3. Leave the storyteller in charge of what it represents.

### At the table

“One royal crest, lightly toasted.”

### The rule underneath

Shaping fire does not extinguish it or enlarge its damage. The effect is a visual prop, not a fire barrier, creature, or protective ward.
