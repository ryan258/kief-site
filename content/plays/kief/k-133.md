Look for conflicting names in accounts of the same landmark.

### When to use it

An old story and a current sign name a place differently.

### Mechanics

1. Ask locals which name their grandparents used.
2. Use History if requested to examine the older reference.
3. Keep both names on the map so future witnesses can recognize either.

### At the table

“Perhaps the bridge changed its name to avoid the bill.”

### The rule underneath

A naming mismatch is a lead, not proof of a hidden portal or magical identity. Do not invent the kingdom’s history to fill the gap.
