The follow-through for [play 2](../k-2/). A cube of Charmed statues is a
resource, and spending it well is its own play.

### When to use it

- The pattern landed and the immediate threat is paused. Now: which
  sleepers wake, in what order, and to what?

### Mechanics

1. The effect ends for a creature the moment it **takes any damage** or
   someone uses an action to shake it awake. Untouched sleepers stay down
   while Kief concentrates, up to a minute.
2. **Tell the party which enemies are affected.** Area damage, a stray
   *Fireball*, or a cleaving ally can wake all of them at once.
3. **Fight the free ones first.** Deal with the enemies who passed their
   saves while the others stand there. Waking a sleeper should be a choice.
4. **One at a time**: if the party chooses to fight the sleepers, surround
   one, wake it with the opening hit, and finish it before touching the
   next.
5. **Tie them up or walk away**: ten rounds is enough for either
   ([play 72](../k-72/)). Rope has to be in someone's pack; Kief's gear is
   still pending.
6. Kief holds Concentration through all of it. His job is to not get hit.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Four are Charmed: the two on the left and both archers. The
sergeant and the dog passed. Everyone on those two first, and nobody touch
the sleepers."

Verbally: *"Kief moves down the line of statue-still raiders like a man
choosing produce. 'Not yet,' he tells Brenna's axe. 'Let's all take a
moment. Them especially.'"*

### The rule underneath

A duration of "up to 1 minute" plus per-creature end conditions makes the
effect a resource with a timer: each Charmed target stays down until
damaged or shaken awake. The party controls the wake order. Binding,
leaving, and fighting are all legal choices, and none of them re-rolls the
save. Reading end conditions turns "the spell worked" into "we planned the
minute."
