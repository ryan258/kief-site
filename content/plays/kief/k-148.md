Slow the one fleeing enemy whose arrival would make the next scene worse.

### When to use it

A visible runner is within 60 feet and stopping pursuit matters.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Ray of Frost* for a ranged spell attack, +6 before situational modifiers.
2. On a hit, apply 2d8 cold and a 10-foot Speed reduction until your next turn starts.
3. Tell the faster teammate the result so they can decide whether to pursue.

### At the table

“A moment, please. Your dramatic exit is ahead of schedule.”

### The rule underneath

The spell reduces Speed; it does not automatically stop Dash, teleportation, or reaching an alarm. Missing supplies neither damage nor the reduction.
