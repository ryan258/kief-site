Check where frightened enemies might run before using Fear.

### When to use it

Driving opponents away seems better than holding them in place.

### Mechanics

1. Look for escape routes, allies, alarms, and other groups beyond the cone.
2. Cast *Fear* only when the likely flight helps the party’s objective.
3. Be ready for fleeing creatures to reach cover and potentially save again.

### At the table

“Before we frighten them, where would a sensible coward go?”

### The rule underneath

Fear is concentration and uses the targets’ actual rules and immunities. Fleeing toward reinforcements can make a technically successful spell a poor decision.
