The plain casting, for when no ally is anywhere near the blast. Keep the
Sorcery Point.

### When to use it

- Three or more dangerous enemies clustered, allies clear of the 20-foot
  radius.
- Softening a formation before it closes. The 150-foot range means the
  fight starts on the party's schedule.
- Control won't solve it, and damage is needed now.

### Mechanics

1. Action, 3rd level, 150 feet, 20-foot-radius sphere: Dexterity save
   against DC 14 (15 under Innate Sorcery), **8d6 fire** on a failure,
   half on a success.
2. No allies in the blast means no Careful Spell, so the SP stays banked.
   Check the radius twice; "probably clear" is what [play 1](../k-1/) is
   for.
3. No upcasting: 3rd level is Kief's ceiling.
4. It ignites flammable objects not worn or carried.
5. Slot math: **two** 3rd-level slots. Spending one here may leave nothing
   for *Counterspell* or *Hypnotic Pattern*. [Play 30](../k-30/) decides
   first.

### At the table

Mechanically: "They're still forming up at the treeline. Fireball, 3rd
level, 150 feet out, centered on the banner. Dexterity DC 14, 8d6 fire,
half on a success. None of ours is within 20 feet of it."

Verbally: *"Kief watches the war band assemble with the patience of a man
letting the pot come to temperature. Then, gently, it does."*

### The rule underneath

The area-of-effect basics: a point you choose within range, a 20-foot
radius that spreads from that point, and a Dexterity save for half. "Each
creature in the area" includes allies, so friendly fire is avoided only by
positioning or Metamagic. The ignition clause makes every Fireball an
environment change; the DM will remember the barn even if you don't.
