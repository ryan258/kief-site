Combine a real breeze with an ally’s performance, keeping illusion and physics separate.

### When to use it

A willing performer wants one simple atmospheric flourish.

### Mechanics

1. Ask for the cue.
2. Use *Elementalism* for a nearby breeze, or *Minor Illusion* for a sound; choose what the scene needs.
3. If both are wanted, spend separate Actions and do not promise simultaneous casting.

### At the table

“Would you prefer ominous wind or an inexpensive thunder department?”

### The rule underneath

The effects do not add up to control over weather. A flourish is support for the performer, not an automatic bonus or a second full performance.
