Constitution save +7 makes Kief the right person to hold the party's most
important spell. Keeping it is a habit, not luck.

### When to use it

- Every fight where Kief is concentrating. Almost all his control and
  support spells are Concentration.

### Mechanics

1. After taking damage: **Constitution save**, DC **10 or half the damage**,
   whichever is higher (maximum DC 30). At +7, DC 10 fails only on a 1 or
   2.
2. **One Concentration spell at a time.** *Web*, *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Fly*,
   *Invisibility*, *Fear*, *Alter Self*, *Dragon's Breath*, and *Detect
   Magic* all compete. Casting a second one ends the first immediately,
   and a ritual needs Concentration while it's being cast.
3. Incapacitated or dead ends it automatically.
4. Protect the anchor: AC 15, *Shield* spent freely while concentrating,
   and positioning out of reach and behind cover.
5. **Dodge is a real turn.** When the running spell is doing the work,
   making attacks against Kief roll with Disadvantage beats a cantrip.
6. No fire resistance at level 5. Big hits raise the DC; a 30-damage blow
   is DC 15.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'm concentrating on Web, announcing it now. I'm taking the
Dodge action behind the pillar; attacks on me have Disadvantage. If I get
hit, that's a plus seven Constitution save."

Verbally: *"The arrow takes Kief in the shoulder and the web doesn't so
much as shiver. He breathes out slow, the way the moss taught him, and
keeps his grip on the pattern."*

### The rule underneath

The Concentration rules, complete: one effect at a time; a new one ends the
old one; each instance of damage forces a Constitution save at DC 10 or
half the damage, whichever is higher; Incapacitated or death ends it. A
concentration caster's defense is a stack of AC, save bonus, and position.
Losing a spell that is disabling four enemies costs far more than the
Reaction or turn it would have taken to protect it.
