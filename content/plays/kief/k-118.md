Read and document a torn clue before making it look untouched.

### When to use it

A torn letter or map has been recovered with its pieces.

### Mechanics

1. Let the group examine and record the tear’s pattern and visible writing.
2. Use *Mending* for one break no larger than 1 foot, taking the full minute.
3. Compare the repaired text with what was actually present.

### At the table

“We shall repair the letter after taking its statement.”

### The rule underneath

Repair restores a physical break, not missing words or lost material. A repaired clue is not proof of who tore it.
