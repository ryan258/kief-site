Make a request that someone can refuse without ending the conversation.

### When to use it

A nervous NPC is protecting their own interests.

### Mechanics

1. State a small, concrete request rather than demanding allegiance.
2. Offer a less risky alternative such as an introduction or a public meeting.
3. Accept their boundary and ask who else might know.

### At the table

“If you cannot take us there, could you tell us who sells boots for the journey?”

### The rule underneath

This is ordinary negotiation. It does not depend on charm magic, a guaranteed attitude shift, or treating every refusal as a failed roll to repeat.
