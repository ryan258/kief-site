Arrange a distraction that the whole party knows is coming.

### When to use it

The group needs one quiet minute to examine something nearby.

### Mechanics

1. Have the face request an ordinary demonstration or inspection from the NPC.
2. Kief contributes a brief *Elementalism* flourish only if it supports that request.
3. Let the investigator use their own actions and checks while everyone stays within the agreed boundaries.

### At the table

“Could you show us the original mechanism? I promise to admire it professionally.”

### The rule underneath

A busy NPC is not automatically unaware. This creates an opportunity to attempt something, not a guaranteed blind spot or a license to steal.
