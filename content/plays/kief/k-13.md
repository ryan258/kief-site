Small comforts open more doors than a good roll, and Kief's whole persona
runs through this cantrip.

### When to use it

- Building rapport with witnesses, guards, innkeepers, and anyone cold,
  tired, or bored, always before the ask.
- Social scenes where a gesture of care is plausible. Casting in some
  places is a provocation; read the room.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, range 10 feet. Non-instantaneous effects last up to 1 hour, up
   to **three** at once. Relevant options: warm, chill, or flavor
   nonliving material; clean or soil an object; light or snuff a candle,
   torch, or small campfire; a harmless sensory effect.
2. The ceremony: produce the satchel of blends, heat the water with a snap,
   flavor it toward the guest's taste. Then the Persuasion check (+6).
3. The payoff is the DM's to price: a warmer attitude, a lower DC, or
   Advantage. Ask for it; don't announce it.
4. The blends are flavor. They don't heal, and nobody confirms what is in
   them.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before I ask the sergeant anything: Prestidigitation warms
his tea and makes it taste like his mother made it. Then the question.
Would that make him more willing to hear me out?"

Verbally: *"'Negotiating blend,' Kief says, as steam curls up from a cup
the sergeant doesn't remember accepting. 'Heavy on the honesty. Light on
the consequences.'"*

### The rule underneath

*Prestidigitation* is a utility toolkit that rolls no dice. Its payoff is
adjudicated socially, and the DM's standard levers are attitude, DC, and
Advantage. Groundwork before the ask is legitimate play: describing a
favor gives the DM a reason to set a kinder DC. It remains a request, not
a mechanic.
