Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saves. The license to
attend the enchanter's dinner party.

### When to use it

- Volunteering for exposure the party should avoid: the parley with the
  hag, the cursed library, the whispering idol.
- Fights against enchanters, illusionists, and fear effects.

### Mechanics

1. **Advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws**,
   passively and always. Charm, fear, psychic assaults, and banishment
   are all rolled twice.
2. With Constitution +7, Kief's weak saves are Dexterity (+2) and Strength
   (−1). Area blasts and grapples are the real threat. Position against
   those.
3. The raw saves are modest: Intelligence −1, Wisdom +0, Charisma +6.
   Advantage helps, but it isn't immunity. At Wisdom +0 against DC 15, the
   chance of success is only about 51%.
4. Party doctrine: when someone must listen to the whispering thing, Kief
   volunteers. Keep *Misty Step* ready anyway.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'll parley with the hag. Gnomish Cunning gives me Advantage
on the Wisdom save when she tries whatever she's going to try, and she is
going to try."

Verbally: *"The hag's honeyed suggestion pours over Kief and finds him
already full: of tea, of moss-light, of a hundred and twenty-four years of
not being in a hurry. 'Lovely offer,' he says. 'No.'"*

### The rule underneath

Monsters aim at weak saves, so know your row. Advantage adds reliability to
whatever bonus you have, but a low bonus is still low. At +0 against DC 15,
one die succeeds 30% of the time; with Advantage, the chance that at least
one die succeeds is 1 − 0.7² = 51%. Advantage never stacks with more
Advantage. Sending the character with the strongest relevant save to face
the threat is a party decision, not bravado.
