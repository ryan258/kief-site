One Sorcery Point buys three allies out of a spell's save. The license to
cast area spells a wizard would have to decline.

### When to use it

- Area save spells with allies inside: *Fireball*, *Hypnotic Pattern*,
  *Fear*.
- *Web*, only to protect allies from the first save. They are still in the
  webs afterward.
- Not when allies are clear. The SP stays banked ([play 28](../k-28/)).

### Mechanics

1. **1 SP** when casting a spell that forces other creatures to save:
   choose up to **3** creatures (Charisma modifier). They **automatically
   succeed** and take **no damage** if a success normally halves it.
2. On *Fireball*, the no-damage clause is the value. On *Hypnotic Pattern*
   and *Fear*, auto-success alone does the job.
3. It protects from the **save at casting**, not the terrain. Allies
   protected from *Web*'s initial save still stand in difficult, flammable
   webbing and face new saves when they start a turn there or re-enter.
4. Three is the cap until Charisma rises. At level 8, Charisma 18 makes it
   four.
5. Say the names before the dice.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fear on the whole melee. Careful Spell, one point: Brenna
and Tam auto-succeed. Everyone else, Wisdom DC 14, drop your weapon and
run."

Verbally: *"The terror rolls through the scrum like boiling water through
leaves, and parts, neat as a poured stream, around the two people Kief was
thinking of fondly."*

### The rule underneath

Careful Spell edits who fails a spell's save, not the world the spell
creates. It applies when the spell forces a save as it's cast; later
saves from a lingering area aren't covered. Metamagic is declared as part
of casting, so decide the protected names before the dice exist.
