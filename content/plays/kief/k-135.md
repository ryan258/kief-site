Record the exact trigger and observed effect before spending spells on a theory.

### When to use it

The group encounters a strange repeated phenomenon.

### Mechanics

1. Write down what happened, what immediately preceded it, and what nobody knows yet.
2. Use *Detect Magic* only for the limited information it can add.
3. Ask the party to choose one safe test, with a clear stopping point.

### At the table

“We have a curse-shaped problem. Let us resist supplying it with free research subjects.”

### The rule underneath

Kief has no Remove Curse. Observation and a detected school do not establish a cure; avoid testing a suspected trigger on a companion or bystander.
