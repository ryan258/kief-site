Offer a small comfort before asking a frightened person to explain themselves.

### When to use it

Someone is cold, upset, and willing to accept a drink or cloth.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Prestidigitation* to warm a suitable nonliving item, within its limits.
2. Offer it without making help conditional on an answer.
3. Ask one concrete question, then let the person or another player respond.

### At the table

“No prophecy in this cup. You look as though you have had enough.”

### The rule underneath

Warming an object does not heal, charm, or force trust. A truce still depends on choices, circumstances, and any social check required.
