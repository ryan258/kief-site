Mr. Big's spirit can't be killed, only inconvenienced, as long as the
incense holds out. Current incense stock is **unknown** until the campaign
sets starting gear.

### When to use it

- Before the first adventure: Mr. Big's first-session summoning is not
  established. Plan the hour.
- After Mr. Big drops to 0 HP (he vanishes; the Fey spirit is unharmed).

### Mechanics

1. 1st level via Magic Initiate (Wizard), always prepared. Casting time
   **1 hour**, or **70 minutes** as a ritual with no slot. One free
   non-ritual cast per Long Rest also exists.
2. **Every casting consumes 10+ GP of incense**, including the free one and
   the ritual. Subtle Spell can't skip it.
3. Stock discipline: buy incense in every town and track it like
   ammunition. A spare portion is a spare Mr. Big.
4. At 0 HP the form vanishes, leaving no body. Recasting brings back Mr.
   Big: Fey, Cat stat block, silent, judgmental.
5. Temporary dismissal to a pocket dimension, and recalling him to within
   30 feet, is a Magic action with no ritual and no incense. Dismiss him
   before the obviously fatal room ([play 63](../k-63/)).
6. The cow appearance needs the DM's approval.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We have the evening, so I'm running the Find Familiar
ritual: 70 minutes, one portion of incense. Mr. Big arrives. Please put
'incense, 10 gold' on the shopping list as a standing order."

Verbally: *"The smoke curls into the shape of something small and patient,
and then Mr. Big is simply there, as if he'd stepped out rather than
arrived. He looks at Kief. He does not moo. He has never needed to."*

### The rule underneath

Consumed components are ammunition. The word "consumes" in the component
line is the whole economy: a free casting from a feat waives the slot, not
the incense. A spell you can't afford to cast is a spell you don't have,
so gold is a spellcasting resource in exactly this way.
