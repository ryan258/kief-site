Summon a companion, not a replacement body. Initial summoning and incense stock remain unconfirmed.

### When to use it

Before the first adventure, once the DM confirms the remaining spell details.

### Mechanics

1. The DM allows summoning and putting the familiar away. Once summoned, that individual is yours.
2. The recorded 2024 baseline is 1 hour, or 70 minutes as a ritual, and 10+ GP consumed incense even for the free feat cast. Confirm these details and starting supplies.
3. If Mr. Big dies, he is gone and Kief suffers an unspecified negative effect. Incense does not buy him back.
4. Confirm the cow appearance, stat block, creature type, and dismissal mechanics.

### At the table

“Before we travel, can we settle the summoning details and bring Mr. Big with us?”

### The rule underneath

The DM’s [confirmed familiar ruling](/rulings/#confirmed-campaign-override) governs this play. Remaining 2024 mechanics are provisional until confirmed.
