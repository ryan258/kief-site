A sound where Kief isn't. The cheapest guard-mover in the game.

### When to use it

- A patrol needs to be somewhere else for thirty seconds.
- The party needs a distraction that costs no slot and leaves no trace.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, range 30 feet, duration 1 minute. Create a sound, from
   a whisper to a scream: footsteps, a dropped blade, a voice calling for
   help. A cow mooing is within range. Mr. Big has never mooed.
2. A creature that uses the **Study** action can make an Intelligence
   (Investigation) check against Kief's spell save DC to recognize the
   illusion. *Minor Illusion* comes from Forest Gnome, not the Sorcerer
   list, so Innate Sorcery does not raise its DC; use 14.
3. Guards who merely hear it get no automatic check. They react to what
   they heard. Make the sound something they must investigate.
4. Sound **or** image, never both in one casting. The image version is
   [play 11](../k-11/).
5. The casting itself has a Somatic component. Cast from where nobody is
   watching Kief's hands.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Minor Illusion, 30 feet down the east corridor: the sound
of a bottle smashing and a slurred curse. I want the sentry to go and be
annoyed about it."

Verbally: *"Somewhere in the dark, a drunk who does not exist knocks over
a shelf. Kief, flat against the wall, mouths 'rude' and counts the
footsteps."*

### The rule underneath

Illusions don't force saves on sight. Creatures react to what they
perceive, and only the Study action or physical interaction reveals the
trick. That's why sound decoys are strong: a guard who hears has no reason
to roll. Know which list a spell comes from. Species and feat spells don't
benefit from class features that name "Sorcerer spells."
