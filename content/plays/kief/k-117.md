Ring an ordinary bell from a useful position to signal an evacuation.

### When to use it

A bell rope or small bell is within 30 feet and physically operable.

### Mechanics

1. Agree what the sound means before using it.
2. Use *Mage Hand* to ring the mundane bell if its movement and load are feasible.
3. Spend further Magic actions only if repeated ringing serves the plan.

### At the table

“The evacuation committee is now audible.”

### The rule underneath

This manipulates an ordinary object; it does not enchant the bell or supply an alarm trigger. Enemies hear it too, and timing follows normal turns.
