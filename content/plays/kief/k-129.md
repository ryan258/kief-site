Compare a magical object with a mundane example before asking bigger questions.

### When to use it

An unfamiliar court presents several symbolic objects.

### Mechanics

1. Ask permission to inspect without handling them.
2. Use *Detect Magic* to compare only what the spell can reveal.
3. Invite the historian or owner to explain differences that magic alone cannot.

### At the table

“Which of these is ceremonial, and which takes the ceremony personally?”

### The rule underneath

An absence of a detected aura is not a general safety certificate. Blocked effects and nonmagical hazards remain possible.
