Light the table’s candles while introducing each guest by name.

### When to use it

A host has supplied candles and you want a warm opening.

### Mechanics

1. Ask before using magic in the room.
2. Use *Elementalism* to light candles, torches, or lamps within one eligible 5-foot cube.
3. Give each teammate an introduction that invites them to speak next.

### At the table

“This is our expert. The candle is merely supporting staff.”

### The rule underneath

Igniting real lights has normal visibility and fire consequences. Harmless embers do not create fuel, and a good introduction grants no automatic social bonus.
