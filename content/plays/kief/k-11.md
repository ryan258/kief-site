A crate that blocks a sentry's view, or a threat that was never there.
Same cantrip, opposite directions.

### When to use it

- Caught in the open with seconds to break a line of sight.
- Standoffs where the enemy is counting heads.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, 30 feet, 1 minute: an image of an object no larger
   than a **5-foot cube**: a crate, a barrel stack, a shadowed alcove.
2. Screen mode: the image blocks sight, not attacks. It gives **no cover**.
   The 2024 Hide action needs Heavy Obscurement or real cover, so whether
   Kief can Hide behind an image is the DM's call. Ask, don't assume. Its
   honest use is breaking a line of sight before a guard looks.
3. Anything that physically touches the image passes through it and learns
   it is an illusion. The Study action against DC 14 also sees through it.
4. Phantom mode: an armored figure in a doorway, a crossbow silhouette in a
   window. The image does not move or make sound, so place it where
   stillness is plausible.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before the sentry rounds the corner: Minor Illusion, a
stack of feed sacks in the 5-foot gap between me and his lantern. I'm
Small and I'm crouching. Does that break his line of sight to me?"

Verbally: *"The lantern light slides over a stack of sacks that wasn't
there at dusk. Behind it, a gnome holds his breath and his opinion."*

### The rule underneath

Concealment and cover are different defenses. Cover is a physical barrier
that adds to AC and Dexterity saves; an image with no substance blocks
only sight. Hiding in 2024 requires a DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) check
while Heavily Obscured or behind Three-Quarters or Total Cover. Illusion
players get into trouble when they mistake concealment for cover.
