Know which five people you can catch before crowding the dangerous edge.

### When to use it

A group must work near a fall and Kief is their emergency backup.

### Mechanics

1. Keep the likely falling creatures within sight and 60 feet.
2. Reserve a Reaction and a 1st-level slot for *Feather Fall*.
3. If more than five could fall, change the crossing order rather than promising everyone protection.

### At the table

“Five tickets on the slow elevator. We will take turns.”

### The rule underneath

The spell is a response to falling, not a flight plan. It does not grant horizontal steering or protect against hazards unrelated to the fall.

Use the [current spellbook](/spellbook/) and [table rulings](/rulings/) for the spell details and campaign overrides.
