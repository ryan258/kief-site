Show a clearly declared illusion of an object so nobody risks the original.

### When to use it

The party needs to discuss a valuable or dangerous item’s appearance.

### Mechanics

1. Tell the group this is a reconstruction.
2. Use *Minor Illusion* for a stationary object image within a 5-foot cube.
3. Describe which details you remember and which you are guessing.

### At the table

“An imitation crown. Its taxes are also imaginary.”

### The rule underneath

The illusion does not identify the original or reproduce its magic. Labeling uncertainty keeps a useful demonstration from becoming false evidence.
