Clear the opacity of a small liquid sample before reaching into it.

### When to use it

Something lies in a shallow basin of opaque liquid candy.

### Mechanics

1. Stay where you can see the liquid, within 30 feet.
2. Use *Shape Water* to change its opacity uniformly within the spell’s volume limit.
3. Inspect from outside before asking a teammate to retrieve anything.

### At the table

“Before we put a hand in the pudding, let us meet the pudding.”

### The rule underneath

The DM approved liquid scope. Opacity changes do not identify hazards, remove a curse, or reveal objects concealed by something other than that liquid.
