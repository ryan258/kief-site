Ask a working animal where a journey became frightening.

### When to use it

A returned cart or mount may have witnessed trouble on the road.

### Mechanics

1. With the owner’s cooperation, use *Speak with Animals*.
2. Ask about sounds, smells, and the last place it felt safe.
3. Let a teammate connect those sensory details to geography.

### At the table

“Not who attacked. What changed just before you ran?”

### The rule underneath

Fear and memory are not perfect evidence. Do not expect names, motives, or knowledge the animal could not possess.
