Once a day, a Short Rest hands back up to 2 SP. The party skips it unless
Kief speaks up.

### When to use it

- Midway through the adventuring day, when Kief has spent at least 2 SP.
- When the party also benefits: hit dice, other short-rest features, a
  ritual that needs casting.

### Mechanics

1. At the end of a **Short Rest**, once per Long Rest: regain up to **2
   expended Sorcery Points** (half of Sorcerer level 5, rounded down).
2. 2 SP is two Careful or Subtle Spells, or a 1st-level slot.
3. Timing: after the SP are actually spent. A morning rest restores
   nothing, and the use is gone.
4. Kief's own Hit Dice: **5d6**, each healing d6 + 4. Roll them and adjust HP
   yourself; the dashboard doesn't.
5. On the dashboard, "Short rest: recover SP" applies this once and marks
   the feature used.
6. At level 6 it becomes up to 3 SP.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we hit the keep, can we take an hour? Everyone spends
Hit Dice, and I recover 2 Sorcery Points, once a day. I'll put the kettle
on; this is literally my job."

Verbally: *"'The universe,' Kief announces, unpacking the satchel, 'unfolds
at exactly the speed it intends. The universe is suggesting lunch.'"*

### The rule underneath

Short Rests last 1 hour and are opt-in. Features that key off them are
worthless if the party never rests, which makes asking for one a rules
skill. Know which party members recharge on a Short Rest and call the hour
when the combined value is worth the time.
