Keep hands away from an unknown substance while transporting its container.

### When to use it

Someone has already placed a sample in a closed, light container.

### Mechanics

1. Confirm the whole container weighs no more than 10 pounds.
2. Use *Mage Hand* to move it to the investigator’s chosen surface.
3. Keep people clear and let the investigator handle opening it.

### At the table

“One jar of unanswered questions, delivered.”

### The rule underneath

Distance is not immunity. The hand does not neutralize fumes, magical triggers, or a container’s hazards; nothing here identifies the contents.
