Compare accounts without deciding in advance which witness is lying.

### When to use it

A person and a Beast describe the same incident differently.

### Mechanics

1. Hear each account without feeding it the other’s wording.
2. Use *Speak with Animals* for the Beast’s own sensory perspective.
3. Ask the party what difference in viewpoint could explain the mismatch.

### At the table

“The guard saw a cloak. The dog smelled someone familiar. Both may be telling us something.”

### The rule underneath

Different perceptions are not automatic proof of deception. Insight can inform an assessment but does not replace evidence or read minds.
