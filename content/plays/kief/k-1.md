The emergency damage play. *Fireball* into a scrum with friends inside it,
and Careful Spell buys up to three of them out of the blast for 1 Sorcery
Point.

### When to use it

- Three or more dangerous enemies are clustered inside a 20-foot radius.
- Allies are mixed into that cluster, which is exactly what the Sorcery
  Point is for.
- The fight is worth one of Kief's **two** 3rd-level slots. Two weakened
  enemies get a cantrip instead. If control would end the danger without
  damage, [Careful Hypnotic Pattern](../k-2/) usually comes first.

### Mechanics

1. Action: cast *Fireball* (3rd level). Range 150 feet, 20-foot-radius
   sphere.
2. Careful Spell (1 SP): choose up to **3** creatures in the area (Charisma
   modifier). They automatically succeed on the save and take **no
   damage**.
3. Everyone else makes a Dexterity save against DC 14 (15 under Innate
   Sorcery): **8d6 fire** on a failure, half on a success.
4. No Elemental Affinity yet. The +3 fire bonus and fire resistance arrive
   at level 6, not now.
5. More than three allies in the radius? Move the point. Careful cannot be
   stretched, and Kief cannot upcast past 3rd level.
6. *Fireball* ignites flammable objects not worn or carried. Say so before
   the DM has to.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fireball, 3rd level, centered behind the ogre, 20-foot
radius. Careful Spell, one Sorcery Point: Brenna, Tam, and the prisoner
auto-succeed and take nothing. Everyone else, Dexterity save DC 14.
Rolling 8d6."

Verbally: *"Kief exhales like a man blowing on hot tea, and the room
catches fire everywhere his friends aren't. 'Bit oversteeped,' he says."*

Name the protected creatures before the dice. That is a declaration, not a
retcon.

### The rule underneath

Save-for-half spells read "half as much damage on a successful save." That
clause is what Careful Spell hooks: protected creatures auto-succeed and
take zero instead of half. Metamagic is declared as part of casting and
costs Sorcery Points, a resource separate from slots. Only one Metamagic
option applies to a spell at level 5, so this Fireball cannot also be
Subtle.
