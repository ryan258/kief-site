Buy the climber time through an ordinary conversation instead of starting a second concentration spell.

### When to use it

You are maintaining Fly or Invisibility and someone approaches.

### Mechanics

1. Keep concentration on the teammate’s safety.
2. Use conversation, movement, or a suitable non-concentration cantrip rather than casually switching to *Detect Magic* or *Alter Self*.
3. Tell the ally if the situation forces a change.

### At the table

“While we wait, may I ask who designed this exceptionally interesting door?”

### The rule underneath

A distraction is not automatic control. The main tactic is avoiding an accidental concentration handoff that exposes the teammate you are supporting.
