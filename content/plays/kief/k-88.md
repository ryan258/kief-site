Close an unheld shutter as the agreed cue for an ally to begin.

### When to use it

The room has a convenient shutter and a teammate waiting on timing.

### Mechanics

1. Check that the shutter is unheld and within 30 feet.
2. Use *Elementalism* to create the breeze that closes it.
3. Let the ally take their own action; your cue does not move their turn.

### At the table

“When the shutter closes, the sensible member of this operation begins.”

### The rule underneath

Beckon Air can close an unheld door or shutter. It does not latch, lock, slam for damage, or force one against resistance.
