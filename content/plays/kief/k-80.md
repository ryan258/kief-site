Demonstrate magic on your own sample instead of altering a merchant’s stock.

### When to use it

Kief wants to impress a vendor without damaging the relationship.

### Mechanics

1. Ask permission and obtain a small disposable sample.
2. Use *Shape Water* to form a simple bow or wave in it.
3. End the demonstration and discuss the actual goods at their actual price.

### At the table

“The syrup performs. The price, unfortunately, remains seated.”

### The rule underneath

A performance is an opening for conversation, not automatic Persuasion. Do not promise the altered sample is safe to consume or permanently improved.
