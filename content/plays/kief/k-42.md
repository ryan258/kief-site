A bag of caltrops, zero magic, and the floor fights for free. **Pending
purchase.**

### When to use it

- Seeding a retreat path, a doorway, or the approach to camp before trouble
  arrives.
- Layering under control Kief is already concentrating on.

### Mechanics

1. **Utilize action**: spread the bag over a **5-foot square** within 5
   feet of Kief.
2. A creature entering the area for the first time on a turn makes a **DC 15
   Dexterity save** or takes 1 piercing damage and has **Speed 0 until the
   start of its next turn**.
3. The damage is nothing; the immediate stop is the value. A stopped
   charger is a target, and the party gets a turn to use the space.
4. Recovering them takes 10 minutes. Enemies who see them may step around
   them, so scatter them in the dark or in a gap that has no other route.
5. Pre-fight placement costs no slot or Concentration. In initiative, it
   costs Kief's action.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we camp: caltrops across the gap between the two
boulders. First entry each turn: DC 15 Dexterity or 1 damage and Speed 0
until the start of its next turn."

Verbally: *"Kief sows the path like a gardener, humming. Anything that
hurries through the dark tonight will learn the local philosophy: slow
down."*

### The rule underneath

Adventuring gear has real rules. A Utilize action and 1 GP buy movement
denial that stacks under any Concentration you're already running. Before
solving a problem with magic, check the equipment list; it never competes
with your slots.
