Ask for Drop when the held object matters more than the enemy’s hit points.

### When to use it

A visible creature holds the item the party needs to recover.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Command* with Drop within 60 feet.
2. If the save fails, wait for the target’s next turn when it drops held items and ends the turn.
3. Have a teammate plan ordinary retrieval with attention to reach and initiative.

### At the table

“Would you put that down? It has been through enough leadership.”

### The rule underneath

The spell does not teleport the item to you or make retrieval safe. Carried objects that are not held and contested access require separate adjudication.
