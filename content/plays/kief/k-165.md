Use Subtle for a necessary casting when its ordinary components would disrupt a delicate moment.

### When to use it

You need a modest spell effect but cannot comfortably speak or gesture.

### Mechanics

1. Choose a spell whose effect serves a clear purpose, such as *Elementalism*.
2. Spend 1 SP on Subtle Spell if removing its components is worth the limited resource.
3. Describe the effect honestly; observers may still notice it.

### At the table

“A small improvement to the atmosphere, with no announcement.”

### The rule underneath

Subtle removes eligible components, not visible effects, consequences, priced materials, or consumed materials. It is not guaranteed anonymity or a free social success.
