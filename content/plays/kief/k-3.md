A d6 sorcerer built like a kettle: AC 15 standing, 20 with *Shield*, 47
HP, and the best Concentration saves at most tables.

### When to use it

- *Shield*: a Reaction after a hit. At AC 15, noncritical totals 15–19
  become misses at AC 20; totals of 20 still hit. Ask for the total if the
  DM shares it. It also stops *Magic Missile*.
- Every fight, passively: the stack is the reason Kief can hold *Hypnotic
  Pattern* or *Web* from the second rank.

### Mechanics

1. Baseline: Draconic Resilience makes AC **15** (10 + Dexterity +
   Charisma) with no armor and no spell. It never costs a slot and never
   turns off.
2. *Shield*: Reaction when hit by an attack roll or targeted by *Magic
   Missile*. +5 AC (15 to 20) until the start of Kief's next turn,
   including against the triggering attack. Every casting costs a
   1st-level slot. Kief has four, and [Feather Fall](../k-33/) wants some.
3. Concentration: Constitution save **+7**. Gnomish Cunning adds Advantage
   on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saves, which covers most spells
   that try to take Kief out of the fight.
4. HP **47** includes +5 from Draconic Resilience. No fire resistance yet.
   Second rank is fine; melee is not ([play 23](../k-23/) is the exit).
5. The Reaction is shared. *Shield*, *Feather Fall*, and *Counterspell*
   compete until Kief's next turn starts.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Does a 17 hit? Then Reaction: Shield, 1st-level slot. AC 20
until my next turn, so it misses. I'm still concentrating on Web."

Verbally: *"The blade skids off air the temperature of a fresh pot. Kief
doesn't spill a drop. 'Careful,' he tells the sword. 'This robe's mostly
repairs.'"*

*Shield* spent on an attack that missed anyway is a slot in the compost.

### The rule underneath

AC is one formula, chosen, not stacked: Draconic Resilience replaces armor
and does not combine with other base AC calculations. *Shield* is a flat
+5 bonus, so it adds to whichever base you use. Reactions happen once per
round, off-turn, on a defined trigger. Asking "does 17 hit?" is legal
information-gathering: you can know the total before spending the
Reaction.
