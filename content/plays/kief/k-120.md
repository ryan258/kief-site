Return a repaired possession with an honest account of what happened.

### When to use it

The party damaged a borrowed item and wants to keep a relationship.

### Mechanics

1. Collect the pieces and use *Mending* if the damage qualifies.
2. Tell the owner what was repaired and what remains uncertain.
3. Offer compensation for damage the spell cannot address.

### At the table

“Good news: the handle. Bad news: the story attached to the handle.”

### The rule underneath

The spell leaves no trace of the repaired break, but it does not erase witnesses, ownership, or consequences. Use the repair to support accountability.
