Repair one qualifying tear in a ferryman’s gear before discussing passage.

### When to use it

The owner has a small damaged object and time to let you help.

### Mechanics

1. Ask permission and identify one complete break or tear.
2. Cast *Mending* for a minute if the break fits the spell’s limit.
3. Ask about the route or fare without claiming the repair purchases obedience.

### At the table

“A stitch first. A question afterward.”

### The rule underneath

A repaired strap does not certify the entire boat or its other parts. The owner decides whether the favor changes the bargain.
