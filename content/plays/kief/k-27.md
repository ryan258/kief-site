Give a willing ally a 15-foot breath weapon they can use every turn. It is
an alternative use of Concentration, so agree on the plan with the
recipient.

### When to use it

- A longer fight, with an ally whose own actions are weaker than a 3d6 cone
  each round.
- On Mr. Big, if the DM agrees that his "can't attack" limit doesn't bar a
  Magic action that forces a save ([Mr. Big play 4](../../mr-big/mb-4/)).
- Not when *Web* or *Hypnotic Pattern* would do more with the same
  Concentration.

### Mechanics

1. 2nd level (Draconic Sorcery, always prepared), **Bonus Action**, touch,
   Concentration up to 1 minute. Choose acid, cold, fire, lightning, or
   poison.
2. Until the spell ends, the recipient can take a **Magic action** to
   exhale a **15-foot cone**: Dexterity save against Kief's spell save DC
   (14; ask the DM whether Innate Sorcery's +1 applies while it is
   active), **3d6** of the chosen type, half on a success.
3. The recipient spends its own action each time. A fighter giving up an
   Attack action is often a bad trade; a hireling or a familiar may not be.
4. Bonus Action casting spends the turn's one slot. Kief's Action can still
   be a cantrip.
5. Careful Spell doesn't apply: the casting forces no save, the exhalation
   does. Aim the cone away from allies.
6. If Kief loses Concentration, the breath is gone mid-fight.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Bonus Action: Dragon's Breath on you, Tam, fire,
concentrating. On your turn you can use a Magic action for a 15-foot cone:
Dexterity DC 14, 3d6 fire. My Action is still free: Ray of Frost."

Verbally: *"Kief taps Tam's shoulder and a warmth settles in the fighter's
chest like a very strong cup of something. 'Breathe out slow,' Kief
advises. 'Away from me.'"*

### The rule underneath

Buffs cast on others often transfer an action cost along with the benefit:
the exhalation uses the recipient's Magic action. Concentration stays with
the caster even when the effect rides someone else. Metamagic applies to
the spell when cast, so riders that trigger later, like this cone, can't
be made Careful.
