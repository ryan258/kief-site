Choose a harmless distraction over a fake disaster that endangers bystanders.

### When to use it

The party wants to clear a public space with an illusion.

### Mechanics

1. State what you actually need: one guard’s attention, a clear doorway, or time to speak.
2. Propose a bounded *Minor Illusion* sound rather than inventing a fire or attack.
3. Let the face negotiate if the audience does not respond as hoped.

### At the table

“We need the clerk to look left. We do not need a stampede.”

### The rule underneath

The sound does not compel behavior. Consider the crowd’s likely response before calling a distraction harmless; the DM determines what people do.
