Teleport to a visible ledge, then help the party establish a mundane route.

### When to use it

A short gap has a visible safe landing and an actual rope is available.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Misty Step* to reach the ledge within 30 feet.
2. On a suitable action, attach the carried rope to a real anchor if feasible.
3. Let the party assess and use the crossing; do not declare that the rope makes climbing automatic.

### At the table

“First the gnome. Then the paperwork for everybody else.”

### The rule underneath

A rope cannot silently teleport everyone or exceed its length. Anchors, loads, carrying, and climbing are ordinary physical questions for the DM.
