Move nearby liquid into a waiting container without putting a hand in it.

### When to use it

A small sample is needed from an awkward but visible spill.

### Mechanics

1. Have an ally place an open container within 5 feet of the liquid.
2. Use *Shape Water* from within 30 feet to redirect a small amount toward the opening.
3. Ask the DM whether the geometry permits the transfer; seal and label it normally.

### At the table

“The bucket approaches the mystery. We remain over here.”

### The rule underneath

Neither the spell nor the container identifies or neutralizes the sample. It cannot pull unseen liquid through walls or supply unlimited lifting force.
