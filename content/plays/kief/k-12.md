Thirty feet of distance between Kief and everything designed to hurt
whoever's standing in the wrong place.

### When to use it

- A found trigger (pressure plate, tripwire, suspicious chest) after
  someone has guessed what it does.
- The object is unattended, under 10 pounds, and walking to it is the
  dangerous part: keys on hooks, a vial across a battlefield.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip (Magic Initiate, Charisma-based), Action, range 30 feet,
   duration 1 minute: a spectral hand appears.
2. As a **Magic action**, control the hand: manipulate an object, open an
   unlocked door or container, stow or retrieve an item from an open
   container, or pour out a vial. Move it up to 30 feet each time.
3. It **can't attack, activate magic items, or carry more than 10
   pounds**. It vanishes if it ends up more than 30 feet from Kief.
4. Everyone stands clear of the guessed blast radius before the hand pulls.
   Say the guess out loud. The hand does not detect traps.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Everyone behind the column. Mage Hand presses the plate
from 30 feet. If the ceiling's coming down, it's coming down on a hand
that isn't attached to anybody."

Verbally: *"A hand of pale light pats the pressure plate the way Kief pats
Mr. Big. The darts hiss out and skewer a fine stretch of nothing."*

### The rule underneath

*Mage Hand* teaches the attended and unattended object rule: the hand
handles unattended objects within its limits, while worn or guarded items
are someone else's Sleight of Hand problem. Controlling it is a Magic
action every time. The trap-tripping use works because distance is the
mitigation the rules respect: the trigger resolves where it is, not where
Kief is.
