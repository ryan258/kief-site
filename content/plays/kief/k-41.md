The old record's two potions are **historical**, not confirmed starting
gear. This play applies only once potions are actually in someone's pack.

### When to use it

- An ally is down and within reach: a potion both stabilizes and brings
  them back into the fight.
- Kief is low between fights, or the next hit would drop him while he
  holds an important spell.

### Mechanics

1. Potion of Healing: **2d4 + 2** HP. Drinking one, **or administering it
   to a creature within 5 feet**, is a **Bonus Action**.
2. The Bonus Action competes with Innate Sorcery, *Misty Step*,
   *Dragon's Breath*, and creating a slot with Font of Magic. Plan which
   turn gets which.
3. A Bonus Action potion leaves Kief's Action free: potion to the fallen
   ally, then *Mind Sliver* on the enemy standing over them.
4. Mage Hand cannot pour a potion into a creature. It can carry the bottle
   ([play 12](../k-12/)).
5. Log consumption immediately. Potions are gone the moment they're used.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Bonus Action: I pour the potion into Tam, 2d4 plus 2, he's
back up. Action: Ray of Frost on the wolf so it can't close on him."

Verbally: *"Kief unstoppers the bottle and administers it like the waking
blend: no haste, some ceremony, immediate improvement."*

### The rule underneath

In 2024, potions are a Bonus Action to drink or to give to someone within 5
feet. That changes the math: a downed ally at 0 HP is back up before their
next turn, and the helper still has an Action. Any healing on a creature at
0 HP wakes it, which is why a small heal on the right target beats a large
one later.
