The resource script from waking up to the last fight. Every doctrine above,
in firing order.

### When to use it

- Every adventuring day. Deviate deliberately.

### Mechanics

1. **Morning**: is Mr. Big summoned? (First session: 1 hour, consumed
   incense.) Count aloud once: **47 HP, 5 SP, slots 4/3/2, Innate Sorcery
   2, Speak with Animals 3, Find Familiar 1, Hit Dice 5d6**, plus whatever
   gear actually exists.
2. **Early fights**: cantrips, *Web*, *Command*, *Mind Sliver*. 0–1 SP.
   Innate Sorcery only if the fight turns serious.
3. **Rituals between scenes**: *Detect Magic* and *Speak with Animals* cost
   time, not slots.
4. **Midday**: ask for a Short Rest once SP are spent. Sorcerous Restoration
   returns up to **2 SP** ([play 47](../k-47/)).
5. **The big fight**: Innate Sorcery, a 3rd-level control spell, Careful as
   the geometry requires ([play 65](../k-65/)).
6. **The floor**: a 1st-level slot for *Shield* or *Feather Fall*, and,
   when a caster is possible, a 3rd-level slot for *Counterspell*. A known
   exit ([play 70](../k-70/)).
7. **Evening**: note spent consumables. Long Rest resets everything on the
   dashboard.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Status: full slots, five points, Mr. Big present, two
Innate. Plan's standard: cheap fights cheap, rest at midday, the keep gets
the big spells. The floor is a first-level slot and, if they've got a
caster, a third."

Verbally: *"Kief packs the satchel in the same order every morning: blends,
moss, incense, pouch. The ritual of a man who improvises constantly and is
never once unprepared for it."*

### The rule underneath

The adventuring day is the invisible chassis. Slots, Sorcery Points,
feature uses, and consumables drain across encounters and refill on
different rests: Long Rest for most, Short Rest for Sorcerous
Restoration. Pacing is matching spend rate to the day's likely number of
fights. Say your counts at dawn; a resource you aren't tracking is one the
rules have already taken.
