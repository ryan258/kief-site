Inspect a mechanism’s surface without reaching into it first.

### When to use it

A nearby latch or panel might have a visible outline under loose dust.

### Mechanics

1. Stand within 30 feet with a view of the surface.
2. Use an *Elementalism* breeze to stir loose dust and watch what changes.
3. Have the party’s investigator examine any newly visible detail.

### At the table

“We are requesting the door’s résumé before its handshake.”

### The rule underneath

The breeze does not disarm a trap or guarantee that a hidden seam becomes visible. If disturbing dust might trigger something, resolve that risk before casting.
