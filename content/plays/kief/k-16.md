A cantrip that makes someone else's saving-throw effect more likely to
land.

### When to use it

- An ally's save-based spell or effect is coming before the end of Kief's
  next turn: a wizard's *Hold Person*, a cleric's control spell, a
  monk's stunning strike.
- Kief is about to cast a save spell himself on his **next** turn against
  that same creature.
- The target's Intelligence looks weak: beasts, brutes, most undead.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, range 60 feet, Verbal component only. The target makes
   an **Intelligence save** against DC 14 (15 under Innate Sorcery).
2. On a failure: **2d6 psychic**, and the target subtracts **1d4** from the
   **next** saving throw it makes before the end of Kief's next turn.
3. Only the next save is affected. If a teammate's *Web* or a
   concentration check comes first, that save consumes the penalty.
   Coordinate the timing.
4. A successful save means no damage and no penalty. Never promise an ally
   a guaranteed result.
5. The damage can force a concentration save on an enemy caster.
   [Play 39](../k-39/) covers that use.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mind Sliver on the captain, Intelligence save DC 14. If it
fails, 2d6 psychic and his next save before the end of my next turn is
minus 1d4. Mira, your Hold Person goes next."

Verbally: *"Kief squints at the captain as though reading a label in poor
light. 'Your aura's got a loose thread,' he says, and tugs."*

### The rule underneath

Initiative order is part of the spell. "Before the end of your next turn"
covers every creature that acts between Kief's turns, so a setup cantrip
is only as good as its timing. Penalties that apply to "the next save" are
used up by whatever save happens first, including ones nobody planned.
Mind Sliver replaced *Shape Water*: it needs no expanded-source approval
and supports the whole party.
