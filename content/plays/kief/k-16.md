Small elemental effects for wit and whimsy: a breeze, a word in dirt, scented smoke, cool mist, or a crude elemental shape.

### When to use it

- A conversation, introduction, or story would benefit from a small flourish.
- An unheld shutter needs closing, a simple dirt message helps, or the party wants a visual prop.

### Mechanics

1. *Elementalism*: Action, 30 feet, Verbal and Somatic components, no concentration. Choose one effect per casting.
2. **Air:** a breeze in a 5-foot cube stirs cloth, dust, or leaves and can close unheld doors or shutters.
3. **Earth:** a thin dust/sand covering over a 5-foot square, or one word in dirt or sand.
4. **Fire:** harmless embers and colored, scented smoke in a 5-foot cube; can light candles, torches, or lamps. The scent lasts 1 minute.
5. **Water:** cool mist lightly dampens a 5-foot cube, or create 1 cup of clean water in an open container or on a surface; created water evaporates in 1 minute.
6. **Sculpt:** existing dirt, sand, fire, smoke, mist, or water within a 1-foot cube takes a crude shape for 1 hour. This does not grant animation.

### At the table

Mechanically: “There’s smoke from the fire. I use Elementalism to shape a little of it into a crude cow, within a one-foot cube.”

Verbally: *“Management is considering your proposal.”*

### The rule underneath

Elementalism replaces Mind Sliver in the current build. It does not deal damage or penalize saving throws. Its listed effects happen as described; persuasion, distraction, cover, or other consequences are the DM’s call. Keep the flourish brief and leave room for the next player. See [campaign direction](/rulings/#campaign-direction-wit-and-whimsy).
