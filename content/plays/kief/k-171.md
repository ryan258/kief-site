Turn the missing Light cantrip into a shared equipment decision before the dungeon.

### When to use it

The party is about to leave a settlement or safe supply point.

### Mechanics

1. Ask who already owns a light source and fuel.
2. If needed, arrange an actual purchase or borrow with the DM’s inventory and prices.
3. Use *Elementalism* to light eligible candles, lamps, or torches when appropriate.

### At the table

“We have excellent magic. We are still purchasing the lamp.”

### The rule underneath

Elementalism ignites suitable existing lights; it does not supply a permanent torch. Darkvision is individual and does not solve every teammate’s illumination needs.
