The current Cat block has **no Keen Smell**. What it does have is a 40-foot
climb and Jumper, which put Mr. Big where the view is.

### When to use it

- The party needs a look over a wall, into a window, down from the rafters.
- The approach is vertical, and a Tiny climber can get there faster than
  anybody else.

### Mechanics

1. **Climb Speed 40**: walls, trees, shelves, and rafters cost him no extra
   movement.
2. **Jumper**: his jump distance is determined by Dexterity instead of
   Strength. Ask the DM to apply the 2024 jump rules with Dexterity 15.
3. From height, his Perception +3 and Darkvision 60 cover the room.
   Combined with Kief's Bonus Action shared senses, Kief sees the layout
   himself.
4. A high perch also keeps a 2-HP familiar out of melee reach.
5. What he can't do: open heavy windows, lift latches beyond a cat's
   ability, or carry the rope up alone.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big climbs the ivy to the second-floor ledge, Climb 40,
then jumps to the sill. Bonus Action: I look through his eyes into the
room."

Verbally: *"The little cow goes up the wall as if the wall had been
horizontal all along and simply never mentioned it."*

### The rule underneath

A stat block changes when the source changes. The old record's Keen Smell
and Climb 30 came from legacy details; the current 2024 Cat has Climb 40
and Jumper. Tactics built on removed traits must be dropped, not argued
for. Movement types are position tools: a climb speed turns vertical
terrain into ordinary movement.
