Let a teammate narrate while your liquid shapes illustrate their story.

### When to use it

A willing performer wants a small visual accompaniment.

### Mechanics

1. Agree on two simple shapes before the scene.
2. Use *Shape Water* on an existing basin of liquid, respecting the two-lasting-effect limit.
3. Follow the performer’s cue and keep the spell description shorter than their line.

### At the table

“I am the puddle department. The talent is over there.”

### The rule underneath

Simple animation does not create a full illusion or a creature. Performance outcomes belong to the speaker’s actual attempt and the DM’s adjudication.
