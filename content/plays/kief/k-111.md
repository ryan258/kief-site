Finish your contribution with a cue that gives another character the floor.

### When to use it

Your magical explanation has done its job and someone else has the expertise.

### Mechanics

1. Use a brief *Minor Illusion* sound, such as a single ceremonial chime, if the scene welcomes it.
2. Name the teammate whose knowledge matters.
3. Stop performing and let them decide what to say.

### At the table

“And now, someone with an actual qualification.”

### The rule underneath

The chime has no mechanical authority. The useful action is yielding attention; leave it out if it would interrupt a tense scene.
