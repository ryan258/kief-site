Use a royal audience to obtain a usable next step before the jokes multiply.

### When to use it

You have brief access to someone with authority.

### Mechanics

1. Agree on the party’s three priorities before entering.
2. Ask one question about the missing person, one about a concrete location, and one about who can help.
3. Let the teammate with the strongest follow-up take the next turn in the conversation.

### At the table

“A person, a place, and someone who answers the door. We can work with that.”

### The rule underneath

Persuasion +6 can support an appropriate Influence attempt; it does not require a ruler to know, reveal, or grant what you ask.
