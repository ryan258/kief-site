*Hypnotic Pattern* ends fights without a damage roll. A 30-foot cube of
enemies stops participating for up to a minute, and Kief's job becomes
telling the party which ones to leave alone.

### When to use it

- Three or more enemies fit in a 30-foot cube, and the party needs the
  fight to pause: outnumbered, ambushed, or protecting a fragile objective.
- Enemies whose Wisdom looks weak: brutes, beasts, mobs.
- Allies in the cube? Careful Spell (1 SP) protects up to three.
- Not against creatures immune to being Charmed, or ones that cannot see
  the pattern. Switch to [Web](../k-25/) or [Fear](../k-31/).

### Mechanics

1. Action: cast *Hypnotic Pattern* (3rd level). Range 120 feet, 30-foot
   cube, Concentration up to 1 minute.
2. Each creature in the cube that can see the pattern makes a Wisdom save
   against DC 14 (15 under Innate Sorcery) or is **Charmed**. While
   Charmed this way it is **Incapacitated** and has **Speed 0**.
3. The effect ends for a creature if it **takes any damage** or someone
   uses an action to shake it awake. The party's next move is the whole
   play ([play 29](../k-29/)).
4. Careful Spell works here: no damage is involved, so auto-success alone
   protects the allies caught in the cube.
5. It is one of Kief's two 3rd-level slots and his one Concentration.
   [Play 51](../k-51/) is how to keep it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Hypnotic Pattern, the 30-foot cube over the war band.
Wisdom save DC 14 or Charmed, Incapacitated, Speed 0, for up to a minute
while I concentrate. Careful Spell on Tam, one Sorcery Point. The four who
failed are marked. Nobody hit them until we've talked about it."

Verbally: *"Kief sketches a slow spiral, the kind of pattern smoke makes
when nobody's in a hurry. 'Let's all take a moment,' he says. 'Them
especially.'"*

### The rule underneath

Conditions stack meaning. Charmed alone only stops a creature attacking
the charmer; this spell adds Incapacitated (no actions, bonus actions, or
reactions) and Speed 0. The per-creature end conditions are the fine
print: damage wakes one sleeper, and an ally's action wakes another. An
area of effect that anyone can undo needs a clear table announcement,
because the rules will not stop an ally from waking the wrong target.
