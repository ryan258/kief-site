The rehearsed answer to "everything has gone wrong." Kief can't teleport
anyone else out at level 5, so the script gets everyone moving at once.

### When to use it

- The party is losing, the objective is lost or secured, and the exit is
  still open.

### Mechanics

1. **Brief the party once, before trouble**: a code word (the table
   suggestion is "Tuesday") and a rally point named at the start of each
   day.
2. **Kief's turn after the word**: control behind the retreat. [Fear](../k-31/)
   sends the pursuers the other way; [Web](../k-25/) in the doorway blocks
   them. Careful protects allies still in the area.
3. **Kief's own exit**: [Misty Step](../k-23/) as a Bonus Action is legal
   alongside a cantrip, not alongside Fear or Web (one slot per turn). If
   he casts the control spell, he walks, or Disengages next turn.
4. **The drop**: if the exit is a ledge or wall, everyone goes together and
   Kief catches them with [Feather Fall](../k-35/). Keep the Reaction free.
5. **Mr. Big**: dismiss him to the pocket dimension, or send him to the rally
   point ahead of the party.
6. **Downed allies**: carried by the strongest party member. Potions or a
   Healer's Kit only if actually owned.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tuesday. Fear, the cone at the doorway, Careful on Brenna
and Tam. Wisdom DC 14, they drop weapons and run the other way. Everyone
to the mill. I'm walking; Mr. Big is already there."

Verbally: *"'Tuesday,' Kief says, conversationally, which is how the party
knows it's real. 'Everyone,' he adds, 'please enjoy the stairs.'"*

### The rule underneath

Retreats fail on action economy: everyone wants to move, but somebody has
to slow the pursuit. Assigning roles before trouble means nobody wastes a
turn deciding. Pre-agreed code words and rally points are established
fiction: zero actions to invoke, and they turn a rout into a maneuver.
