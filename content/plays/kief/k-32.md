Sixty feet of flight for a teammate: the gap, the wall, the tower window.

### When to use it

- Somebody needs to cross a gap, climb a wall, or reach something high,
  and rope alone won't do it.
- The party's best melee fighter needs to reach a flying or elevated
  enemy.
- **Ask first.** The recipient should agree to the flight plan.

### Mechanics

1. 3rd level (Draconic Sorcery, always prepared), Action, touch, willing
   creature: **Fly Speed 60 feet**, Concentration up to **10 minutes**.
2. When the spell ends, a creature still aloft **falls** unless it can stop
   the fall: 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet. If Kief's Concentration breaks,
   his friend drops.
3. **Agree on a landing before ending it**, and keep Kief's Reaction free
   for [Feather Fall](../k-33/) while someone is high up.
4. Mr. Big can deliver the touch within 100 feet ([play 61](../k-61/)).
5. Kief can't upcast past 3rd level, so it's one flyer per casting.
6. Casting it ends Kief's current Concentration spell. Fly on the fighter
   ends *Web*. Say that tradeoff aloud.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fly on you, Brenna, if you want it: 60 feet of flight for
up to ten minutes while I concentrate. That drops my Web. Land on the
tower roof, not in the air; if I lose it, I'll try to Feather Fall you."

Verbally: *"Kief taps the fighter's boot and she rises like steam leaving a
cup. 'The stairs,' he explains to nobody, 'are more of a suggestion.'"*

### The rule underneath

Flight is Concentration, and the fall is real: 1d6 per 10 feet, up to
20d6. That is why Constitution save bonuses matter for flight casters.
Feather Fall's Reaction trigger (a creature falling within 60 feet) is the
insurance, but only if the Reaction is unspent and the ally is within
range. Touch range means anyone willing can receive it.
