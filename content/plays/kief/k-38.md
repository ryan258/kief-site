The unglamorous rule that decides whether a spell can be cast at all.
Kief's starting gear is **pending**; get this item first.

### When to use it

- Before session one: buy an arcane focus or component pouch.
- Whenever Kief is disarmed, bound, searched, or has his hands full.

### Mechanics

1. Material components **without a listed cost** (Fireball's bat guano,
   Feather Fall's feather, Web's spiderweb) are covered by an **arcane
   focus** or **component pouch**.
2. Components **with a cost** or that **the spell consumes** must be
   physically owned: the 50+ GP diamond for *Chromatic Orb*, the 10+ GP
   incense for *Find Familiar*. No focus replaces them.
3. A hand must be free to hold the focus or reach the pouch. The same hand
   can perform Somatic components.
4. **Subtle Spell** (1 SP) removes Verbal, Somatic, and unpriced Material
   components. It never removes priced or consumed ones.
5. Searched and stripped of the pouch? Spells with no Material component
   still work: *Misty Step*, *Mind Sliver*, *Command*, *Shield*,
   *Counterspell*, *Fire Bolt*, and *Ray of Frost*. *Hypnotic Pattern*,
   *Fear*, *Web*, and *Fireball* need a focus or pouch, or Subtle Spell.
6. Kief's desired kit (kettle, blends, moss jar, herbalism kit) is
   character equipment, not a focus, unless the DM agrees otherwise.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we spend the rest of the allowance: I need a
component pouch, and 10 gold of incense for Mr. Big. The diamond only if
there's room."

Verbally: *"Kief's component pouch is labeled, in careful handwriting, 'NOT
TEA.' Some of it is tea."*

### The rule underneath

Components are a three-part system: Verbal (you can speak), Somatic (you
have a hand to gesture), and Material (you have the item, or a focus that
stands in for unpriced items). Priced and consumed components are the
exception every caster needs to track. When a spell is suddenly
impossible, it is almost always a component problem.
