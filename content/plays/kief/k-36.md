*Fly* solves a specific obstacle. Rope keeps solving it after the spell
ends.

### When to use it

- A chasm, cliff, river, or wall that the whole party needs to cross, when
  Kief can fly only one person per casting.
- The route may be needed again on the way out.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Fly* on the ally who is best at climbing and tying knots, carrying
   the rope ([play 32](../k-32/)). Concentration up to 10 minutes.
2. The flyer crosses, anchors the rope, and lands **before** the spell is
   ended. Kief keeps his Reaction free for *Feather Fall* until then.
3. Everyone else crosses on the rope: a climbing or Athletics check if the
   DM calls for one, with the anchor making it far safer.
4. Mr. Big can carry only a very light line. A Tiny cat can't haul a
   climbing rope; ask the DM what he can manage ([Mr. Big play
   77](../../mr-big/mb-77/)).
5. Rope, pitons, and a grappling hook are **pending purchase**. Confirm them
   in the campaign's starting gear.
6. Kief himself is Small and Strength 8. He crosses last, belayed.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fly on Brenna with the rope: she crosses, ties it off to
the pillar, and lands. Then the rest of us go hand over hand, and it's
still there on the way back. I'll hold my Reaction until she's down."

Verbally: *"'Magic's lovely,' Kief says, watching the rope go taut across
the gap. 'Rope's lovelier. Rope doesn't need me to concentrate.'"*

### The rule underneath

A spell with a duration is a temporary solution. Converting it into a
permanent one (a tied rope, an opened door, a moved obstacle) is the core
of efficient resource play. Mundane gear doesn't compete for Concentration
or slots, which is why a 3rd-level slot plus a 1 GP rope beats two 3rd-level
slots.
