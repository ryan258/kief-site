Repair a small break, then let the climber assess the whole route.

### When to use it

A ladder or frame has one visible defect and enough time for inspection.

### Mechanics

1. Ask the DM whether the break is within *Mending* limits and its material is present.
2. Complete the minute-long repair if appropriate.
3. Let the climber check the remaining structure and choose whether to trust it.

### At the table

“That rung has recovered. The rest have not submitted references.”

### The rule underneath

One repaired break is not a load rating or a guarantee against rot. The play improves a specific defect instead of declaring the obstacle solved.
