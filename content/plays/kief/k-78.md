Keep a temporary ice token as a deliberately short-lived agreement prop.

### When to use it

Two willing parties want a memorable marker for a brief appointment.

### Mechanics

1. Use ordinary water with no creature in it.
2. Freeze a small amount with *Shape Water* in an available mundane mold; allow the normal hour-long thaw.
3. State the agreed meeting time separately and write it down if precision matters.

### At the table

“If the appointment outlives the ice, we have both become impolite.”

### The rule underneath

The token is neither a magical contract nor an exact clock. Shape and freezing may require separate castings, and the result depends on the mold.
