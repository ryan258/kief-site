Make ordinary water into temporary ice for a mundane cooling task.

### When to use it

A provisioner or artisan needs a little ice and has a suitable container.

### Mechanics

1. Use existing ordinary water with no creatures in it, within 30 feet and a 5-foot cube.
2. Cast *Shape Water* to freeze it for 1 hour; keep the ice separate from any substance you do not want contaminated.
3. Let the owner decide how to pack and use it, accounting for the eventual thaw.

### At the table

“An hour of winter, collection only.”

### The rule underneath

Freezing water is a listed effect. Refrigeration results depend on the amount, insulation, conditions, and DM adjudication; this does not cure spoiled food or provide healing. Freezing unusual candy liquids still needs its own ruling.
