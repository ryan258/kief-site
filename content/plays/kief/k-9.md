Forty feet of usable light for the teammates who can't see in the dark,
with no Concentration.

### When to use it

- Any dungeon, cellar, or night road where someone in the party lacks
  Darkvision.
- Before combat starts. Casting it in initiative costs Kief's Action.
- Not when the party is sneaking. Light is a beacon; cover the object.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, touch: one Large or smaller object not worn or carried
   by someone else sheds **Bright Light in a 20-foot radius** and **Dim
   Light for another 20 feet** for 1 hour.
2. **No Concentration.** It never competes with *Web*, *Hypnotic Pattern*,
   *Fly*, or *Invisibility*.
3. Cover the object with something opaque to block the light instantly.
   A lidded lantern, a pouch, or a hat is a light switch.
4. Casting it again ends the previous light. One Light at a time.
5. It is a touch spell, so Mr. Big can deliver it within 100 feet
   ([play 61](../k-61/)): light up the far side of a chasm.
6. The material component is a firefly or phosphorescent moss. Kief carries
   a jar of the latter and considers this a sign.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Light on the end of my walking stick: 20 feet bright, 20
more dim, for an hour. If we need dark, it goes in the satchel."

Verbally: *"Kief taps the stick with a finger that smells faintly of moss,
and the end of it wakes up like a lantern that has been waiting for its
cue."*

### The rule underneath

Lighting is a rules system: bright light is normal vision, dim light is
Lightly Obscured (Disadvantage on sight-based Perception), and darkness is
Heavily Obscured, which is effectively Blinded for anyone without
Darkvision. Kief's Darkvision 60 turns darkness into dim light for him
only. A no-Concentration light spell is a party feature disguised as a
cantrip, and it is why this build dropped *Dancing Lights*.
