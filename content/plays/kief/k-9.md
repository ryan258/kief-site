Manipulate visible liquid within 30 feet. The DM approved liquids; use the normal spell limits and clarify unusual freezing outcomes.

### When to use it

- Existing water or liquid candy offers a playful prop, a simple signal, or a small flow adjustment.
- A little animated liquid shape helps tell a story or include a teammate in a scene.

### Mechanics

1. *Shape Water*: Action, 30 feet, Somatic component, no concentration. The visible liquid must fit within a 5-foot cube. Choose one effect per casting.
2. Move or change its flow up to 5 feet; this movement cannot deal damage.
3. Form simple shapes and animate them for 1 hour, or uniformly change color or opacity for 1 hour.
4. The published freezing option requires water with no creatures in it and ends after 1 hour. The DM approved liquid scope, but has not specified freezing or hardening for every unusual candy liquid.
5. At most two non-instantaneous effects can remain active. Dismiss an effect as an Action.
6. This uses existing liquid. It does not create a liquid creature, permanent candy, automatic restraint, or a damage effect.

### At the table

Mechanically: “I shape a little of the syrup into a simple cow and animate it bowing. It fits within the spell’s five-foot cube.”

Verbally: *“Management would like to sweeten the offer.”*

### The rule underneath

Ryan reports that the DM approved Shape Water affecting liquids. Apply the spell’s listed limits and ask about particular outcomes that go beyond them. Shape Water replaces Light; arrange a party light source. See [the campaign ruling](/rulings/#shape-water-campaign-liquid-ruling).
