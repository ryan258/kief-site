Put a harmless object where it can invite a conversation instead of an intrusion.

### When to use it

An NPC will not approach but might accept an offered item.

### Mechanics

1. Ask where they would like the cup, letter, or token placed.
2. Use *Mage Hand* to move the item within its range and weight limit.
3. Withdraw the hand and let them decide whether to take it.

### At the table

“The cup will approach. The merchant will maintain a respectful distance.”

### The rule underneath

The hand is visible and casting is perceptible. Delivery is not a Sleight of Hand trick, compulsory acceptance, or proof that the contents are harmless.
