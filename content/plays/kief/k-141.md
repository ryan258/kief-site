Give an invisible scout a check-in plan that does not require another spell.

### When to use it

The party is sending a willing ally on a bounded reconnaissance task.

### Mechanics

1. Agree on a location, time, and abort signal before casting *Invisibility*.
2. Ask for one specific piece of information.
3. Stay ready to receive them and avoid replacing your concentration without warning.

### At the table

“One answer, then back. Curiosity may submit a second request later.”

### The rule underneath

The spell does not provide remote communication or locate the scout. A missed check-in calls for a new decision, not an assumed magical recall.
