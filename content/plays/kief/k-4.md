The enemy caster's big turn fails. With Subtle Spell, nobody even saw Kief
do it.

### When to use it

- Enemy magic is the central threat: their area spell, their mass charm,
  the heal that undoes the party's round.
- Hold the Reaction deliberately. It competes with *Shield* and *Feather
  Fall*; against a real caster, denial usually beats +5 AC.
- Add Subtle Spell (1 SP) when the enemy may counter back. A casting with
  no components gives it nothing to see.

### Mechanics

1. Reaction when Kief **sees** a creature within **60 feet** casting a
   spell with Verbal, Somatic, or Material components.
2. Cast *Counterspell* (3rd level). The caster makes a **Constitution save
   against DC 14** (15 under Innate Sorcery). On a failure the spell
   dissipates and the action used to cast it is wasted; any slot it used
   is **not** expended.
3. Kief's slot is spent whatever happens. With only **two** 3rd-level
   slots, [play 30](../k-30/) is how to keep one available.
4. Out of 3rd-level slots? 5 SP builds one as a Bonus Action on Kief's
   own turn ([play 46](../k-46/)), which empties the Metamagic reserve.
5. **Subtle Counterspell** (1 SP): no Verbal or Somatic components means an
   enemy caster has no casting to see and counter.
6. There is no automatic success against low-level spells in 2024. Every
   counter is a save.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Reaction: Counterspell, 3rd level, and I'm making it Subtle
for a Sorcery Point, so there's nothing to see. Constitution save DC 14 or
her spell fails."

Verbally: *"The necromancer's incantation reaches its crescendo and then
simply goes bitter. Across the room, Kief sips from his cup. 'No, mate.
That's the wrong blend.'"*

Tell the DM at the start of caster fights: "I'm holding my Reaction for
enemy spellcasting."

### The rule underneath

*Counterspell*'s trigger teaches Reaction anatomy: "when you see a
creature within 60 feet casting a spell." See means imperceptible casting
cannot be countered. The 2024 version resolves as a Constitution save by
the target caster, and the target keeps its slot on a failure. One
Reaction per round means *Shield*, *Feather Fall*, and *Counterspell*
compete. Budgeting that Reaction is a real caster skill.
