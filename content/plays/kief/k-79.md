Move a small puddle away from the one dry step everyone needs.

### When to use it

A doorstep is messy but nobody needs a combat solution.

### Mechanics

1. Check that the puddle fits the spell’s volume and that the receiving spot is safe.
2. Use *Shape Water* to move it at most 5 feet.
3. Invite the party across and check whether more liquid immediately flows back.

### At the table

“A red carpet was unavailable. I have arranged a less wet one.”

### The rule underneath

A single movement does not create a permanent dam or negate an ongoing current. The DM decides whether footing actually improves.
