Freeze the enemy's position and hand the party Restrained targets, for a
2nd-level slot.

### When to use it

- Enemies approaching through a doorway or corridor, or melee threats to
  be kept off the backline.
- Hypnotic Pattern isn't an option: charm-immune enemies, or the 3rd-level
  slots are spoken for.
- Allies can stay outside the cube. Careful Spell helps with the first save
  only ([play 49](../k-49/)).

### Mechanics

1. 2nd level, Action, range 60 feet, **20-foot cube**, Concentration up to
   **1 hour**. The webs need anchors (walls, trees) or a surface to layer
   across, or the web collapses at the start of Kief's next turn.
2. The area is **difficult terrain** and **Lightly Obscured**.
3. The first time a creature enters the webs on a turn, or starts its turn
   there, it makes a **Dexterity save against DC 14** (15 under Innate
   Sorcery) or is **Restrained** while in the webs.
4. Restrained: Speed 0, attacks against it have Advantage, its attacks and
   Dexterity saves have Disadvantage. Breaking free costs its action: a
   Strength (Athletics) check against Kief's DC.
5. **Flammable**: any 5-foot cube exposed to fire burns away in a round,
   dealing 2d4 fire to a creature that starts its turn in the fire. That
   ends the control in that cube. Burning it on purpose is
   [play 66](../k-66/).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Web across the corridor, anchored wall to wall. Anyone in it
or entering it: Dexterity save DC 14 or Restrained. Tam, stay out; you'd
be rolling too. I'm concentrating, and nobody light it until I say."

Verbally: *"Grey silk fills the hallway like the world's least negotiable
curtain. 'The room,' Kief tells the raiders, 'would like you to slow
down.'"*

### The rule underneath

*Web* is a conditions lesson: Restrained (Speed 0, Advantage for
attackers, Disadvantage on the victim's attacks and Dexterity saves),
applied by a save that repeats on entry and at turn start. Careful Spell
protects from one save when the spell is cast, not from the later saves or
the difficult terrain. Environmental text is mechanical text: anchors,
flammability, and burn timing all matter.
