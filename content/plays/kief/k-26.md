Disguise, gills, or claws. One spell, re-chosen on the fly.

### When to use it

- **Change Appearance**: walk into the counting-house as somebody else.
- **Aquatic Adaptation**: the moat, the flooded cellar, the harbor.
- **Natural Weapons**: an emergency only. Kief in melee is still a mistake.
- Out of combat, when nothing else needs Kief's Concentration.

### Mechanics

1. 2nd level (Draconic Sorcery, always prepared), Action, self,
   Concentration up to 1 hour. Choose one option; switch options with a
   **Magic action** for the duration.
2. Change Appearance: alter how Kief looks, within the spell's limits
   (read them: size and basic shape constrain what he can become). It does
   not change his voice or his skills, and he has no Deception proficiency.
3. Aquatic Adaptation: gills, and a Swim Speed equal to his Speed (30).
4. Natural Weapons: magical claws, fangs, or horns. Unarmed Strikes deal
   1d6 plus Kief's spellcasting modifier and use it to attack (+6).
5. It is Concentration. A disguised Kief cannot also hold *Invisibility* or
   *Web*.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Alter Self, Change Appearance: I'm now a grey-bearded
Consortium clerk of about my own height, concentrating, up to an hour. I'll
let Brenna do the talking; I'll do the nodding."

Verbally: *"Kief's face rearranges itself with the ease of leaves settling
in hot water. The man who walks into the counting-house disapproves of
Kief Firelight on principle."*

### The rule underneath

Self-buff spells with modes often let you swap the active option as a
Magic action, so one casting can carry several configurations. Natural
Weapons shows a 2024 pattern: when a spell says so, a caster attacks with
the spellcasting modifier, not Strength. A disguise is fiction the DM
adjudicates; it doesn't grant skill. Pair it with the party member whose
Deception is real.
