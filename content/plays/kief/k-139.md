Send the teammate with the relevant skill to inspect the inaccessible clue.

### When to use it

The best investigator cannot reach an upper shelf, mural, or balcony.

### Mechanics

1. Ask the specialist whether flight helps and where they can safely work.
2. Use *Fly* if the clue justifies one of your limited 3rd-level slots.
3. Let them perform the investigation while you maintain concentration from safety.

### At the table

“I supply altitude. You supply conclusions.”

### The rule underneath

Flight adds movement, not investigation proficiency. Touch range for the initial casting and a later safe descent still matter.
