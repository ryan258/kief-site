Use a visible escape destination instead of trusting a remembered floor plan.

### When to use it

Kief needs to escape a room with a reachable window or open sightline.

### Mechanics

1. Choose an unoccupied spot you can currently see within 30 feet.
2. Use *Misty Step* as a Bonus Action.
3. Spend your Action on a cantrip or another action that does not cast a second spell with a slot.

### At the table

“I admire the room. I would like to admire it from outside.”

### The rule underneath

Knowing a room exists behind a wall does not satisfy sight. Misty Step moves Kief, not a passenger, and does not unlock an escape for the rest of the party.
