Place control to protect an extraction route rather than maximize the number of targets.

### When to use it

An ally is carrying or helping someone out of danger.

### Mechanics

1. Choose an anchored or surface-supported *Web* area that does not cover the rescuers’ route.
2. Announce the clear lane before casting.
3. Maintain concentration while the party withdraws through the intended space.

### At the table

“That passage is for leaving. This one is for reconsidering your choices.”

### The rule underneath

Web creates difficult terrain and saves on entry or turn start, not an immediate casting save. Careful’s delayed-save interaction is unresolved; keep allies out.
