Speed 40, Climb 40, Stealth +4, Darkvision 60, and a shape nobody's threat
assessment covers. The full doctrine lives in
[Mr. Big's plays](../../mr-big/mb-1/).

### When to use it

- Point position in dungeons, camps, and streets, before the party's first
  footstep into the unknown.

### Mechanics

1. The 2024 **Cat** stat block, as a Fey: AC 12, HP 2, **Speed 40, Climb
   40**, Perception +3 (Passive 13), Stealth +4, Darkvision 60, Jumper.
   Intelligence 3.
2. **Telepathy within 100 feet**: silent reports, no action. Beyond 100
   feet, he follows simple prior instructions.
3. **Shared senses**: Kief's Bonus Action lets him see and hear through Mr.
   Big until the start of Kief's next turn.
4. He rolls his own initiative, **cannot attack**, and can take other
   actions: Hide, Search, Help, Dash, Dodge.
5. He is not an automatic trap detector. He reports what a cat notices.
6. The cow appearance and first-session summoning still need the DM's
   confirmation.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big takes the wall-top route ahead of us, Climb 40,
Stealth plus four, and stops at each corner. He reports by telepathy
inside a hundred feet: people, lights, dogs, and doors."

Verbally: *"A very small cow picks along the battlements in perfect
silence, pausing to study each sentry with the patience of a portrait."*

### The rule underneath

Every creature you control runs on its own stat block. Speed and Climb are
where he can go, his Perception and Stealth are his checks, 100 feet is
the telepathy limit, and "can't attack" is a hard boundary. The fastest way
to misplay a familiar is to assume it inherits your numbers.
