Arcana +2 and History +2: modest numbers with a workable method.

### When to use it

- Identifying magic, reading ruins, placing heraldry and old wars.
- Questions about herbs, teas, trade routes, and regional goods, where
  Kief's life as a merchant is the expertise.

### Mechanics

1. Intelligence (Arcana) **+2**, Intelligence (History) **+2**: proficient
   from Sage training, held back by Intelligence 9.
2. State the method and the specifics: "did my merchant circuit ever trade
   near this sigil" beats "do I know it."
3. The **Leaf & Ledger**'s scattered herb merchants are a loose family.
   Downtime letters to them can answer what a +2 can't, if the DM agrees.
4. For tea, herbs, and trade, ask whether the background makes it common
   knowledge. The DM may skip the roll.
5. Hard scholarship goes to the party's scholar. Kief's role is knowing
   which question to carry to whom.

### At the table

Mechanically: "History, plus two, but specifically: decades of tea routes
through these hills. Do I know whose banner that was from market-day talk,
if not from books?"

Verbally: *"'I'm not a learned man,' Kief says, turning the old coin over,
'but I've been paid in currencies that stopped existing. This one bought
me a mule once. That was a bad week.'"*

### The rule underneath

Knowledge checks use Intelligence, and proficiency is what turns a weak
score into a usable one. The 2024 Study action is the formal container.
Two legal ways to know things without good dice: specific framing, which
may let the DM skip the roll for documented expertise, and networks, which
answer questions checks can't. The sheet is not the ceiling on what your
character knows.
