Use two differently colored liquid samples to make competing proposals easy to follow.

### When to use it

A complicated bargain is losing the table.

### Mechanics

1. Put two actual samples in separate containers.
2. Cast *Shape Water* twice, uniformly recoloring each sample; these occupy both lasting effects.
3. Point to one concrete proposal per sample and ask which term the other party would change.

### At the table

“Blue is your offer. Gold is ours. Notice that both still fit on the table.”

### The rule underneath

Color changes do not alter taste, value, or ingredients. The negotiation still depends on the terms and any check the DM requests.
