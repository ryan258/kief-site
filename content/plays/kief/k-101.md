Put a brief sound at a different door to create a possible opening for an ally.

### When to use it

A watcher’s attention matters more than their defeat.

### Mechanics

1. Agree where the ally will move if the watcher reacts.
2. Cast *Minor Illusion* to make a suitable sound within 30 feet.
3. Let the DM determine the reaction; the ally still moves and hides normally.

### At the table

“A knock from the door we are very much not using.”

### The rule underneath

A sound is not an order to investigate. Recasting ends the previous illusion, and a skeptical listener can scrutinize what they hear.
