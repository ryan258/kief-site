Remove Counterspell’s components when the interruption itself must avoid a component-based response.

### When to use it

You see an eligible enemy cast within 60 feet and have both resources available.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Counterspell* as your Reaction and spend 1 SP for Subtle Spell.
2. The enemy still makes its Constitution save; no automatic interruption is promised.
3. Tell the party whether the dangerous cast actually stopped.

### At the table

“Kief says nothing. For once, it is the useful part.”

### The rule underneath

Subtle removes components, not all consequences or evidence. On a failed save, a slotted enemy spell preserves its slot even though its casting action is wasted.
