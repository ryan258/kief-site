+5 AC as a Reaction. It costs a real slot every time, so spend it well.

### When to use it

- A noncritical attack total of 15–19 hits AC 15; Shield raises AC to 20.
- *Magic Missile* is incoming. *Shield* negates it entirely.
- Kief is concentrating on a spell that is helping the whole party. A
  1st-level slot is cheap next to a lost *Hypnotic Pattern*.
- **Not** against a critical hit or an attack total of 20 or more, when the damage is trivial, or
  when an ally may fall this round and Feather Fall is the better use of
  the Reaction.

### Mechanics

1. Reaction when hit by an attack roll or targeted by *Magic Missile*: +5
   AC until the start of Kief's next turn, including against the
   triggering attack, and no damage from *Magic Missile*.
2. Every casting costs a 1st-level slot (or 2 SP converted into one on
   Kief's own turn). Kief has four 1st-level slots, and *Feather Fall*,
   *Command*, *Chromatic Orb*, and *Detect Magic* want them too.
3. If the DM shares the attack total, use it: totals 15–19 become misses. A critical hit still hits.
4. It lasts until the start of Kief’s next turn, covering subsequent attacks
   before then.
5. Using the Reaction here gives up *Counterspell* and *Feather Fall*
   until Kief's next turn.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Does the 18 hit me? By how much? …Three? Reaction: Shield,
1st-level slot. AC 20, both claws miss, and the Web holds."

Verbally: *"The air in front of Kief hardens like the skin on cooling tea.
The claws skate off it twice, and he looks mildly apologetic about the
whole thing."*

### The rule underneath

*Shield* reacts to a declared hit; whether the DM reveals the total is a
table convention. It cannot defeat critical hits or totals of 20 or more
at Kief’s current AC. It also blocks Magic Missile. Your Reaction
refreshes at the start of your next turn, not at the round boundary.
