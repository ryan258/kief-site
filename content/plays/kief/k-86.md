Turn a harmless demonstration into a graceful exit when a joke fails.

### When to use it

Your liquid-candy flourish receives silence instead of laughter.

### Mechanics

1. Stop escalating the joke.
2. Dismiss the lasting *Shape Water* effect with an Action and return the scene to ordinary conversation.
3. Offer a useful question or let another player speak.

### At the table

“Management withdraws the presentation.”

### The rule underneath

Ending an effect does not undo mundane consequences or social reactions. This play spends attention carefully rather than adding another spell to recover a laugh.
