Create a calm place to regroup without pretending comfort is a rest spell.

### When to use it

The party has reached actual shelter after a difficult scene.

### Mechanics

1. Let the lookout establish whether the location is safe.
2. Use *Prestidigitation* for a warm drink or cleaned cloth from actual supplies.
3. Ask the party whether they are taking a real rest or only a brief pause, and track resources accordingly.

### At the table

“This is tea. The Short Rest is a separate service.”

### The rule underneath

A cantrip does not grant rest benefits, replenish supplies, or remove Exhaustion. Kief’s Sorcerous Restoration requires the actual Short Rest and its normal limits.
