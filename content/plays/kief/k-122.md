Repair a costume’s qualifying tear so the disguise specialist can do their job.

### When to use it

A teammate’s outfit has a small, repairable defect.

### Mechanics

1. Ask what detail matters to their approach.
2. Use *Mending* on the tear, or *Prestidigitation* for ordinary dirt; use the right spell for the damage.
3. Hand the presentation back to the teammate.

### At the table

“Your hem is now more convincing than my accent.”

### The rule underneath

Neither spell grants disguise proficiency or transforms an outfit into a uniform. A complete disguise depends on the remaining clothes, behavior, and checks.

Use the [current spellbook](/spellbook/) and [table rulings](/rulings/) for the spell details and campaign overrides.
