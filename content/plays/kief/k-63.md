Two hit points, an hour of ritual, and consumed incense. The math behind
every cow decision.

### When to use it

- Every time a play puts Mr. Big within reach of anything armed.

### Mechanics

1. The body: AC 12, **2 HP**. Almost any hit or area effect ends the form.
   He vanishes, leaving no body; the Fey spirit is fine.
2. Careful Spell can protect him from Kief's own area spells, at the cost
   of one of three protected slots.
3. The replacement cost: **1 hour** (70 minutes as a ritual) and **10+ GP of
   consumed incense**. Current incense stock is unknown until starting gear
   is set.
4. **Pocket dismissal**: as a Magic action, Kief dismisses him to a pocket
   dimension; another Magic action brings him back within 30 feet. No
   ritual, no incense. Pocket him before the dragon's chamber.
5. Priority of use: scouting (low risk) > touch delivery (managed risk) >
   Help (high risk, rationed) > anything else (no).
6. Mr. Big eats first. Always. Not a mechanic.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We're breaching the drake's chamber. Magic action: Mr. Big
goes to the pocket dimension until it's over. He has two hit points and
I'm not sure we have incense."

Verbally: *"Kief kneels, and the little cow steps into a fold of air as
calmly as a stall he owns. 'Nothing in there for you,' Kief agrees. 'I'll
describe it badly later.'"*

### The rule underneath

Asset risk is replacement cost. A familiar at 0 HP isn't truly lost, but
recovering it costs time and gold, and pocket dismissal reduces exposure
to zero at the cost of coverage. Knowing each resource's replacement cost
and cheapest protection turns sentiment into decisions, and the table can
still care about the cow.
