Mr. Big’s death is permanent loss and harms Kief. Plan for his safety and respect his refusal.

### When to use it

Whenever a plan exposes Mr. Big to danger.

### Mechanics

1. The proposed Cat block has 2 HP; its use still needs confirmation. Do not treat fragility as cheap replacement cost.
2. Agree on cover, an exit, and an abort signal before scouting or touch delivery.
3. He can help intelligently but will not willingly sacrifice himself on command.
4. Put him away before known danger using the dismissal procedure the DM confirms.
5. If hurt, ask the DM about 0 HP and rescue options; those mechanics are not yet specified.

### At the table

“There’s no safe way back from that doorway. Mr. Big stays with us.”
