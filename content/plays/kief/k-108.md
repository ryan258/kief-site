Separate taste from identity before drawing conclusions about suspicious food.

### When to use it

A clue seems to depend on an unfamiliar flavor.

### Mechanics

1. Ask the group to preserve an untouched sample.
2. Explain that *Prestidigitation* can change flavor, so taste alone may not establish origin.
3. Let the investigator or relevant expert choose a safer method of inquiry.

### At the table

“A convincing cinnamon note is not an alibi.”

### The rule underneath

Do not taste suspected poison to test this theory. Flavor magic neither detects nor removes poison, and this play claims no diagnostic ability.
