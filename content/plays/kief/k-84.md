Give a performer a visible cue made from an existing cup of liquid.

### When to use it

A teammate needs a cue during a speech or staged distraction.

### Mechanics

1. Agree that one simple wave means begin.
2. Use *Shape Water* to animate the liquid when the cue is due, keeping it in view and range.
3. Let the performer lead; keep your contribution to one signal.

### At the table

“When the tea bows, you are on.”

### The rule underneath

The animation is a visible signal, not telepathy or a programmed surveillance trigger. It can attract other observers too.
