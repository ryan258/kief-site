Put a single useful word where the party can see it.

### When to use it

A discussion keeps branching away from the immediate objective.

### Mechanics

1. Choose a nearby patch of dirt or sand.
2. Use *Elementalism* to write one word, such as EXIT or WITNESS.
3. Invite the group to finish that task before returning to the other questions.

### At the table

“I have shortened the minutes to one word.”

### The rule underneath

The word is in Kief’s handwriting, not a forged signature or a universal language. It supplies no magical compulsion and may be disturbed normally.
