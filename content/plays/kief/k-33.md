The rescue Reaction. Up to five falling creatures slow down and land
unhurt.

### When to use it

- A trapdoor opens, a bridge snaps, a flyer loses the spell, an ally is
  shoved off the parapet.
- Kief himself falls.
- Before the fall happens: keep the Reaction free whenever allies are
  somewhere high.

### Mechanics

1. 1st level, **Reaction**, taken when Kief or a creature he can see within
   **60 feet** falls. Range 60 feet.
2. Choose **up to five falling creatures** in range. Their rate of descent
   slows to **60 feet per round** for up to 1 minute.
3. A creature that lands before the spell ends takes **no falling damage**,
   and the spell ends for it.
4. No Concentration. It doesn't disturb *Web* or *Fly*.
5. Components: Verbal, plus a small feather or piece of down. Gagged or
   empty-handed? Subtle Spell (1 SP) removes both, since neither is priced
   or consumed.
6. It uses the Reaction that *Shield* and *Counterspell* also want. A
   Reaction spent on Shield this round means nobody gets caught.
7. Feather Fall prevents falling damage only. It doesn't stop a creature
   landing in the river, the lava, or the middle of the enemy.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Reaction: Feather Fall on Brenna, Tam, and the mule. You
fall 60 feet a round and land with no damage. The ground can wait."

Verbally: *"The floor drops away. Kief says one word, and three people and
a very surprised mule drift down the shaft like leaves settling into a
cup. 'Nobody rush the landing.'"*

### The rule underneath

Reactions with a trigger ("when a creature falls") are insurance you have
to leave room for: the Reaction must be unused, and the creature must be
visible and in range. Falling damage is 1d6 per 10 feet fallen, up to
20d6, so a 60-foot fall is 6d6. A 1st-level slot that saves several
people 21 average damage each is one of the best trades on the sheet.
