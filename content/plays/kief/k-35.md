Feather Fall isn't only an accident policy. It's also a route down.

### When to use it

- The fast way down is a drop: a cliff, a shaft, a castle wall, a rooftop
  escape.
- The party needs to leave faster than a rope descent allows.
- The landing zone is known and safe.

### Mechanics

1. Agree the plan: everyone steps off together, within 60 feet of Kief and
   where he can see them.
2. As they fall, Kief takes his **Reaction** and casts *Feather Fall* on up
   to **five** falling creatures, himself included.
3. Descent is **60 feet per round** for up to 1 minute. Anything deeper than
   600 feet needs a second plan.
4. The spell has Verbal and unpriced Material components. Gagging or
   silence prevents normal casting; **Subtle Spell (1 SP)** removes both.
   Confirm the Reaction, slot, and components or SP before anyone jumps.
5. Kief's Reaction must be unused. Don't spend it on *Shield* the round
   before the jump.
6. A slow fall in the open is exposed to archers for several rounds. Drop
   when nobody is shooting, or into cover.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Rope takes five minutes. Instead: all four of us step off
the wall together on my count, and I Feather Fall everyone. Sixty feet a
round, the wall's forty tall, we land next round with no damage."

Verbally: *"'After you,' Kief says to the drop, and steps into it with his
hands clasped behind his back like a man boarding a slow ferry."*

### The rule underneath

A Reaction spell can be triggered deliberately: jumping is falling, so the
trigger happens when you choose. Without the spell, the whole drop is
1d6 per 10 feet on landing. Planning
around a Reaction means protecting the Reaction beforehand, the same
discipline as holding *Counterspell*.
