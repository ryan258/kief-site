Show a craftsperson what the absent part looked like before buying the wrong replacement.

### When to use it

A broken object needs a component you do not possess.

### Mechanics

1. Use *Minor Illusion* for a simple stationary image of the remembered part.
2. Ask the craftsperson to assess its dimensions and function.
3. Buy or obtain the real replacement through ordinary means.

### At the table

“I can supply the appearance. We are shopping for the substance.”

### The rule underneath

An image cannot serve as a physical component. Mending does not conjure missing material, so showing a design and repairing an item remain separate tasks.
