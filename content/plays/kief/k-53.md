Kief's best skill, wrapped in a persona nobody guards against.

### When to use it

- Negotiations, gates, merchants, nobles: Kief talks first unless the scene
  demands intimidation, which is not his register.
- Once the party knows who has the best social numbers, adjust. Another
  character may be better.

### Mechanics

1. Charisma (Persuasion) **+6**. The **Influence** action structures it:
   state what Kief wants and how he's asking; the DM sets the DC from the
   creature's attitude.
2. Sequence: [tea service](../k-13/), then a small real favor
   ([Mending](../k-15/), warmth, listening), then the ask.
3. Insight +3 first when honesty matters ([play 55](../k-55/)).
4. **No Deception proficiency** (+3 from Charisma alone). Kief's game is
   technically true things, warmly delivered. Route lies to someone
   trained, or restructure the truth until it doesn't need lying about.
5. Intimidation is also untrained (+3).

### At the table

Mechanically: "I want the toll waived. Method: Kief has shared his rain
cover, warmed the guard's soup, and explains, truthfully, that we're headed
to deal with the thing eating the caravans. Persuasion, plus six."

Verbally: *"Ten minutes with Kief and the gate sergeant has somehow agreed
to something, feels good about it, and has a full cup."*

### The rule underneath

The Influence action: say what you want; the DM sets the DC from attitude
(Friendly, Indifferent, Hostile) and picks the skill from your described
approach. Persuasion is honest asks, Deception is lies, Intimidation is
threats. Your phrasing at the table selects your modifier, so describe the
approach that uses the number you actually have.
