Buy time for a repair by assigning the actual protection to teammates.

### When to use it

A crucial small break can be repaired, but trouble is nearby.

### Mechanics

1. State that *Mending* requires a full minute, not one combat Action.
2. Ask the party whether holding the position is worth that time.
3. Begin only after they establish the plan; abandon the attempt if safety takes priority.

### At the table

“I need a minute. A real one, regrettably.”

### The rule underneath

Long casting requires focus and concentration under the casting rules. Do not maintain another concentration spell while assuming this minute is free.
