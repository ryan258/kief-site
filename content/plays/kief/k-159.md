Spend a quiet turn protecting the spell that is already winning the scene.

### When to use it

Your existing concentration effect is doing more than another attack would.

### Mechanics

1. Move to a useful safer position without losing the plan’s necessary sightlines.
2. Take Dodge when its benefits apply.
3. Tell the party what you are continuing to maintain so the turn feels purposeful.

### At the table

“The web is doing beautifully. I shall avoid interrupting its work.”

### The rule underneath

Dodge is not immunity and does not improve every saving throw. It can be the best use of an Action without adding a second spell or another rules discussion.
