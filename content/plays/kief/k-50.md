One Sorcery Point removes the visible casting: no words, no gestures, no
tell, nothing to counter.

### When to use it

- **Polite company**: *Command*, *Misty Step*, or *Detect Magic* in courts,
  taverns, and negotiations where visible casting is a crime or a
  declaration of war.
- **Counterspell wars**: a casting nobody can see can't be countered
  ([play 4](../k-4/)).
- **Bound and gagged**: Subtle needs no Verbal or Somatic components.

### Mechanics

1. **1 SP**: cast without Verbal, Somatic, or Material components, **except**
   Material components that are consumed or have a cost. *Chromatic Orb*'s
   diamond and *Find Familiar*'s incense still apply.
2. The **effect** is still visible. A Subtle *Fireball* is an anonymous
   explosion, not an invisible one.
3. A creature that passes its save against a Subtle *Command* may still feel
   something. The DM decides what it notices.
4. Budget: 1 SP from the same 5-point battery. One Subtle casting is
   usually the whole scene.
5. Subtle and Careful can't both go on one spell at level 5
   ([play 48](../k-48/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Hands folded on the table, mid-sentence. Subtle Spell, one
point, Command: 'Drop.' No components, nothing to see. Wisdom save DC 14
and the magistrate's seal hits the desk."

Verbally: *"Kief never stops stirring his tea. Across the table, the
magistrate's fingers open by themselves, and the only witness is a small
cow who will testify to nothing."*

### The rule underneath

Components are how casting is perceived: Verbal and Somatic components
are what observers, and *Counterspell*'s trigger, can see or hear. Removing
them hides the act of casting, not its consequence. In social play, what
casting looks like at your table is worth asking the DM about explicitly.
