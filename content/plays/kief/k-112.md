Retrieve a visible loose key without sending someone through the bars.

### When to use it

A key or small tool lies within reach of the spectral hand.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Mage Hand* within 30 feet and guide it to the visible object.
2. Keep the object under 10 pounds and the hand within 30 feet of Kief.
3. Bring it to the person who can use it; later control uses a Magic action.

### At the table

“The hand has volunteered for the narrow part.”

### The rule underneath

The hand cannot pass through solid barriers, pick a lock as a special feature, or tell whether the key fits. Its path and manipulation still need to be feasible.
