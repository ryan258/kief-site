Recover your own dropped mundane component pouch while keeping your feet in cover.

### When to use it

Your pouch is visible within 30 feet and light enough for the hand.

### Mechanics

1. Check that you can cast *Mage Hand* with its Verbal and Somatic components.
2. Retrieve the pouch without assuming this also casts another Action spell.
3. Use the following turn for the spell you recovered it to cast.

### At the table

“The pouch returns to its natural habitat: excessive preparedness.”

### The rule underneath

The feat cantrip needs no material component, but it does require its other components. A secured, held, or inaccessible object is not automatically retrievable.
