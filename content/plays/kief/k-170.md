Rehearse a retreat once so nobody must decode it during trouble.

### When to use it

The party is about to enter a place with several exits.

### Mechanics

1. Use a stationary *Minor Illusion* object image as a crude model of the known layout.
2. Have each teammate indicate their intended exit and who they help.
3. Let the image expire after a minute; retain a simple spoken cue.

### At the table

“If things become educational, we leave through this door.”

### The rule underneath

The model reflects what you know, not hidden architecture. Rehearsal grants no extra movement or Actions, but makes the real decisions easier to communicate.
