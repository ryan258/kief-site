Ask before removing the grime that may matter to a witness or investigator.

### When to use it

An inscription or possession is dirty and someone else is examining it.

### Mechanics

1. Have the investigator record anything relevant first.
2. With permission, use *Prestidigitation* to clean an eligible object or area within its 1-cubic-foot limit.
3. Hand the newly visible details back to the investigator.

### At the table

“I can remove the dirt. I would prefer not to remove the clue.”

### The rule underneath

Cleaning changes the scene and can destroy useful traces. It does not restore faded writing or disclose invisible information.
