Give the party a concrete price and fallback before spending a scarce spell.

### When to use it

Several clever solutions compete for Kief’s remaining resources.

### Mechanics

1. State the cost: slot, SP, concentration, casting time, and which teammate acts.
2. Offer a simpler alternative using an available cantrip or mundane action.
3. Let the group choose with the next obstacle in mind.

### At the table

“The flying solution costs our last third-level slot. The rope solution costs patience.”

### The rule underneath

Resources have specific limits: 5 SP and slots 4/3/2 at full level-5 capacity. A clever description does not waive components, concentration, or action costs.
