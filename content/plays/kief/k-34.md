A ritual that tells the party what is enchanted before anyone touches it.

### When to use it

- A strange door, a suspicious chest, a crypt, an item nobody recognizes.
- Time permits a 10-minute ritual. In a hurry, spend a 1st-level slot.
- Another party member doesn't already cover this reliably. If they do,
  [the growth plan](/growth/) says Detect Magic is the first preparation to
  reconsider.

### Mechanics

1. 1st level, Action (or ritual: 10 extra minutes, no slot), self,
   **Concentration up to 10 minutes**.
2. Kief senses the presence of magical effects within **30 feet**.
3. If he senses any, he can take a **Magic action** to see a faint aura
   around a visible creature or object bearing magic, and learn the
   **school** of a spell that created an effect.
4. Blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood, 1 inch of metal, or a thin
   sheet of lead.
5. It detects magic, not mundane traps, poison, or ill intent. After the
   aura check, follow up with ordinary investigation from the party member
   with the best Investigation.
6. Casting it ends Kief's current Concentration spell. The ritual also
   needs Concentration during the casting.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Ten minutes, ritual Detect Magic, no slot. Then I take a
Magic action to look at the chest: is there an aura, and what school? After
that, Sable checks the lock the ordinary way."

Verbally: *"Kief squints at the chest for a long moment. 'That's either
enchanted,' he says, 'or exceptionally badly stored.'"*

### The rule underneath

Rituals are a slot-free casting mode: 10 minutes longer, only for spells
with the Ritual tag. Detection spells have precise limits (range, blockers,
what exactly is revealed), and the school of magic is a clue, not an
identification. Knowing what a tool does not answer is as important as
what it does.
