Move a measured amount of liquid and observe the mechanism rather than claiming to solve it.

### When to use it

An unfamiliar basin, gutter, or water puzzle has a visible inlet.

### Mechanics

1. Describe the question: where does this small amount go?
2. Use *Shape Water* for one controlled flow change of at most 5 feet.
3. Stop and have the party’s investigator interpret the observed response.

### At the table

“One cup of evidence before a barrel of confidence.”

### The rule underneath

The spell supplies movement, not knowledge of hidden plumbing. Pressure, machinery, and downstream effects remain physical circumstances for the DM to resolve.
