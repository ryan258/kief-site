Corners and doorways, cleared at ankle height.

### When to use it

- Every blind corner and open doorway in a dungeon crawl, when the party is
  moving fast.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big puts one eye past the frame, low. In bad light, a Tiny creature
   at floor level is hard to spot.
2. Closed doors: he listens at the gap. He can't open a heavy or latched
   door; that's a party job, or [Mage Hand](../../kief/k-12/).
3. Kief's Bonus Action share at the moment of the peek gives the party a
   caster's look in one round.
4. Peek, report, move. Anything that needs longer becomes a full advance
   ([play 1](../mb-1/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Corner. Mr. Big puts one eye around it at floor level.
Bonus Action, I take the look myself. Then we go or we don't."

Verbally: *"The corridor never notices the question being asked, at a
height nobody guards."*

### The rule underneath

Cover is graded: half (+2 AC and Dexterity saves), three-quarters (+5), and
total (can't be targeted). A creature showing one eye around a frame has
substantial cover while seeing fine. Sound ignores cover. Peeking is far
safer than standing in the doorway.
