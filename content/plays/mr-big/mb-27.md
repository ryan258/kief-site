Mr. Big's Passive Perception is 13. Know what that does and doesn't catch.

### When to use it

- Deciding whether Mr. Big's watch is enough, or someone should actively
  search.

### Mechanics

1. **Passive Perception 13** (10 + his +3). The DM compares it to a creature's
   Stealth check total when nobody is actively looking.
2. In dim light or darkness, sight-based passive Perception is lower: 5
   less for Disadvantage. In the dark, his passive sight check is 8.
3. A sneaking enemy with +4 to +6 Stealth beats 13 regularly.
4. **Active watching** (the Search action) rolls instead: +3, with the same
   Disadvantage in poor light.
5. Kief's own Passive Perception is 10. The cow is the better lookout, not a
   great one.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big's Passive Perception is 13, but out here at night
it's 8 for sight. Can he at least hear anyone moving through the brush?"

Verbally: *"Mr. Big watches the treeline with complete commitment and a
modest success rate."*

### The rule underneath

Passive score = 10 + all modifiers; Advantage adds 5, Disadvantage subtracts
5. Passive checks are the DM's tool for secret, repeated checks. Knowing
your lookout's real number, adjusted for light, tells you when to post a
second watcher.
