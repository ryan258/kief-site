Physical signals for everyone who isn't telepathic.

### When to use it

- Every operation where allies need to read the cow directly.

### Mechanics

1. Agree a few signals in downtime: **sit** = hold; **circle** = fall back;
   **stare** = look here; **lie down** = danger, freeze; **return** = abort.
   Write them down.
2. Signals should look like things a small animal does anyway, so enemies
   don't recognize them.
3. Kief drives them by telepathy from within 100 feet. Mr. Big follows
   commands; he doesn't decide when to signal on his own.
4. One cow, one team. The other team uses pre-agreed timing instead.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we split: sit means hold, circle means fall back,
stare means look there, lie down means freeze. He's with your team; I'm
driving him from the wall."

Verbally: *"The cow folds himself down onto the flagstones, and three
seasoned professionals stop mid-stride as one, obedient to a creature the
size of a teapot."*

### The rule underneath

On your turn, a brief utterance is free but audible. Pre-arranged signals
avoid both limits: agreed meanings are established fiction that cost zero
actions and zero sound. Anything the table establishes in advance (code
words, formations, protocols) transfers information outside the action
economy.
