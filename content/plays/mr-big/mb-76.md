Wires live at ankle height. So does he.

### When to use it

- Corridors, stairwells, doorways, and trails where someone probably set a
  cheap trap.

### Mechanics

1. The DM compares the wire's DC to his Passive Perception (13) or asks for a
   Search roll (+3). Poor light imposes Disadvantage on sight
   ([play 27](../mb-27/)). Whether his low vantage helps is the DM's call.
2. Slow advance along the edge, a look at each threshold, sit-signal at each
   find ([play 35](../mb-35/)).
3. He never touches. Found wires go to the party, or to Kief's
   [Mage Hand](../../kief/k-12/) from 30 feet with everyone in cover.
4. He can miss things. A clear walk isn't a cleared corridor.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Stairwell's a classic wire spot. Mr. Big takes it first, slow,
with a Light ahead of him. If he sits, there's something. Everyone waits;
Mage Hand pulls it from cover."

Verbally: *"The wire has waited two years at the perfect height to catch a
man. It is found by something whose entire world happens at that height."*

### The rule underneath

Passive Perception (10 + modifiers) is what the DM checks silently when
nobody searches; an active search rolls instead. Describing how you search
lets the DM adjust the difficulty. Light matters for sight-based checks.
