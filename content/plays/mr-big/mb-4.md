*Dragon's Breath* on Mr. Big: a 15-foot cone of fire on his own initiative.
**DM ruling required.**

### When to use it

- A longer fight, where an extra 3d6 cone each round beats what Kief's
  Concentration could hold otherwise.
- Only after the DM rules that Mr. Big's "can't attack" limit allows a
  Magic action that forces a save.
- Ledges and chokepoints where he can exhale and stay behind cover.

### Mechanics

1. Kief: **Bonus Action**, *Dragon's Breath* (2nd level, touch,
   Concentration up to 1 minute) on Mr. Big, cast while adjacent.
2. On Mr. Big's turn: **Magic action**, 15-foot cone. Dexterity save against
   Kief's DC 14, **3d6** of the chosen type, half on a success.
3. The case for allowing it: the exhalation is a save, not an attack roll.
   The case against: the table may read "can't attack" as "can't deal
   damage." Ask before the fight, not during it.
4. He can still move 40 the same turn: lean out, breathe, withdraw.
5. It spends Kief's Concentration. No *Web*, *Pattern*, or *Fly*.
6. **2 HP**. Any area effect or stray arrow ends the platform.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Bonus Action: Dragon's Breath on Mr. Big, fire,
concentrating. On his turn he leans off the bookshelf: 15-foot cone,
Dexterity save DC 14, 3d6 fire. My Action's still free: Fire Bolt."

Verbally: *"Mr. Big opens his mouth for the first time in recorded history.
What comes out is not a moo."*

### The rule underneath

*Find Familiar* says a familiar can't attack but can take other actions.
Whether a granted Magic action that deals damage through a save counts is
exactly the kind of edge the DM owns. Buffs cast on others transfer an
action cost (the recipient's Magic action), and Concentration stays with
the caster.
