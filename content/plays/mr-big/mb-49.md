Nobody surprises the concentrating caster while the cow watches his back.

### When to use it

- Every fight where Kief holds a Concentration spell the enemy wants broken.

### Mechanics

1. Station: 20–30 feet from Kief, watching the arc Kief can't: a doorway,
   a window, the dark side of the room. Hidden and still.
2. Telepathy lands mid-round with no action: *someone, that door*.
3. That warning is time for Kief to Dodge, step behind cover, or save his
   Reaction for *Shield*.
4. Low risk: the warden is away from the melee and never engages.
5. Upgrade: Kief's Bonus Action share turns the warning into a picture.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'm concentrating on the Pattern. Mr. Big holds my back arc
from the alcove. Anything through that side door, I know first, and my
Reaction's waiting for it."

Verbally: *"The assassin enters through the door the gnome can't see, on
feet that have never made a sound, and is immediately, comprehensively
noticed by a cow."*

### The rule underneath

In 2024, a creature that is surprised has Disadvantage on Initiative, so
ambushes are decided by the detection contest before combat. Concentration's
biggest threat is the hit you never saw coming. A warning before contact is
worth more than any +1.
