The way out, found before the way in.

### When to use it

- Before the party enters any building, cave, or social trap it might need to
  leave quickly.

### Mechanics

1. While the party watches the front, Mr. Big circles the back: rear doors,
   cellar hatches, roof access (Climb 40), the low spot in the wall.
2. Grade each exit: cow-sized, gnome-sized, everyone-sized. A grate only he
   fits is still a message route ([play 32](../mb-32/)).
3. Kief's [Misty Step](../../kief/k-23/) needs a space he can see; the
   survey tells him which window matters.
4. Re-run it when things change. Exits get locked and guarded exactly when
   they become interesting.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we walk into the guildhall, Mr. Big circles it. I
want every exit graded: what fits him, what fits me, what fits Brenna in
armor."

Verbally: *"Kief steps through the front door already knowing about the
kitchen door, the coal chute, and the loose grate over the culvert."*

### The rule underneath

A creature can squeeze through a space sized for a creature one size
smaller, paying extra movement. Tiny creatures fit where others can't.
Surveyed routes are pre-paid options: every known exit is a movement or
teleport destination the crisis round doesn't have to discover.
