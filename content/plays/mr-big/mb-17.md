Following a person of interest through town, below eye level.

### When to use it

- A suspect leaves the meeting, and the party needs the destination without
  showing a face they'll need later.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big follows at 30–60 feet, Stealth +4, using carts, stalls, and
   doorways.
2. Kief trails a block behind, keeping Mr. Big within 100 feet. The cow
   follows the target; Kief follows the cow.
3. Target goes indoors: Mr. Big marks the door, circles for windows
   ([play 7](../mb-7/)), and waits for Kief.
4. Lose visual contact and the tail is over. There's no scent trail to
   re-acquire; try [the animal witnesses](../mb-30/) instead.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The clerk's moving. Mr. Big follows at street level, and I
shadow a block back, keeping him within a hundred feet. If the clerk goes
indoors, I want the building and every door."

Verbally: *"The clerk doubles back twice and checks the reflections in two
shop windows. No one is following. Something is."*

### The rule underneath

Tailing is Stealth against the target's Perception stretched over time.
The DM decides how often to check, usually when the situation changes.
Controlling distance and cover decides where the checks happen.
