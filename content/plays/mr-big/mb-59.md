Ride the target's own deliveries through the gate.

### When to use it

- A guarded compound that receives deliveries: grain, wine, candles.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big boards at the last stop before the gate, under the tarp,
   Stealth +4 against loaders who aren't looking for passengers.
2. Inside, the compound is probably beyond 100 feet of anywhere Kief can
   wait. It becomes a **standing-orders** mission
   ([play 90](../mb-90/)) with **simple** orders: "find the person with
   the red scarf, stay near, come back on the next empty cart."
3. A cat can't map a keep from memory. The realistic product is one fact
   and a way in.
4. Set a deadline. If he doesn't return, Kief recasts *Find Familiar* when
   incense allows, which brings him back ([play 88](../mb-88/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Every Thursday the wine cart goes up to the keep. This
Thursday it carries one extra passenger. Orders: find the room with the
guards outside it, and come back on Friday's empties."

Verbally: *"The gate guards probe the wine casks with their usual
thoroughness. In the dark between a cask and the cart's rail, held perfectly
still, the keep's troubles begin at two miles an hour."*

### The rule underneath

Hiding among cargo is Stealth against whoever looks, with the DM weighing
how thoroughly it's handled. Vehicles move at their own pace, which makes
them free transport for small creatures. Orders to a low-Intelligence
creature have to be simple enough for it to follow.
