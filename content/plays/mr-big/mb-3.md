Mr. Big notices well and understands like a cat. Treat his reports that
way.

### When to use it

- Every time Mr. Big reports anything.
- Deciding whether a question needs Mr. Big, Kief's shared senses, a
  ritual, or a party member.

### Mechanics

1. **What he has**: Perception +3 (Passive 13), Darkvision 60, hearing and
   sight at cat height, Stealth +4.
2. **What he doesn't have**: Keen Smell (the old record's legacy trait is
   gone), language, or the ability to read, count reliably, or identify
   magic. Intelligence 3.
3. His telepathic report is impressions: *two people, one light, a dog,
   metal noises*. Kief interprets; the DM filters what a cat would
   understand.
4. Need detail? Kief's Bonus Action shares the senses ([play 2](../mb-2/)).
5. Need smell, magic, or motives? Route the question elsewhere
   ([play 29](../mb-29/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "What does Mr. Big notice in the storeroom? Perception plus
three if it's a check. And tell me what a cat would make of it, not what I
would."

Verbally: *"Mr. Big stares at the storeroom for a long moment and reports,
in effect: people, recently; also, a box. Kief nods as if that settles the
matter."*

### The rule underneath

Checks use the rolling creature's modifiers: the cat's +3, never Kief's
numbers. A sensor returns what its source can plausibly perceive and
understand. Matching the question to the sensor, and dropping traits that
aren't on the current stat block, keeps reports honest.
