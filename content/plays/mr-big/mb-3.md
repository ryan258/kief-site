Mr. Big is intelligent and can legitimately help. Ask what he perceives and understands; his exact capabilities remain unspecified.

### When to use it

When Mr. Big reports something or the party asks him to investigate.

### Mechanics

1. The proposed Cat block supplies Perception +3 and Darkvision 60 feet; confirm the block before relying on those numbers.
2. Do not carry over the old Intelligence 3 assumptions about counting, memory, or comprehension.
3. Intelligence does not automatically grant literacy, languages, specialist skills, or magical detection. Ask the DM what applies.
4. Let him report observations and exercise judgment, including declining unsafe work.
5. Shared senses remain a provisional option pending confirmation of the remaining spell mechanics.

### At the table

“What did Mr. Big notice, and what does he make of it?”

### The rule underneath

The DM’s [confirmed familiar ruling](/rulings/#confirmed-campaign-override) governs this play. Remaining 2024 mechanics are provisional until confirmed.
