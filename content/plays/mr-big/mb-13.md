The standard room check before the party settles in.

### When to use it

- Any room the party intends to rest in, search, or hold, not just pass.

### Mechanics

1. **Occupants**: a full interior walk at Stealth +4: behind furniture, under
   beds, up the walls and into the rafters with Climb 40.
2. **Exits**: every door, window, grate, and gap at floor and wall level.
3. **Anything odd**: a sound, movement, light where there shouldn't be one.
4. What he **can't** certify: magic, mechanisms, glyphs, or traps. Route
   those to [Detect Magic](../../kief/k-34/) and the party's best searcher.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we bed down: Mr. Big checks for occupants, then exits.
If he sits down in the middle, nothing moved. The walls and the chest still
get a proper search."

Verbally: *"The cow inspects the room like a landlord. Only when he folds
himself down on the hearthstone does the party file in behind him."*

### The rule underneath

In 2024, the Search action is a Wisdom check (Perception, among others) and
the Study action is an Intelligence check (Investigation, Arcana). An
Intelligence 3 creature isn't the one to Study a mechanism. Route each part
of a task to the creature whose actions and numbers fit it.
