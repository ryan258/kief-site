The perfect blend is out there: part tea, part herb, part prophecy. The cow
helps look.

### When to use it

- Downtime and travel days when Kief is foraging.
- Roleplay for Kief's bond, with no invented healing or drug mechanics.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big looks for plants that match a sample **by sight**. The DM decides
   what a cat can tell apart.
2. Kief asks the local animals what they eat and avoid, with
   [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/).
3. Kief is **not proficient** with the herbalism kit: identification checks
   are plain ability checks, usually Intelligence (−1) or Wisdom (+0).
4. The glow-moss jar: Kief insists it responds to warmth and respect.
   Prestidigitation keeps it at temperature. No rule supports this. None is
   asked to.
5. Never confirm what's in the blends.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Foraging day. Mr. Big goes looking for anything with the same
silver leaf as this sample, and I'll ask the goats what they won't eat.
Herbalism kit on what we find, plain Intelligence."

Verbally: *"They work the hillside in their oldest pattern: the cow drifting
from plant to plant like a small connoisseur, the gnome trailing behind with
the basket, and the jar of moss against his chest glowing very faintly, as
if interested."*

### The rule underneath

Tool checks add proficiency only if you're proficient with the tool.
Foraging during travel is often Wisdom (Survival). Letting different
creatures own different steps of a task is good play; inventing mechanical
benefits for flavor items isn't.
