*Minor Illusion* moves the watcher; the cow uses the empty spot.

### When to use it

- A sentry stands where Mr. Big needs to be, and thirty seconds of absence
  solves it.

### Mechanics

1. Kief casts [Minor Illusion](../../kief/k-10/) (cantrip, 30 feet): a sound
   the sentry must check.
2. Plan both directions first: the sound pulls the sentry **away** from where
   the cow goes. Plan the sentry's return route too.
3. Mr. Big uses the vacated post for a short job: a look at the key hook, a gap
   entry, a listening post.
4. Illusion first, cow second, never both noticed together. Two odd events
   make a pattern, and patterns get reported.
5. The sentry may also just shout for help. The DM plays the guard.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The guard's by the stair. Minor Illusion: a bottle smashing
down the east corridor. He goes to be annoyed at it, and Mr. Big slips up
the stair behind him."

Verbally: *"The sentry investigates the sound of someone else's carelessness,
finds nothing, and walks back past the spot where, during his eleven seconds
of irritation, the night's actual event quietly happened at ankle height."*

### The rule underneath

Cantrips are at will, so they belong in routines, not emergencies. A creature
that merely reacts to an illusion gets no check; only Study or physical
interaction reveals it. And two action budgets beat one: Kief's action casts,
the cow's turn exploits.
