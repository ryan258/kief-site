The room softens before Kief says a word.

### When to use it

- Opening a social scene that benefits from warmth: nervous witnesses, wary
  farmers, bored guards.
- Not in courts, funerals, or war councils, where a cow may read as mockery.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big arrives first: visible, calm, faintly ridiculous.
2. Kief makes the ask with Persuasion (+6). Whether the cow improved the
   creature's attitude, the DC, or granted Advantage is the **DM's call**.
   Describe what happened; don't claim a bonus.
3. It combines naturally with the [tea service](../../kief/k-13/).
4. While the room watches him, he watches the room.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before I ask the village anything, Mr. Big wanders the green
and lets the children say hello. Then I'm 'the nice little fellow with the
cow' asking questions. Does that change how the elder receives me?"

Verbally: *"By the time Kief clears his throat, half the village has met his
cow and the other half is waiting its turn."*

### The rule underneath

The 2024 Influence action: state what you want, the DM weighs the target's
attitude (Friendly, Indifferent, Hostile) and asks for a check if one is
needed. Attitude moves through fiction, not bonuses. Groundwork that shifts
an attitude can change the DC or remove the roll.
