The threat of the cone holds ground. Only if [play 4](../mb-4/)'s ruling
went the party's way.

### When to use it

- Mid-fight doorways, once enemies have seen the cow breathe.

### Mechanics

1. With *Dragon's Breath* running, Mr. Big at a doorway is a 15-foot cone
   problem (Dexterity DC 14, 3d6) for anyone coming through.
2. It lasts while Kief concentrates, up to 1 minute.
3. The deterrent is the DM playing the enemies honestly; some will simply
   charge.
4. Once the spell ends, it's a bluff. Move him out when it drops.
5. He's still a 2-HP creature in a doorway. Area attacks and arrows end it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "They watched the cow torch the first two through the door.
Dragon's Breath is still running, so he just stands there. Does anyone want
to test the doorway?"

Verbally: *"The war band stacks at the door and stops. In the gap stands
something very small that has already been on fire once tonight, on
purpose, from the inside."*

### The rule underneath

There's no zone of control in 2024: creatures don't stop movement past them
except through Opportunity Attacks and readied actions. Area denial is
threat, priced by the enemy. Bluffing with an expired effect works only
until someone tests it.
