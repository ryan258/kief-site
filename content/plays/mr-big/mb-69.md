The Leaf & Ledger's scattered herb merchants know the description.

### When to use it

- Contacting the Leaf & Ledger in a town where faces are unknown but stories
  travel.

### Mechanics

1. Among the herb merchants Kief regards as a loose family, "the tea gnome
   with the tiny cow" is a recognizable description. Whether a given
   merchant has heard it is the DM's call.
2. With recognition, favors and gossip are easier: Persuasion (+6), with
   the introduction already made.
3. The mirror risk: the Greenvale Tea Consortium disputes Kief's license and
   his account of the explosion. In Consortium towns, the cow is a signpost.
   Pocket him.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I don't know the local herb-seller and she doesn't know me,
but she may have heard of the cow. Mr. Big sits on her counter and I mention
the Leaf & Ledger. Has she heard of us?"

Verbally: *"The herb-seller's eyes move from the gnome, who could be anyone,
to the small cow regarding her with courteous patience, and the shop's back
room becomes available."*

### The rule underneath

Organizations and reputation live in established fiction: recognition can
replace persuasion. Background relationships are the DM's to define; ask
what the Leaf & Ledger means in this campaign before leaning on it.
