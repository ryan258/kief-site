Every doctrine above, in firing order.

### When to use it

- Every adventuring day.

### Mechanics

1. **Before the first adventure**: summoning done? Cow appearance approved?
   Incense bought? ([plays 87](../mb-87/), [88](../mb-88/)).
2. **Dawn**: Mr. Big eats first ([play 91](../mb-91/)). Incense count.
   Standing orders if the day has a known shape ([play 90](../mb-90/)).
3. **On the move**: point position ([play 1](../mb-1/)); Kief routes himself
   to keep the 100-foot range ([play 33](../mb-33/)); the signal book live
   ([play 35](../mb-35/)).
4. **At the objective**: survey before entry: circuit, windows, exits
   ([plays 6](../mb-6/), [7](../mb-7/), [19](../mb-19/)). Price the job on the
   risk ladder first ([play 89](../mb-89/)). Take a balk seriously
   ([play 70](../mb-70/)).
5. **In the fight**: pocket or post, decided at the door
   ([play 86](../mb-86/)). If posted, one job, with an exit.
6. **After**: exit check ([play 62](../mb-62/)), watch the aftermath, log
   consumables.
7. **Dusk**: camp loop or room watch ([plays 12](../mb-12/),
   [16](../mb-16/)). Mr. Big eats first.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Morning, out loud like always: he's fed, incense counted,
orders are 'standard column.' Tonight, the camp loop. Whatever the day is,
there's a play for it."

Verbally: *"The day breaks over a gnome and a very small cow eating breakfast
in strict order, and whatever the world intends to be today, it will be met
by the two of them the way it always is: scouted first, priced against two
hit points, and faced together. He has never mooed. The day has never needed
him to."*

### The rule underneath

The adventuring day is the chassis the rules are built on: resources drain
across encounters and refill on rests. Checklists turn that into habit, so
crisis rounds take seconds instead of minutes. Mastery isn't knowing what the
character can do. It's having decided what they do first.
