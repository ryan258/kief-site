A sleeping household, mapped room by room.

### When to use it

- The night before, or the hours before, any operation in an occupied
  building.

### Mechanics

1. Small hours, interior access by an earlier play (the lock-in, a gap, a
   window). Mr. Big walks the house at Stealth +4 in Darkvision.
2. Kief within 100 feet looks and listens through him: sleepers per room,
   dogs, anyone awake and where.
3. Compare with the daytime story. More sleepers than the household admits is
   the finding of the night.
4. Censuses go stale. Run it the same night as the operation.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tonight's census before tomorrow's arrest: Mr. Big walks the
magistrate's house at the second bell, and I'm across the street. Sleepers
per room, dogs, and anyone awake."

Verbally: *"Room by room, the house tells its sleeping truth to a visitor
lighter than a candlestick: the magistrate snores alone; the maid is not in
the maid's room; and in the attic, where no one lives, two men are
breathing."*

### The rule underneath

Sleeping creatures are Unconscious, but this play never touches them;
information is the payload. Darkvision's dim-light Disadvantage applies to
sight in the dark, while sound is unaffected. Intelligence decays: time-stamp
your reconnaissance.
