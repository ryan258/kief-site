What he can carry, and what should never ride on a cow.

### When to use it

- Every courier, fetch, and drop play: this is the shared load math.

### Mechanics

1. The number: Strength 3 × 15, halved for Tiny, is about **22 pounds**.
   What a cat can carry in its mouth or a small harness at speed is far less.
   Establish a practical limit with the DM once.
2. Tiers: notes and tokens (nothing), a key or small vial (light), anything
   bigger (slow, visible, short distances only).
3. A small harness or pouch would make carrying reliable. **Pending
   purchase**, and the cow form needs to accept it.
4. Never ships by cow: anything whose loss ends the mission, anything that
   clinks when quiet matters, anything that stops him running at full speed.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Load for tonight: one note, under a pound, so he keeps full
speed. The signet ring does not go with him; it goes in Brenna's pocket,
where losing it takes a fight."

Verbally: *"The quartermaster's ledger for the party's smallest member
lists: string, one letter. Total burden: negligible."*

### The rule underneath

Carrying capacity in 2024 is Strength score × 15 pounds, adjusted by size.
Encumbrance aside, what limits a courier is fiction and DM rulings: mouths,
harnesses, and noise. "Can he carry it?" is math; "can he carry it quietly?"
is the DM.
