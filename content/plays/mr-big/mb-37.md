When Kief can't see, the cow can.

### When to use it

- Kief is Blinded, in magical darkness, in fog or smoke, and the fight is
  still on.

### Mechanics

1. Bonus Action: share Mr. Big's senses until the start of Kief's next turn.
   If the cow is outside the darkness looking in, Kief has a picture of the
   room.
2. **Targeting honesty**: shared senses supply a view. Whether that meets a
   spell’s sight requirement needs a table ruling. Range and a clear path
   still come from Kief unless the spell says otherwise.
3. The picture is for movement: walking out of the cloud along the cow's
   view. Once Kief's own eyes clear it, his spells work again.
4. Kief's own Darkvision handles normal darkness. This play is for magical
   darkness, fog, and blindness.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The fog's on me. Bonus Action: I take Mr. Big's view from the
doorway. I use the view to navigate: I move twenty
feet left and I'm out. Now I can see."

Verbally: *"Inside the fog, the little gnome walks a careful, curving line
between two swords he cannot see, guided by a spectator at the door with
the best seat in the house."*

### The rule underneath

Blinded: automatic failure on checks requiring sight, Advantage on attacks
against you, Disadvantage on yours. The 2024 spell does not blind or deafen Kief’s own senses while sharing.
Spell targeting through shared senses remains an explicit table ruling;
it never moves the spell’s origin to Mr. Big.
