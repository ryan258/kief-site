The breath weapon, installed on someone with more hit points than the cow.

### When to use it

- A 3d6 cone belongs on the ally holding a door against a crowd, and they're
  not next to Kief.
- The ally's action is worth less than a cone this fight: swarms, mobs,
  tight doors.

### Mechanics

1. Kief casts [Dragon's Breath](../../kief/k-27/) (Bonus Action, 2nd level,
   Concentration). Mr. Big, beside the willing ally and within 100 feet,
   delivers it with his Reaction.
2. The ally exhales as a **Magic action** on their own turns: 15-foot cone,
   Dexterity save against Kief's DC 14, 3d6 damage, half on a success.
3. Tradeoff: the ally survives return fire far better than the cow
   ([play 4](../mb-4/)), but spends their whole action to breathe.
4. Kief's Action that turn can still be a cantrip. The Bonus Action spell used
   the turn's slot.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tam's holding the doorway against the swarm. Bonus Action:
Dragon's Breath, and Mr. Big's at his heel to deliver it. Tam, on your
turns: Magic action, 15-foot cone, Dexterity DC 14, 3d6 fire."

Verbally: *"The fighter feels the little hoof at his ankle, tastes cinders,
and grins through the door at the goblins, who are about to discover that
the man with the shield is now also, technically, a dragon."*

### The rule underneath

Spells that grant an ability keep the caster's numbers and move the action
cost to the recipient. Compare that cost to the recipient's normal turn:
the best target is someone whose action is worth less than the granted
effect.
