The cow in the window means Kief is home, as far as a watcher can tell.

### When to use it

- Someone is watching where Kief sleeps, and Kief needs to be elsewhere.

### Mechanics

1. Watchers use proxies: a lamp, a cow on the sill. Mr. Big visible at the
   window at his usual hours suggests Kief is in.
2. Build the pattern honestly for several days before spending it.
3. Beyond 100 feet, he follows a simple standing order
   ([play 90](../mb-90/)): sit on the sill in the evening. A cat can't keep a
   precise schedule.
4. It breaks the moment someone knocks. Spend it once per watcher.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The Consortium's man watches my window from the tavern. Mr.
Big sits on the sill in the evening as always. I'll be across town. Is the
watcher convinced?"

Verbally: *"The watcher's notebook fills with the truth as he knows it: cow
at the window, subject therefore at home. Across town, the subject raises a
quiet cup."*

### The rule underneath

Deception can be performed through actions, not just words, and the DM
resolves it against the watcher's attention. A cover works until tested,
and every use invites a test. Spend deceptions like slots.
