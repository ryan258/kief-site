Count what's inside without opening anything.

### When to use it

- Before entering any occupied building: how many, where, armed, awake or
  asleep.
- Checking an informant's floor plan against reality.

### Mechanics

1. Climb to each windowsill in turn; look and listen from the corner of the
   frame, low profile, Stealth +4.
2. Kief's Bonus Action sense-share ([play 2](../mb-2/)) at each stop turns
   cat impressions into Kief's own count.
3. Shuttered windows still leak sound and light at the seams. A dark, silent
   room is also a report.
4. Top floor down: upper windows are less watched, and the descent ends at
   the exit.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Window by window: Mr. Big on each sill, me looking through
his eyes. Room one: two sleeping. Room two: lamplit, one awake, writing.
Now we plan."

Verbally: *"The house believes its second story is private. The house has
never been audited by a cow."*

### The rule underneath

Vision needs line of sight and enough light, and windows grant both ways.
The obscurement ladder: Lightly Obscured imposes Disadvantage on
sight-based Perception; Heavily Obscured blocks sight entirely, but not
hearing.
