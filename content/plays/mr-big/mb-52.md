What happens when the form drops, and why the plan survives.

### When to use it

- The moment any hit or blast connects. At 2 HP, this is a "when," not an
  "if."

### Mechanics

1. At **0 HP** Mr. Big **disappears**: no body, no death saves. He returns
   when Kief casts *Find Familiar* again ([play 88](../mb-88/)).
2. The loss is operational: whatever job he had (rear arc, courier, delivery
   point) is gone. Say which one, and reassign it.
3. Mid-fight: no rescue. The party fights on.
4. Replacement cost: an hour (70 minutes as a ritual) and **10+ GP of
   consumed incense**. If there's no incense, there's no Mr. Big until town.
5. Say it at the table so nobody mourns: the spirit is fine.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The blast catches him: 2 HP, the form's gone. No body; he'll
be back when I recast. We've lost the rear-arc watch, so Tam, that door is
yours now."

Verbally: *"Where the little cow stood there is briefly nothing at all. No
wound, no sound, no cow. Somewhere outside the world, something small and
patient begins composing the stare it will give Kief on its return."*

### The rule underneath

0 HP means different things: most monsters die, player characters fall
Unconscious and make death saves, and a familiar disappears until
resummoned. Knowing what 0 HP means for everything on your side changes how
you spend risk: a familiar's loss is priced in gold and time, not grief.

**Cargo reminder:** Worn and carried items remain in Mr. Big’s space when he enters the pocket dimension or disappears at 0 HP. Retrieve them separately. See [shared table rulings](/rulings/).
