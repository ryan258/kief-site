At 0 HP, ask the DM what happens. Death means losing Mr. Big and an unspecified negative effect on Kief.

### When to use it

When Mr. Big is hurt or his safety is uncertain.

### Mechanics

1. Do not assume he disappears unharmed. The DM has not yet specified 0 HP, death saves, stabilization, or healing.
2. Resolve any available rescue with the DM immediately; do not assume either automatic death or automatic survival.
3. Reassign his scouting, watching, or delivery job to keep the party covered.
4. If he dies, he is gone. Resolve the bond’s negative effect on Kief with the DM; no numerical penalty has been supplied.
5. Recasting Find Familiar is not a recovery plan.

### At the table

“Mr. Big is down. What happens at zero, and can we help him?”
