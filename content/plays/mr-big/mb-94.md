Plan illumination before sending a fragile companion ahead. Kief no longer has the Light cantrip.

### When to use it

- The party needs to see a dark hall, cellar, or crossing.
- Someone proposes sending Mr. Big ahead to place a light.

### Mechanics

1. Confirm an actual torch, lantern, or teammate’s light source. Starting equipment is still pending.
2. Kief’s Elementalism can light an existing candle, torch, or lamp within its range; it does not create a lantern or extend through the familiar.
3. Kief cannot deliver Light through Mr. Big: that cantrip was replaced by Shape Water.
4. Do not assume Mr. Big can carry a burning light safely. His form, carrying ability, and willingness matter.
5. Keep the light with the party unless another safe placement is established. Illumination can also reveal the party’s position.

### At the table

Mechanically: “Who has our light source? Let’s illuminate the approach before asking Mr. Big to inspect it.”

Verbally: *“Management requests that we see the problem before stepping in it.”*

### The rule underneath

Removing Light changes party logistics. Neither Shape Water nor familiar touch delivery replaces it. Equipment and teammate abilities must be confirmed rather than assumed.
