Light, delivered somewhere Kief isn't.

### When to use it

- The party needs to see the far side: a chasm, a long hall, a cellar floor.
- Kief doesn't want to be standing in the light himself.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big goes ahead, within 100 feet of Kief, and stands beside a suitable
   object: a stone, a crate, a torch bracket.
2. Kief casts [Light](../../kief/k-9/); Mr. Big delivers the touch with his
   Reaction. The object sheds bright light 20 feet and dim light 20 more,
   for an hour.
3. **No Concentration.** It doesn't compete with Kief's control spells.
4. Light is symmetric: it shows the party the area, and it shows anyone there
   the light. Mr. Big steps out of it.
5. Casting Light again ends the previous one.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big crosses the beam to the far side of the chasm and
stands by a loose stone. I cast Light, he delivers it: forty feet of light
over there, none on us."

Verbally: *"On the far side of the dark, a stone begins to glow, and a very
small cow walks out of the circle of light with the air of a stagehand who
has hit his mark."*

### The rule underneath

Touch spells plus a familiar become ranged utility. Light levels are
tactical terrain, and illumination is symmetric: put light where you want to
look, not where you stand.
