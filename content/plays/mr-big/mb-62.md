The last look before the party steps into the open.

### When to use it

- The end of every job, between "we have it" and "we're out."

### Mechanics

1. Before the party exits, Mr. Big runs the route first: the alley, the
   wagons, the rooftops.
2. Kief looks through him at the likely ambush spots.
3. Signal by the book ([play 35](../mb-35/)): sit = hold; lie down =
   danger, freeze. Kief verbally relays a clear route; do not reverse the signals.
4. An ambush found is the payoff. Leave by the second route
   ([play 19](../mb-19/)), or [Web](../../kief/k-25/) their position.
5. Hidden ambushers may still beat his Perception. A clear check is good
   news, not a guarantee.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We've got the ledger. Nobody moves. Mr. Big checks the alley
and I look through him… He's lying down. Three of them behind the wagons.
Cellar route, or a Web on those wagons."

Verbally: *"The ambushers hold their positions beautifully, eyes on the
door. The door does not open. Twenty feet above them, their evening has
already been reported."*

### The rule underneath

Hidden attackers have Advantage on attacks against creatures that can't see
them, and surprised creatures have Disadvantage on Initiative. Finding the
ambush first turns the attackers into known, stationary targets who have
already spent their positioning.
