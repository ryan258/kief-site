The cow reads the creature before Kief talks to it.

### When to use it

- A guardian **Beast** stands between the party and its goal: the bear in the
  pass, the mastiffs in the yard, the giant owl in the tower.
- Talking might work better than fighting.

### Mechanics

1. From cover, Mr. Big watches the beast: fed or hungry, calm or agitated,
   alone or guarding young. Kief looks through him.
2. **Go / no-go**: a hungry, cornered, or protective beast will likely fight.
   Choose a different approach.
3. On go: Kief casts [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/) (Beasts only) and
   approaches slowly on the line Mr. Big found. Mr. Big stays out of reach; a
   cat near a predator is a snack.
4. Influence checks with Persuasion (+6). Offer something it wants: food, a
   way around, to be left alone.
5. Monstrosities and other non-Beasts don't understand this spell.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we try to pass the bear, Mr. Big watches it from the
rocks. It's eating, calm, alone. Then I cast Speak with Animals and walk up
slowly with the rest of our salted fish."

Verbally: *"The bear's afternoon is improved by a gnome with an offering and
excellent manners, and supervised, at a respectful distance, by a small cow on
a rock."*

### The rule underneath

Spells often gate on creature type; Speak with Animals affects Beasts only.
Information gathered before a social attempt is worth more than bonuses
during it: the DM sets the DC from the creature's attitude, and approaching a
calm, fed animal is a different check from a hungry, cornered one.
