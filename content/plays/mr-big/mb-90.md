Give an intelligent companion a clear job, an exit, and room to refuse or retreat.

### When to use it

Before scouting or separating from the party.

### Mechanics

1. Agree on the job, what to report, and when to return.
2. The recorded 100-foot telepathy limit is a provisional 2024 detail; confirm it with the DM.
3. Out of contact, let Mr. Big use judgment and return when circumstances become unsafe.
4. The DM considers him intelligent. Do not impose Intelligence 3 comprehension limits or invent a new score, languages, or skills.
5. Clear instructions reduce confusion; they do not create absolute obedience or willingness to sacrifice himself.

### At the table

“Watch the door from cover. Come back if it opens, if you feel unsafe, or by dusk.”

### The rule underneath

The DM’s [confirmed familiar ruling](/rulings/#confirmed-campaign-override) governs this play. Remaining 2024 mechanics are provisional until confirmed.
