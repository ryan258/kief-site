Beyond 100 feet, he runs on what he was told. Say it simply.

### When to use it

- Every mission that leaves telepathy range: the orders are the mission.

### Mechanics

1. A familiar acts independently but obeys Kief's commands. Beyond telepathy,
   the last orders stand.
2. Format, three short lines: **the job** (watch that door), **the trigger**
   (if someone opens it), **the return** (come back to Kief by dark, no
   matter what).
3. The unconditional return line is mandatory.
4. Keep it concrete. Intelligence 3 follows "a person, a door, dark." It
   doesn't follow "anyone suspicious" or "every twelve minutes."
5. Orders can't handle surprises or be updated remotely. The answer to
   surprise is the return line.

### At the table

Mechanically: "He'll be out of range tonight, so short orders: watch the
boathouse door; if anyone goes in, come and find me; come back by dawn no
matter what."

Verbally: *"The gnome speaks three sentences to the cow, and the cow, who
has never once acknowledged an order in any visible way, turns and walks
into the dark carrying them."*

### The rule underneath

A familiar's obedience is unusual: absolute obedience with independent
action. Intelligence limits the complexity of what it can carry out, and
the DM enforces that honestly. When a rule gives you an agent, its command
interface is its power level.
