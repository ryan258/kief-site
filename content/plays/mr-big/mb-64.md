Children come to see the cow, and children talk.

### When to use it

- Town gossip the adults won't share: who visits at night, where the
  strangers stay.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big draws children; parents see a harmless novelty.
2. Kief asks nothing sharp. He listens. Insight +3 sorts observation from
   make-believe.
3. **Strictly passive.** No tasks, no pressure, no secrets extracted, no
   child sent anywhere. Once information points toward danger, children are
   out of it and the party is in.
4. Children also grab. Tiny creatures can be hurt by enthusiastic hands;
   Mr. Big tolerates one at a time.
5. Mr. Big eats whatever they offer, first ([play 91](../mb-91/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "I sit on the well-edge and let the children meet the cow. I
ask nothing. Twenty minutes later I've heard about the cellar lights and the
wagon that comes when it rains. Insight to sort it."

Verbally: *"The village's counterintelligence apparatus, forty adults with
closed mouths, is defeated in an afternoon by a tiny cow and the fact that
Ada, age six, has been dying to tell someone about the rain-wagon."*

### The rule underneath

When there's no uncertainty, there's no check: people volunteer information
to a friendly listener. The roll happens at the margin, usually Insight to
sort what's reliable. Low-reliability sources are a corroboration problem,
not a roll-harder problem.
