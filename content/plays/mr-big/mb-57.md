Tiny is a movement mode. The building's smallest doors are open.

### When to use it

- Grates, vents, drains, and gaps: routes nothing person-sized has used.

### Mechanics

1. The DM decides whether a specific gap fits a Tiny creature. The survey
   habit ([play 19](../mb-19/)) finds them before they're needed.
2. Uses: the vent into the anteroom ([play 54](../mb-54/)), the drain out
   of the cell block ([play 32](../mb-32/)).
3. **Never into a space he can't turn around in** without a confirmed exit.
   Kief can dismiss him from any distance only if the DM allows; the
   reliable rescue is Kief getting within range.
4. Pair with [Alter Self](../../kief/k-26/): the cow takes the gaps, the
   disguised gnome takes the doors.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The anteroom has an air grate. Mr. Big's confirmed it goes
through and opens out. That's his door. He'll be inside before the clerk
unlocks the front, and I'll be in range from the lobby."

Verbally: *"The architect spent a fortune on the doors. The budget for the
four-inch gap behind the cornice was zero."*

### The rule underneath

Squeezing into a space sized for one size smaller costs extra movement, and
the DM decides whether a real gap is large enough. Routes that only one size
class can use turn architecture into a private road network.
