Kief unseen, cow conspicuous: attention management with two bodies.

### When to use it

- A watched gate or courtyard, when something will be noticed, so the party
  chooses what.

### Mechanics

1. Kief casts [Invisibility](../../kief/k-24/) on himself (2nd level,
   Concentration, up to an hour). It ends if he attacks, deals damage, or
   casts a spell.
2. Mr. Big walks a parallel, **visible** line: the odd little animal
   ([play 10](../mb-10/)) the watchers look at.
3. Invisible isn't silent. Kief still rolls Stealth when the DM asks; the
   cow gives a plausible explanation for small sounds.
4. Whether the watchers look at the cow is the DM's call.
5. Meet at a rally point past the watchers. Kief can't be seen, so Mr. Big
   finds him by telepathy.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility on me, concentrating. Mr. Big strolls up the
middle of the lane while I cross behind the guards. Stealth for the gravel.
We meet at the well."

Verbally: *"Three sentries watch a tiny cow promenade past the gatehouse with
the gravity of a visiting dignitary. The second most interesting thing passes
four feet behind them, unseen, on held breath."*

### The rule underneath

The Invisible condition hides you from sight only: you can still be heard,
and creatures that guess your space attack with Disadvantage. Hiding is
contested attention as well as dice: a Perception check spent on a decoy is a
check not spent on you.
