An extra alarm inside the room.

### When to use it

- Inn rooms and guest quarters behind a door someone else has the key to.

### Mechanics

1. He beds down facing the door, between the party and the entry. Passive
   Perception 13 and Darkvision 60.
2. Wake protocol: physical, a hoof to Kief's face, then telepathy.
3. Layer it: the lock, a party watch, and the cow fail in different ways.
4. He's one Tiny observer. A skilled intruder can still beat Passive 13.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We take the room. Mr. Big sleeps facing the door. Anything at
the latch, he puts a hoof in my face and I wake everyone quietly."

Verbally: *"The assassin picks the lock beautifully. What his training never
covered is the pair of calm dark eyes already open at knee height,
unimpressed, and counting his knives."*

### The rule underneath

Unconscious creatures drop what they hold, automatically fail Strength and
Dexterity saves, give attackers Advantage, and take critical hits from
attackers within 5 feet. That's why watches are worth table time. Passive
scores keep working for whoever is awake.
