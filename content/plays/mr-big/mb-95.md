Kief takes the inside in disguise. The cow takes the outside.

### When to use it

- A survey of a target that allows visitors: a shop, a hall, an office.

### Mechanics

1. Kief casts [Alter Self](../../kief/k-26/), Change Appearance
   (Concentration, up to an hour), and visits as someone else.
2. Mr. Big walks the outside within 100 feet: exits, windows, patrols
   ([play 6](../mb-6/), [play 19](../mb-19/)).
3. Telepathy is free; looking through Mr. Big costs Kief a Bonus Action and
   his attention while he's supposed to be in conversation.
4. The product is a layered picture: the room Kief saw, and where people go
   when they leave it.
5. Kief has no Deception proficiency. Let someone else talk, or keep it to
   honest questions.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I go into the counting-house as a grey clerk under Alter Self.
Mr. Big circles the building outside. When the manager steps out the back, he
tells me which door."

Verbally: *"Inside, a grey-bearded clerk asks dull questions and receives dull
answers. Outside, a small cow notes which door the manager uses when he
doesn't want to be seen."*

### The rule underneath

Different sources see different things: the caster's own eyes, the
familiar's eyes, and each has its own limits. Only Kief's own sight counts for
targeting spells. Multi-channel recon is legal and strong; multi-channel
targeting isn't.
