"Mr. Big eats first." Canon, and a little public ceremony.

### When to use it

- Every shared meal. The bond is played straight.

### Mechanics

1. A man who ceremonially feeds his cow first reads as gentle, disciplined,
   and harmlessly eccentric. Social groundwork, renewed at every table.
2. **It is not a poison test.** Mr. Big has no Keen Smell, 2 HP, and a cat's
   judgment. If poison is a real concern, ask the DM what the party can do
   about it.
3. In negotiations, the pause while he eats is a moment to think.
4. Consistency is the point. Never skip it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before I touch the duke's dinner: Mr. Big eats first, as
always. It's not a test. It's manners."

Verbally: *"The duke's table waits, forks hovering, while a tiny cow
considers his portion with the gravity of a wine steward. The court thinks
it charming."*

### The rule underneath

Habits become established fiction, and established fiction doesn't need
dice. The old guide attached a poison screen to this ritual through a
removed trait. When a trait leaves the stat block, the tactics built on it
leave too, even the charming ones.
