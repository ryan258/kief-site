Sometimes the plan is to get picked up.

### When to use it

- A room the party can't reach, whose owner collects small curiosities.
- Only when the DM agrees the NPC would plausibly do it, and the party
  accepts the fallout when the "toy" walks away.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big holds still where the target's habits will find him
   ([play 9](../mb-9/)). You can't make an NPC pick him up; you can make it
   plausible.
2. **Handled up close, a living animal is obviously alive.** This works on an
   inattentive or distracted carrier, or with a disguise the DM accepts.
3. Inside, the usual surveys apply, with Kief within 100 feet for live
   information.
4. Exit when the household sleeps, or recall him through the pocket
   dimension when Kief is near ([play 85](../mb-85/)).
5. Consequences are real. Use it on targets the party can afford to puzzle.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The collector buys every oddity at the market. Could he be
persuaded to carry off a very still little cow if it's sitting in a basket of
carvings? If yes, Mr. Big rides upstairs, and I'll be in the street outside."

Verbally: *"'Charming little thing,' the collector murmurs, and carries it
upstairs past both guards, unlocking every door for it personally."*

### The rule underneath

NPCs act according to the DM's reading of them, not the players' script.
Every con works by stacking the fiction until the NPC's most in-character
action is the one you want. Once carried, an object goes everywhere its
carrier goes.
