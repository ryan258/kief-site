A second watch that doesn't get sleepy.

### When to use it

- Camps in unsecured territory: a slow loop around the sleeping party.
- Layered **under** the party's watch shifts, never instead of them.

### Mechanics

1. Loop route 50–80 feet out, inside telepathy range of Kief.
2. Alert protocol: anything larger than a rabbit, he returns and wakes Kief.
   He doesn't investigate alone ([play 90](../mb-90/)).
3. Telepathy to a sleeping Kief is unreliable. The drill is physical: a hoof
   to the face, then the report.
4. Passive Perception 13 and Darkvision 60 help, but he's one small observer
   and the DM decides what he notices.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Camp watch: Mr. Big loops the perimeter at sixty feet, all
night. Standing order: anything bigger than a rabbit, he comes back and
wakes me. Then I wake everyone."

Verbally: *"The party sleeps inside a moving circle of attention. The
forest sends its usual volunteers, and each one is noticed, catalogued,
and found boring."*

### The rule underneath

A sleeping creature is Unconscious and wakes when it takes damage or
someone uses an action to shake it; whether a noise wakes it is the DM's
call. That's why a physical wake-up is the reliable protocol. Watches are
built into how Long Rests work; plan them, don't hope.
