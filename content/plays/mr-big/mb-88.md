An hour of ritual, scheduled like the resource it is.

### When to use it

- **Before the first adventure.** Mr. Big's first-session summoning is not
  established.
- After any form loss ([play 52](../mb-52/)).

### Mechanics

1. *Find Familiar*: casting time **1 hour**. As a **ritual**, 70 minutes and
   no slot. The free Magic Initiate cast (once per Long Rest) also needs the
   full hour.
2. Every casting consumes **10+ GP incense** ([play 87](../mb-87/)).
3. Schedule it inside other time: travel breaks, a watch shift, downtime.
   Casting is activity; ask the DM how it interacts with a rest.
4. Seventy minutes of ritual in hostile territory needs a watch posted by the
   party.
5. The DM must approve the cosmetic cow appearance.
6. He returns as himself: same Mr. Big, same silence, same opinions.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We lost the form at noon, so tonight during Tam's watch I
ritual-cast Find Familiar: seventy minutes, no slot, one portion of incense.
By midnight, Mr. Big is back."

Verbally: *"Smoke curls up from the little brazier through the whole length
of the watch, smelling of ten gold pieces and forgiveness. At the end of it,
something small steps back into the world and resumes the patrol."*

### The rule underneath

Any prepared spell with the Ritual tag can be cast as a ritual in 2024: 10
extra minutes and no slot. Long casting times need uninterrupted focus, and
the DM decides what counts as an interruption. The daily rhythm of slots,
rests, rituals, and hours is a real economy.
