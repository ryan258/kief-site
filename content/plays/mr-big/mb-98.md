*Hypnotic Pattern* freezes the room; the cow guards the minute it bought.

### When to use it

- Every successful Pattern ([Kief plays 2](../../kief/k-2/),
  [29](../../kief/k-29/), [72](../../kief/k-72/)).

### Mechanics

1. Division of the minute: the party works the sleepers, Kief holds
   Concentration and calls the order, and Mr. Big watches **everything that
   isn't the cube**: doors, stairs, windows.
2. He's posted against three threats: reinforcements, anyone who might wake a
   sleeper, and the enemy who passed the save and went quiet.
3. A telepathic ping sends the party's next free hands to the problem before
   it grows.
4. As the work finishes, he checks the exit route first
   ([play 62](../mb-62/)).
5. He stays out of the cube and away from waking enemies.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Pattern's holding on four. Rope by pairs, I call the order. Mr.
Big has the stairwell and the side door. If he pings, Brenna, you're the
response; the rest keep tying."

Verbally: *"Four raiders stand spellbound in the lamplight while the party
moves among them with rope, and at the room's dark edge, the smallest member
stands the watch that decides whether this minute stays a miracle."*

### The rule underneath

A "disabled" enemy is disabled by specific clauses, and every clause is a job:
Hypnotic Pattern ends for a creature that takes damage or is shaken awake,
and ends for everyone if Kief loses Concentration. Control magic turns fights
into logistics, and logistics is a team sport.
