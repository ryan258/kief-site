Fixed-post observation, for as long as it takes.

### When to use it

- A door, cache, or crossroads that will pay off eventually, while the
  party has other things to do.

### Mechanics

1. Post him with sight of the target and somewhere to be furniture: a
   woodpile, a wall-top, a shelf ([play 9](../mb-9/)).
2. Within 100 feet, live pings. Beyond it, a **simple** standing order
   ([play 90](../mb-90/)): "when someone opens that door, come find Kief."
3. His report is impressions: *two came, hooded, one tall*. Kief translates.
   Don't expect times, names, or counts beyond a few.
4. The DM may ask for Stealth checks over long watches.
5. He needs food and rest like any creature. Swap him out, or accept gaps.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We can't sit on the crypt for two days, so Mr. Big does:
post in the hedge with sight of the door. Standing order: when anyone opens
it, come and find me."

Verbally: *"For a day and a night the crypt is watched by something the
graveyard accepts as an ornament. When the door finally opens, the ornament
is already gone, and the news is traveling at forty feet a round."*

### The rule underneath

Outside combat, the rules charge time, not actions. What makes a stakeout
mechanical is the report's quality: an Intelligence 3 creature holds
impressions, not transcripts. Match the sensor to the question.
