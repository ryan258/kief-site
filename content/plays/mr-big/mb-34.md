A live warning with a little judgment: the cow watches, Kief knows.

### When to use it

- Guarding an approach, a door, or the party's rear during an operation.

### Mechanics

1. Post Mr. Big with eyes on the watched thing, inside 100 feet. His alert is
   instant, silent, and costs no action.
2. He can report roughly: *people, several, coming*. Kief's Bonus Action
   share turns that into a picture before the party commits.
3. One cow, one direction. He watches the likeliest approach; the party
   covers the rest.
4. His Passive Perception is 13, lower in poor light
   ([play 27](../mb-27/)). Quiet enemies can get past him.

### At the table

Mechanically: "While we work the vault, Mr. Big holds the stair landing.
Anything on the stairs, I know right away, and I'll look through him
before we decide what to do."

Verbally: *"The vault crew works in confidence, because the stairwell is
watched by a sentry who has never once, in his entire career, asked for a
break."*

### The rule underneath

The Ready action lets a creature pre-commit a response at the cost of its
action and Reaction. A familiar's telepathic alert costs nothing, so the
whole party gets warning without anyone paying the Ready cost.
Information that arrives without an action lets everyone spend their real
actions on the response.
