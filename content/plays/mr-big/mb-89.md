Judge every job by safety, an exit, and Mr. Big’s willingness. His life is not a consumable.

### When to use it

Whenever anyone proposes a job for Mr. Big.

### Mechanics

1. Prefer observation from cover and work beside the party; even these jobs require judgment.
2. For courier runs or scouting, agree on a safe return and a reason to abort.
3. Help in melee and delivery under fire expose him to danger. Seek a safer alternative; urgency does not compel consent.
4. Never plan on tanking, springing a trap with his body, or deliberately getting him hit.
5. If he dies, he is gone and Kief is negatively affected. The exact penalty remains unknown.

### At the table

“Can he do it safely and get back? If not, we need another plan.”
