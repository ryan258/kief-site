Every job priced against 2 HP in advance, so nobody argues mid-round.

### When to use it

- Whenever anyone, Kief included, proposes a cow job.

### Mechanics

1. **Tier 1, always yes**: scouting from cover, watching, pocket transit,
   social presence.
2. **Tier 2, yes with a route**: courier runs, gap runs, interior plants, bait
   runs. Anything with a planned exit.
3. **Tier 3, only when it decides the fight**: Help in melee, touch delivery
   under fire, the breath platform in the open.
4. **Tier 4, never**: tanking, springing traps by body, testing wards by
   touch, anything where the plan is him getting hit.
5. The price of loss is bounded: an hour, 10+ GP of incense, and missing
   coverage until then ([plays 52](../mb-52/), [87](../mb-87/),
   [88](../mb-88/)). Spend him correctly, neither wasted nor hoarded.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Proposal: he carries the rope across the archers' lane.
That's Tier 3. Does it decide the fight? No? Then Mage Hand does thirty feet
and Brenna crawls the rest."

Verbally: *"The party has a currency it rarely spends, small and warm and
worth exactly one emergency. Everyone at the fire knows tonight's question:
is this the emergency?"*

### The rule underneath

Resource management is the spine of D&D: hit points, slots, Sorcery Points,
consumables, and familiars are all budgets. A familiar at 0 HP disappears
until resummoned, so its loss is economic: time and a component. Price risks
in the right currency.
