Can the cow pour a potion into the downed fighter? Ask once, in downtime.

### When to use it

- Only if the party **owns** potions (none are confirmed) and the DM has
  ruled that Mr. Big can administer one.

### Mechanics

1. The 2024 rule: drinking a potion, or administering it to a creature within
   5 feet, is a **Bonus Action**.
2. Mr. Big can take actions other than attacking. Whether a cat body can
   unstopper and pour is the **DM's ruling**. The current record says this
   depends on his form and adjudication; don't assume it.
3. If yes: he carries one potion into fights where the risk is acceptable.
4. If no: he can still bring the bottle to a conscious ally, or Kief's
   [Mage Hand](../../kief/k-12/) carries it.
5. Log consumption the moment a potion moves.

### At the table

Mechanically: "One ruling please, before the session: can Mr. Big
administer a potion to someone, or only carry it? We'll note the answer."

Verbally: *"The fighter's eyes flutter open to the taste of cherries and the
sight, inches away, of a small cow standing over an empty bottle with
immense professional satisfaction."*

### The rule underneath

Whether a specific creature can perform an action (anatomy, hands) is a DM
ruling layered on top of the action rules. Rulings secured calmly are table
law; rulings begged mid-crisis are coin flips. Keep a short list of rulings
the build depends on and clear them early.
