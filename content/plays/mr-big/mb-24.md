The gap between two roofs has no wall to climb. Jumper handles it.

### When to use it

- Rooftop-to-rooftop, ledge-to-ledge, shelf-to-shelf routes.
- A gap Mr. Big needs to cross that Climb 40 doesn't solve.

### Mechanics

1. **Jumper**: Mr. Big's jump distance is determined using **Dexterity**
   rather than Strength. His Dexterity is 15.
2. Apply the 2024 jump rules with Dexterity in place of Strength. A long
   jump with a 10-foot run-up covers feet up to that score; without the
   run-up, half. Confirm the numbers with the DM.
3. Each foot jumped costs a foot of movement.
4. A missed landing onto hard ground from a roof is a fall. At 2 HP, don't
   jump what you aren't sure of.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big runs the ten feet along the ridge and jumps the alley
to the next roof. Jumper, so his Dexterity sets the distance: is that
enough?"

Verbally: *"A very small cow sails across the alley with no apparent effort
and lands on the far roof as if he had simply continued walking."*

### The rule underneath

Traits that change which ability governs a calculation are small but
decisive. A cat with Strength 3 would barely jump; with Dexterity 15 it
clears alleys. Jump distance also costs movement, so a jump longer than the
remaining speed doesn't happen that turn.
