He looks like a toy. Let him be a toy.

### When to use it

- Observation posts in occupied rooms: shelf, mantel, market stall.
- He's been glimpsed, and freezing is better than fleeing.

### Mechanics

1. Standing still among clutter, Mr. Big can pass as a carved cow curio. The
   DM decides whether a check is needed, and a creature examining him
   closely may see through it.
2. Placement sells it: among bric-a-brac, not alone in an empty corridor.
3. If handled, stay still. He is a warm, breathing cat under the cow look;
   picking him up may end the illusion.
4. Close inspection, animals, or a relevant magical detection effect may
   expose him. Detect Magic does not identify Fey; ask the DM what ongoing
   magic it detects here. In warded rooms, consider pocketing him
   ([play 86](../mb-86/)).
5. Listening through him costs Kief a Bonus Action each round.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before the meeting fills the room, Mr. Big takes a spot on
the mantel between the candlesticks and the ugly vase and holds still. I'll
listen through him when the talking starts."

Verbally: *"The conspirators sweep the room for listeners: under the table,
behind the tapestry, out the window. On the mantel, a charming little cow
figurine watches them check everywhere he isn't."*

### The rule underneath

Perception notices with senses; Investigation examines closely (the 2024
Study action). A perfectly still thing defeats the casual cues of movement
and sound, so the DM may require a closer look to find it. Plausibility is
a DM call before it is a roll.
