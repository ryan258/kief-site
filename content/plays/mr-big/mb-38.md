Messaging when Kief and the cow can't meet.

### When to use it

- Long watches and any plan where Kief and Mr. Big trade information beyond
  100 feet.

### Mechanics

1. Agree a drop point during setup: a hollow post, a loose brick.
2. Mr. Big can carry and drop a small light object in his mouth, if the DM
   agrees. He can't write, so his reports are tokens.
3. Keep the code tiny. A cat reliably learns "bring the pebble when the door
   opens"; it won't manage five colors.
4. Kief checks the drop at fixed times.
5. Drops beat live contact when someone might be watching Kief.

### At the table

Mechanically: "He watches the safehouse from the hedge. Drop point is the
hollow fencepost. If the courier comes back, Mr. Big drops a pebble there.
I check at dawn and dusk."

Verbally: *"Espionage, as practiced between a gnome and a cow: a fencepost
and a pebble, saying everything that matters in the county this week."*

### The rule underneath

The DM resolves declared routines without playing them out, and
established protocols persist until something interferes. Instructions to a
low-Intelligence creature have to match what it can learn. Prefer systems
whose failures you can predict over checks you have to keep winning.
