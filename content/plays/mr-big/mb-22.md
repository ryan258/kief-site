Sometimes listening beats looking.

### When to use it

- A conversation behind a door or shutter, where sight is blocked but sound
  isn't.
- A dark room where Darkvision is at Disadvantage but hearing isn't.

### Mechanics

1. Post Mr. Big where sound carries: beside the door gap, under the window,
   in the rafters above the table.
2. Kief spends a **Bonus Action** to hear through Mr. Big's ears until the
   start of his next turn. **Kief** understands the words; the cat doesn't.
3. Out of combat, renew each round for as long as the conversation lasts.
4. Perception +3 applies to Mr. Big noticing sounds; Kief's own
   understanding of speech is Kief's languages (Common, Draconic, Gnomish).
5. Thick walls, distance, and noise still limit what can be heard. The DM
   decides.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big sits under the council room window. Bonus Action
each round, I listen through him. It's Common, so I follow every word."

Verbally: *"Kief leans back in his chair at the tavern two streets away,
eyes half closed, and occasionally raises an eyebrow at something only he
can hear."*

### The rule underneath

Heavily Obscured blocks sight, not sound. Hearing-based Perception isn't
penalized by darkness. When a familiar shares senses, the caster perceives
directly and understands with their own mind, which is why a low-Intelligence
familiar can still deliver a precise transcript to a listening caster.
