The top of everything, inspected.

### When to use it

- Objects out of reach without ladders or noise: the top of the bookcase, the
  high shelf where interesting things are put.

### Mechanics

1. Climb 40 up the furniture or wall; look with Kief's shared senses.
2. Retrieval only if it's light enough ([play 78](../mb-78/)) and
   unattended.
3. No glass, no stacked crockery. A crash turns a quiet method into an alarm.
   [Mage Hand](../../kief/k-12/) handles the last few feet instead.
4. A familiar is a second turn-holder: one climbs, the other acts.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The box is on the top shelf, twelve feet up. Mr. Big climbs
the end of the bookcase and I look through him. If it's light and loose,
Mage Hand brings it down."

Verbally: *"The wizard put it on the highest shelf, as wizards do, where it
was safe from everything that needs a ladder."*

### The rule underneath

Climbing with a climb speed is ordinary movement. Handling objects takes a
free interaction or the Utilize action, and whether a cat body can manage a
given object is a DM ruling. Splitting a task across two turn-holders is
often better than overloading one.
