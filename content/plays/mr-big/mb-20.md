Find the edge of a guarded or warded area by watching, not touching.

### When to use it

- Suspected wards, alarms, sentries, or watchdogs on a perimeter the party
  must eventually cross.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big walks **outside** the suspected line: where do guards look, where
   do the dogs go, what do the locals avoid?
2. He doesn't test magic with his body. A glyph that would sting the fighter
   ends the cow.
3. Local animals know where they won't walk. Kief follows up with
   [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/) and asks why.
4. [Detect Magic](../../kief/k-34/) confirms magic within 30 feet of Kief.
5. The crossing is a party decision. Mr. Big's product is a map of where
   not to step.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big walks the tower's perimeter thirty feet out, touching
nothing. Where do the rats not go? Then I ask the rats. Free cast."

Verbally: *"The tower's defenses are secret from people. They are not secret
from the mice, who have opinions, or from the small cow who collects
opinions."*

### The rule underneath

Magical traps trigger on defined conditions and don't scale down for a
small victim, so a 2-HP probe is a sacrifice, not a test. The legal way to
find wards without tripping them: environmental evidence the DM describes,
and detection magic. Ask "what would be true if this were warded?"
