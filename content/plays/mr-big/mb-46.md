An extra turn on the board every round. Spend it like one, quickly.

### When to use it

- Every combat Mr. Big is present for.

### Mechanics

1. He acts on his own initiative: move 40 or climb 40, and an action that
   isn't an attack: Help, Hide, Search, Dash, Dodge, or a Reaction delivery
   later in the round.
2. Default priorities: watch the flank ([play 49](../mb-49/)) > reposition
   for next round's delivery or Help > Hide.
3. Never spent on standing in the open or crossing threatened ground without
   cover.
4. Table etiquette: decide his turn before his initiative arrives. A familiar
   that slows the round wears out its welcome.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big's turn: he climbs to the gallery rail over their
caster. Next round that's a Help or a delivery point. Done."

Verbally: *"Between the fighter's turn and the wizard's, a small shape flows
up the gallery post like a decision being made."*

### The rule underneath

Every creature in combat, familiars included, rolls initiative and takes a
full turn. There's no "delay" option in 2024; Ready is the only way to act
later, and it costs a Reaction. A familiar isn't an accessory; it's a second
turn for the party, and unspent turns are wasted.
