Mr. Big can't track by scent. A dog can, and Kief can ask one.

### When to use it

- Someone left, and the party needs to follow: the fled suspect, the
  kidnapped witness.
- A friendly or persuadable dog is nearby.

### Mechanics

1. Kief casts [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/): free cast or ritual. Ten
   minutes of conversation.
2. Influence check (Persuasion +6) to ask the dog to follow a scent article:
   a glove, a scarf. A treat helps; the DM sets the DC.
3. The dog follows the trail using its own senses and checks. Mr. Big
   shadows it within 100 feet of Kief and reports turns.
4. The spell ends after 10 minutes, but a willing dog may keep going. What it
   understood of the request is the DM's call.
5. Rain, rivers, and time degrade a trail.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Speak with Animals on the butcher's dog, free cast. I offer
the sausage and ask him to follow the smell on this glove. Persuasion, plus
six. Mr. Big goes with him and tells me where they turn."

Verbally: *"The dog sets off with tremendous enthusiasm, nose down. A small
cow follows at a dignified distance, as supervisor."*

### The rule underneath

Speak with Animals allows the Influence action's skills with Beasts, so a
favor is a Persuasion check against a DC set by the animal's attitude.
Delegating a task to a creature with the right senses is often better than
stretching your own. Everyone rolls with their own numbers.
