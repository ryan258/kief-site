Frightened witnesses and rescued prisoners: send the cow.

### When to use it

- The rescued person is panicking; the witness can't stop shaking long
  enough to talk.

### Mechanics

1. A small, warm, silent, gentle animal can settle a frightened person. The
   DM decides whether a check is still needed.
2. Calm people follow directions and move quietly during an escape.
3. People sometimes talk more easily to the cow than to the questioner.
4. Comfort isn't credulity: Kief's Insight (+3) still applies to everything
   said.
5. Mr. Big accepts the duty with dignity and expects a snack.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The clerk's too scared to whisper. I stop asking questions;
Mr. Big climbs into her lap and I pour the tea. When she finally talks, it's
to him. I just listen."

Verbally: *"The rescued girl hasn't spoken since the cellar. On the third
day of the road home she's found asleep at the fire, both arms around a
small cow who is looking at the others with an expression that says: not
one word."*

### The rule underneath

When an approach makes failure implausible, the DM shouldn't call for dice.
Where checks remain, favorable circumstances may grant Advantage. Kind
adjudication and honest adjudication are the same job: Insight still runs.
