Kief's touch spells, delivered up to 100 feet away with a Reaction.

### When to use it

- *Fly* on an ally who's across the battlefield from Kief, when the wall,
  gap, or flying enemy demands it now.

### Mechanics

1. When Kief casts a **touch** spell, Mr. Big, within 100 feet of Kief,
   delivers it with his **Reaction**. The spell is Kief's in every respect.
2. Mr. Big must already be able to touch the recipient. Position him the
   round before.
3. [Fly](../../kief/k-32/): 3rd level, Concentration, Fly Speed 60 for up to
   10 minutes. Ask the ally first.
4. Other touch spells that route this way: *Invisibility*, *Dragon's
   Breath* ([play 42](../mb-42/)), *Light*, *Mending* ([play 43](../mb-43/)).
5. His Reaction is spent. It resets at the start of his next turn.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Brenna needs to reach the archer on the tower, and she's
sixty feet away. I cast Fly; Mr. Big is already at her boots, and his
Reaction delivers it. Fly Speed 60, I'm concentrating."

Verbally: *"A small hoof taps the fighter's boot in the middle of the melee,
and gravity's claim on her is transferred, effective immediately, to
nobody."*

### The rule underneath

The familiar delivery clause: you cast, and your familiar within 100 feet
spends its Reaction to deliver the touch. Range "Touch" plus a familiar
equals an effective 100-foot range with a mobile delivery point. Reactions
are one per round, so the familiar can't deliver twice before its turn.
