Before the big fight, the cow leaves the board on purpose.

### When to use it

- Boss rooms, enemy casters, breath weapons, and any fight where area damage
  is expected, including Kief's own.

### Mechanics

1. Triggers, agreed in advance: a telegraphed breath weapon, a confirmed enemy
   caster, a party plan built on area spells. Any one triggers the stow.
2. Magic action before the door: pocket him ([play 85](../mb-85/)). Fight.
   Recall after.
3. The exception: a fight where one specific cow job matters (a
   [delivery](../mb-41/), [the forward observer](../mb-45/)). Then he does
   that job, and Careful Spell can protect him from Kief's own save spells.
4. Redeploy immediately after: the aftermath is where scouting pays.

### At the table

Mechanically: "That's a dragon's lair. At the threshold: Magic action, Mr.
Big's in the pocket. We fight without a 2-HP variable. When it's over he's
back out, following whatever escaped."

Verbally: *"The party checks its weapons at the lair mouth, and its smallest
member steps out of the world entirely: not fled, not hidden, but filed away
like the good silver before a brawl."*

### The rule underneath

A *Fireball* averages 28 damage in a 20-foot radius, half on a save. Half of
that ends a 2-HP creature many times over. Saves don't protect low-HP
allies; only absence does. Most area spells affect every creature in the
area, allies included; count everyone before choosing the point.
