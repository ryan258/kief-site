Kief performs; Kief watches the crowd through the cow.

### When to use it

- Any scene where Kief holds attention and the audience's reaction is the
  real information.

### Mechanics

1. All eyes on the talking gnome; nobody watches the small shape at the edge
   of the room.
2. Kief drops trigger words deliberately (a name, a cargo, a date) and uses a
   Bonus Action to look through Mr. Big as he says it.
3. The DM decides who reacts and what can be seen. Kief's Insight (+3) reads
   what he observes.
4. The product is a short list for [the tail](../mb-17/).

### At the table

Mechanically: "I do the town-square story about the missing courier and say
'Greenvale' in the middle of it. Right as I say it, Bonus Action: I look
through Mr. Big at the fountain. Who reacts?"

Verbally: *"The square watches the gnome. Only the fountain's smallest
ornament sees the miller's son go pale at a word that should mean nothing to
a miller's son."*

### The rule underneath

NPC reactions are observable fiction, and the DM uses passive scores to
decide what you notice without a called roll. Designing a moment to watch
for turns an open search into a yes-or-no observation at a known time.
Narrowing attention is the legal way to make Perception easier.
