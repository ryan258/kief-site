Party property, searched for by the member closest to the ground.

### When to use it

- A dropped pouch on the day's trail, a dagger lost in the river fight.

### Mechanics

1. No Keen Smell: he searches **by sight**, from ground level, where dropped
   things are. Perception +3, and Kief can look through him.
2. Check the event sites first: fights, fords, scrambles.
3. Light finds come back if he can carry them ([play 78](../mb-78/)). Heavier
   finds get a sit-signal, and a person walks to the mark.
4. Morning habit: have him walk the campsite before departure. Two minutes,
   and fewer things get left.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Someone's pouch is somewhere on yesterday's trail. Mr. Big
backtracks to the ford where we fought, and I look through him at the bank.
If he finds it, he sits by it."

Verbally: *"The cow trots back to the ford and circles it slowly, peering
under each root, and at last sits beside a pouch-shaped absence that has
become a pouch."*

### The rule underneath

Finding a lost object is a Wisdom (Perception) or Survival check, and the DM
picks the pairing that fits the method. Searching where the loss probably
happened narrows the problem more than any bonus does.
