Kind questions, long silences, and a small cow who never looks away.

### When to use it

- A composed subject who expects hard questions and is unprepared for warmth
  and silence.

### Mechanics

1. Staging: subject seated, Kief warm and patient, tea poured, Mr. Big on the
   table watching. Nobody mentions him.
2. Kief lets silences run. Insight +3 reads what the subject does with them.
3. Whether the cow unsettles the subject, and whether that grants Advantage,
   is the **DM's call**.
4. Once per subject: Kief glances at Mr. Big and says "he doesn't believe
   you." Use it on the important lie only.
5. No threats, no harm. This is theatre and patience.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'm friendly, the tea's good, and Mr. Big just watches him. I
let the silence sit. Insight: is he managing the cow in the corner of his eye
instead of his story?"

Verbally: *"The smuggler has outlasted three magistrates. Forty minutes into
being cordially not-accused by a gnome while a toy cow declines to blink, he
hears himself explaining things."*

### The rule underneath

Silence is legal pressure: no rule requires the interrogator to speak, and
Insight reads behavior. Insight against Deception is the check; circumstances
the DM finds convincing can grant Advantage. A bluff backed by visible
strangeness may land, once.
