The resummoning economy: track incense like ammunition, because it is.

### When to use it

- Continuously. Losing the form is a bad hour with incense, and a cowless
  week without it.

### Mechanics

1. Each *Find Familiar* casting **consumes** incense worth **10+ GP**,
   including the free Magic Initiate cast and the ritual.
2. **Current stock is unknown.** The old record's two portions are
   historical, not confirmed starting gear. Settle it with the DM before
   session one.
3. Standing rule: buy incense in every town. Log a portion as spent the
   moment the casting starts.
4. The reserve logic: a spare portion means losing the form
   ([play 52](../mb-52/)) costs an evening, not a shopping trip.
5. The dashboard doesn't track incense. Keep it on the character sheet.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Logging it now: ritual's started, one portion of incense
burning. Next town, we buy more at 10 gold before anything else."

Verbally: *"In Kief's journal, between a tea recipe and a sketch of a
suspicious fencepost, the most carefully maintained figure in the party's
economy: 'INCENSE.'"*

### The rule underneath

Material components come in three tiers: unpriced and not consumed (a
component pouch covers these across Kief's spell sources; his Sorcerer focus
works only for Sorcerer spells), priced but reusable (must be owned), and
consumed (must be owned and are used up). A focus never covers the last two,
and neither does Subtle Spell. Wizard Magic Initiate *Mending* needs either
the pouch or its two lodestones if Kief brings only a Sorcerer focus.
Scan every spell for "consumes."
