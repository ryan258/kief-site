Track confirmed summoning supplies. A spare portion of incense is not a spare Mr. Big.

### When to use it

When arranging first summoning or updating actual inventory.

### Mechanics

1. Starting incense stock is unknown; historical inventory does not establish current ownership.
2. The provisional 2024 reference consumes 10+ GP incense per casting, including a free feat cast or ritual. Confirm that this carries over.
3. Record supplies and actual consumption on the character sheet.
4. Putting an existing familiar away is distinct from summoning for the first time; confirm the procedure and costs.
5. Death cannot be budgeted as another portion of incense.

### At the table

“What supplies do I need for his first summoning? I’ll record what we actually have.”

### The rule underneath

The DM’s [confirmed familiar ruling](/rulings/#confirmed-campaign-override) governs this play. Remaining 2024 mechanics are provisional until confirmed.
