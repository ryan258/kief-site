The dismissal and recall: a pocket dimension is a parking space.

### When to use it

- Transit: checkpoints, ships, crowds, anywhere a visible cow is cost without
  benefit.
- The abort handle on every risky play in this guide.

### Mechanics

1. **Magic action** (Kief's): temporarily dismiss Mr. Big to a pocket
   dimension. No ritual, no incense.
2. **Magic action** (Kief's): he reappears in an unoccupied space **within 30
   feet of Kief**.
3. Both are Kief's actions. Mr. Big can't dismiss or recall himself.
4. In the pocket he can't sense, report, or be harmed: zero risk, zero
   coverage.
5. In combat, each is Kief's whole Action for the turn.
6. Repositioning: dismiss at the south wall, walk an hour north, recall. He
   arrives beside Kief, unseen on the route.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Checkpoint town, and they impound animals. Magic action: Mr.
Big goes to the pocket dimension at the gate. Tonight, Magic action, he's
back within thirty feet."

Verbally: *"The little cow steps sideways into a fold of air he treats as a
private study, and the checkpoint searches a party that has never, on any
official record, owned a cow."*

### The rule underneath

The pocket dimension is written into *Find Familiar* itself; read your
spells all the way down. Dismiss and recall are separate Magic actions, so
saving the cow mid-combat costs Kief his action. Long-lasting spells hide
management verbs in their text; knowing them early is free power.
