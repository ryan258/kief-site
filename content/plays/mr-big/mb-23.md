The best seat in the room is above it.

### When to use it

- Meetings, warehouses, temples, and halls with exposed beams.
- A fight is likely, and Mr. Big should be out of reach while still
  watching.

### Mechanics

1. Climb 40 up a post or wall to the rafters before the room fills.
2. From above, he sees hands under tables, weapons under cloaks, and who
   moves where. People rarely look up.
3. Height is safety from melee, not from arrows or area spells.
4. Kief listens and looks through him with Bonus Actions
   ([play 22](../mb-22/)).
5. A fight breaks out: he stays up there. His job is reporting positions,
   not joining in.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big is in the rafters before the auction starts. When the
bidding gets tense, I look through him: who's got a hand on a weapon?"

Verbally: *"Beneath the auction, fortunes change hands. Above it, a tiny cow
sits on a beam and keeps the real minutes."*

### The rule underneath

Elevation changes line of sight and reach. Melee attacks need the target
within reach, so a creature in the rafters is out of melee entirely.
Ranged attacks and area spells ignore height. Perception is still modified
by light and obscurement from above.
