The scout's natural enemy isn't the guard. It's the guard's dog.

### When to use it

- Any site with dogs: farms, manors, military camps, kennels.

### Mechanics

1. Dogs have keen senses and a natural interest in small animals. Expect
   them to notice Mr. Big sooner than people do. The DM decides.
2. A barking dog chasing a small animal may be ignored by the guards, or
   may not. Don't count on it.
3. **Route around**: roofs and walls (Climb 40) keep him above the dogs.
4. **Ask first**: [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/) lets Kief talk to the
   dogs. An Influence check might make them friendly, or at least tell Kief
   where they patrol.
5. Never let a dog catch him. One bite ends the form.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Two dogs in the yard. Mr. Big stays on the wall-top the whole
way. Later, I'll have a quiet word with the dogs themselves: free Speak with
Animals."

Verbally: *"The dogs track the little cow along the wall with deep
professional suspicion. The cow, from a height, ignores them with deeper
professional contempt."*

### The rule underneath

Many beasts have Keen Smell or Keen Hearing, giving them Advantage on
Perception checks that use those senses. Their Passive Perception is often
higher than a person's. Check an enemy stat block's senses before assuming
your scout's Stealth is enough.
