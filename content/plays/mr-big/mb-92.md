Once summoned, that individual is your familiar. Mr. Big’s intended cow appearance and Cat block still need approval.

### When to use it

When confirming Mr. Big’s identity and capabilities before play.

### Mechanics

1. The DM described a persistent physical companion. Do not assume recasting offers a new body or a menu of replacement forms.
2. The intended appearance is a tiny, silent cow; the proposed mechanics use the 2024 Cat block. Both need confirmation under this ruling.
3. Creature type and any ability to change form remain unspecified.
4. If a job needs flight or another capability, use an approved spell or a teammate rather than assuming a different familiar form.

### At the table

“Mr. Big is the companion I have in mind. How would you handle his form and stats?”

### The rule underneath

The DM’s [confirmed familiar ruling](/rulings/#confirmed-campaign-override) governs this play. Remaining 2024 mechanics are provisional until confirmed.
