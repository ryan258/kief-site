The spell offers other shapes. The cow stays a cow.

### When to use it

- Every resummoning, when the rules offer a menu and Kief doesn't look at
  it.

### Mechanics

1. *Find Familiar* lets the caster choose a form, and recasting can change
   it. An owl would fly.
2. The choice: **Cat stat block, miniature cow appearance**, pending the DM's
   approval of the look. The form is the identity and most of this guide.
3. Need flight? Kief's [Fly](../../kief/k-32/) can go on Mr. Big for 10
   minutes, at the cost of a 3rd-level slot and Concentration.
4. The cow appearance adds no attacks, size, carrying capacity, or bovine
   abilities.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Recast tonight, and before anyone says 'owl': no. It's the
cow. If we need something airborne this week, I have Fly."

Verbally: *"The spell offers its menu the way it always does: wings, fangs,
eyes for the dark. The incense smoke settles, as it always settles, into the
small stubborn silhouette of a toy cow."*

### The rule underneath

Some choices in D&D can be revisited: familiar forms per casting, spells at
level-up. Knowing where you can change your build is system mastery.
Optimization is one input; identity is another, and spending a little
efficiency on it is a legitimate choice.
