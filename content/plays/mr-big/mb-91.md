"Mr. Big eats first." The bond, played to the letter.

### When to use it

- Every meal, every rations count, and every time someone asks whether the
  party really waits for a cow.

### Mechanics

1. The bond from the record: **Mr. Big eats first. My people all get home.**
   No exceptions for sieges or royalty.
2. Whether a Fey familiar needs food isn't settled by the spell. At this table
   it's irrelevant: he's served first.
3. Count him in the rations. His portion is small; his priority is not.
4. Daily grooming and a one-sided debrief. No mechanical effect. He permits
   it.
5. It's flavor. It never slows a combat turn.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Rations check includes Mr. Big. And before anyone eats
tonight: Mr. Big eats first. Yes, the duke can wait."

Verbally: *"Kings and sieges have waited on a small cow's supper. He accepts
this as the barest minimum of a functioning civilization, and eats, if he
eats, with his eyes closed, first."*

### The rule underneath

Personality traits, ideals, bonds, and flaws are the character's behavioral
contract, and the DM can build scenes from them. A bond that's visible and
testable at every meal is one the game can use. Consistency is what lets the
DM hand you scenes built for you.
