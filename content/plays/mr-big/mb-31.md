The party's silent channel, within 100 feet.

### When to use it

- The party splits inside 100 feet of Kief: entry team inside, Kief outside,
  cow with the entry team.

### Mechanics

1. Telepathy connects Kief and Mr. Big only. With a Bonus Action, Kief sees
   and hears what the team is doing through Mr. Big ([play 2](../mb-2/)).
2. Replies travel as physical signals agreed beforehand: sit (hold), circle
   (fall back), stare at a door (that one) ([play 35](../mb-35/)).
3. It needs no components or sound and passes through walls. It is strictly
   100 feet ([play 33](../mb-33/)).
4. Beyond that, fall back to the note run ([play 32](../mb-32/)) or the dead
   drop ([play 38](../mb-38/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Sable and Tam take the cellar; Mr. Big goes with them and I
hold the alley, inside a hundred feet. I hear what he hears. If I need them
to stop, he sits down. That's the signal."

Verbally: *"In the cellar, a cow sits down for no visible reason, and two
burglars freeze mid-step, because the man in the alley has seen something
they can't."*

### The rule underneath

A familiar's telepathy works only between caster and familiar, within 100
feet, with no action and no line of sight. Allies can't hear it. Building a
relay from one silent channel plus pre-agreed visible signals costs zero
actions and zero sound.
