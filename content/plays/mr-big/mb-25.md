A continuous feed, not a single glance.

### When to use it

- Mr. Big is moving through a space Kief needs to understand in detail: a
  floor plan, a crowded room, a route.
- Out of combat, when Bonus Actions aren't needed for anything else.

### Mechanics

1. Each round, Kief spends a **Bonus Action** to see and hear through Mr.
   Big until the start of his next turn. Out of combat, that's effectively
   continuous.
2. While sharing, Kief perceives through Mr. Big and still has his own
   senses. Tell the DM if Kief is also walking, talking, or watching.
3. Mr. Big must stay within 100 feet.
4. Kief does the interpretation: maps, counts, languages, faces.
5. In combat, every round spent looking is a Bonus Action not spent on
   Innate Sorcery or *Misty Step*.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I sit down behind the wagon and ride along with Mr. Big as
he walks the manor ground floor. Bonus Action each round. I'm sketching the
layout as he goes."

Verbally: *"Kief draws a floor plan with his eyes closed, pausing now and
then to correct a doorway, while the cow he's seeing through is two walls
and a garden away."*

### The rule underneath

"Until the start of your next turn" effects can be chained by renewing them
each turn. Outside combat, turns are loose, so a chained Bonus Action
behaves like a continuous feed. The limit returns in initiative, where the
Bonus Action has competition.
