Horses know things.

### When to use it

- Stables and picket lines: mounts are well-informed witnesses, and nobody
  guards the stable from cats.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big walks the stalls; Kief looks through him. Which horse is still
   sweating, muddy, or saddle-marked tonight?
2. Kief's [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/) takes the statement. Horses
   report concrete, recent, horse-relevant facts.
3. Whether horses are calm around him is the DM's call.
4. Before buying a horse, Kief and the cow can look it over. Kief has no
   Animal Handling proficiency; this is a look, not an assessment.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big does the stable while I hold the yard and look
through him. Stall three's horse is still lathered, and the innkeeper said
nobody rode out. Free cast: I go and ask the horse."

Verbally: *"The horses regard the tiny cow with the peaceable acceptance of
large animals for small ones, and by morning Kief has the yard's testimony:
who rode, who lied, and which mare bites."*

### The rule underneath

Speak with Animals works on Beasts only, and the DM caps what they know at
what a beast would notice. The ritual tag means 10 extra minutes and no
slot, so the free casts are for when time is short.
