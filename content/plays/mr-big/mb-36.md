Mr. Big finds the witnesses; Kief interviews them.

### When to use it

- Feeding Kief's [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/) network: the
  witnesses are rats, crows, and stable cats, and someone has to find the
  right one.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big can't talk to beasts. He can find them: the rats in the
   counting-house wall, the cat on the stable roof, the crows over the gate.
2. Kief's free casts last 10 minutes each (3 per Long Rest), or use the
   ritual. Having the witness located first saves the clock.
3. Whether an animal follows a cat, or runs from one, is the DM's call.
   Usually it's easier for Kief to walk to the witness.
4. Witnesses expect payment. Mr. Big eats first ([play 91](../mb-91/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big, find me where the rats go in and out of the
counting-house. Then I'll go and sit near the hole with a biscuit and cast
Speak with Animals."

Verbally: *"The interview room is a doorstep. The bailiff is a cow. The
witness is a rat with ink on its feet and a biscuit in its future."*

### The rule underneath

Speak with Animals works on Beasts only. Mr. Big is Fey, so the spell
doesn't let Kief talk to him, and it doesn't make animals trust a cat.
Creature type lines gate many effects; check them first.
