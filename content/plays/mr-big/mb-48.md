A deliberate lure, on the party's terms.

### When to use it

- Enemies that chase small prey (beasts, hungry creatures) and a prepared
  kill zone: [Web](../../kief/k-25/) across the corridor, caltrops, an
  ambush.

### Mechanics

1. Route first: the run ends through a gap only he fits or up a wall only he
   climbs. No escape route, no run.
2. He shows himself at the enemy's edge and runs the line at 40 feet a round,
   Dash in reserve. Pursuers reach the web or the crossfire.
3. Disciplined soldiers don't chase animals. This works on hunger and
   instinct; the DM decides who chases.
4. **Web timing**: an existing web restrains the cow too. Cast it after he's
   through, or route him around it.
5. Abort: Kief dismisses him to the pocket dimension ([play 85](../mb-85/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "The ghouls chase anything alive. Mr. Big shows himself at the
tunnel mouth and runs back past us and up the wall. When they're in the
corridor, I cast Web."

Verbally: *"Hunger has one thought, and it is running ahead of them, small
and unhurried, exactly as fast as it needs to be and not one foot faster."*

### The rule underneath

Movement on your turn is yours to route. Whether a creature chases has no
core rule; it's the DM's call about the creature's nature, so bait plans
are predictions. The trap itself is concrete: a save-or-Restrained effect
doesn't care why the victim walked in.
