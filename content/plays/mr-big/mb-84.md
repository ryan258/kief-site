Animal behavior as a channel the DM can use for weather, and for worse.

### When to use it

- Route planning, camp choices, and days the party debates tents versus
  stars.
- Only as a receiver. The DM decides what Mr. Big senses.

### Mechanics

1. Tell the DM: "if Mr. Big would behave oddly before bad weather or
   something wrong, show us." Agree a couple of signs: pacing = storm
   coming; hiding = storm now.
2. The DM may use it or not. Nothing on his stat block predicts weather.
3. He's a barometer, not an oracle: vague, sometimes wrong, and silent.
4. The party's own Survival and the sky stay in the forecast.

### At the table

Mechanically: "He's been pacing since noon and now he won't leave the lee of
the rocks. That's 'storm now' in our book. We camp here and rig for wind."

Verbally: *"The sky is blue and nobody sees anything. But every bird has left
the valley, and the little cow is standing very still, facing north."*

### The rule underneath

Exploration runs on the DM describing, players declaring, and the DM
narrating results. The DM can hand you information through the world:
animal behavior, weather, dread. Agreeing in advance how to read it is free
fiction that never needs a roll.
