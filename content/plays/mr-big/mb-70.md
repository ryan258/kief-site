When the cow won't go in, the party stops and looks. Then the party decides.

### When to use it

- Mr. Big balks at a door, a room, or a host.

### Mechanics

1. A balk is data. The DM may use animal behavior to show something the
   characters haven't noticed.
2. Protocol: stop, don't argue, investigate from outside: Kief's shared
   senses, [Detect Magic](../../kief/k-34/), the party's searcher.
3. If investigation finds nothing and the mission says go, the party decides.
   **Kief listens to the party when lives are at stake**; his faith in the cow
   is flavor, not a veto.
4. The drama can be fun. Keep it short.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big stops at the cellar door and won't cross. So we
stop. I look through his eyes; Tam checks the hinges; I ritual Detect Magic.
If it's clear and you all want to go, we go, and I'll apologize to the cow."

Verbally: *"The little cow plants himself at the threshold like a closed
gate. Kief kneels beside him. 'Noted,' he says. 'We'll check.'"*

### The rule underneath

The DM runs perception you don't have, and creature behavior is one way to
hand you a result without narrating "you sense danger." Character flaws are
real obligations, but the current build explicitly limits this one: Kief
listens to the party when lives are at stake.
