Range discipline: the geometry every Mr. Big play depends on.

### When to use it

- Constantly. Every play in this guide assumes the 100-foot range.

### Mechanics

1. **Telepathy**, **shared senses**, and **touch-spell delivery** all require
   Mr. Big within **100 feet** of Kief. Outside that, he acts on standing
   orders only.
2. Kief moves to serve the leash. During any cow operation, Kief's position
   is within 100 feet of the cow. Say so: "I'm holding the alley to stay in
   range."
3. Telepathy doesn't need line of sight, so a three-story building is often
   in range from the street. A keep isn't.
4. Recall from the pocket dimension brings him to within 30 feet of **Kief**
   ([play 85](../mb-85/)). It's not a way to reach him beyond range; he has to
   be dismissed first, which also happens at Kief's command.
5. Plan the return: a cow beyond range walks back.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The warehouse is ninety feet deep. I can run him from the
front door and keep contact the whole way. Tomorrow's keep is bigger, so
we'll agree his orders and his return trigger before he goes in."

Verbally: *"Kief drifts along the street like a man walking off a large
lunch, maintaining, with great precision, the exact radius of a secret."*

### The rule underneath

Every ability has a governing number: 100 feet for familiar telepathy and
touch delivery, 30 feet for recall. Whether a plan works is usually a
geometry fact decided before any dice. Know your radii the way a fighter
knows their reach.
