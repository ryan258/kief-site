The standard procedure: Mr. Big walks every unknown space before the party
does.

### When to use it

- Every dungeon corridor, building, camp, and alley whose far side the party
  hasn't seen.
- Once he's summoned. First-session summoning is still unconfirmed
  ([play 88](../mb-88/)).

### Mechanics

1. He advances 20–40 feet ahead at Stealth +4, hugging walls and shadow,
   stopping at corners and doorways.
2. Telepathy within **100 feet** carries his report: no action from Kief,
   no sound. If the party stops, he stops ([play 33](../mb-33/)).
3. Kief's **Bonus Action** shares his senses until the start of Kief's next
   turn when the report needs Kief's judgment.
4. What he's looking for, in order: creatures, light, exits, and anything
   unusual. He stops at danger and reports.
5. He is **not a trap detector**. A tripwire a cat walks under is still
   there for the fighter.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Order of march: Mr. Big thirty feet ahead, Stealth plus
four, stopping at each corner. He reports by telepathy, and I'll spend my
Bonus Action to look through his eyes at the junction."

Verbally: *"A very small cow steps into the dark first, as he always does.
The dungeon does not know what to make of him. Nothing ever has."*

### The rule underneath

A familiar acts independently but obeys Kief's commands. Out of combat the
DM narrates and routine activity needs no roll. Marching order is a real
exploration question: surprise and first contact resolve against whoever
is in front. Putting the perceptive, recoverable creature there is the
rules-legal answer.
