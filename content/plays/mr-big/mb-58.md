He has never made a sound. Treat that as a feature, and as a limit.

### When to use it

- Every stealth plan involving Mr. Big.
- Every plan that needs him to raise an alarm. He won't.

### Mechanics

1. Stealth +4 covers movement noise. Vocal noise is absent by character: no
   mew, no moo, no startled cry. The DM should respect that trait.
2. Grabbed, he stays silent. The person holding him has to raise the alarm
   themselves.
3. The flip side: he can't warn anyone who isn't Kief. Allies read his
   signals ([play 35](../mb-35/)) or hear from Kief.
4. Silence doesn't stop him being seen, or noticed by animals.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The guard grabs him and gets nothing. No squeal; the room
still doesn't know anything happened. Now it's one man holding a silent
animal, and we're already moving."

Verbally: *"Every animal in the world announces its capture. The thing in
the guard's hands announces nothing and watches him with dark, patient eyes
until he begins, quietly, to want to put it down."*

### The rule underneath

In 2024, hiding ends if you make noise louder than a whisper. A creature
established never to vocalize removes a failure mode the DM might otherwise
invoke. Established traits constrain adjudication, so agree on them before
they matter.
