Pits, shafts, and cellars, surveyed by the member with a climb speed.

### When to use it

- Any vertical unknown: the well, the collapsed stair, the shaft behind the
  grate.

### Mechanics

1. Climb 40 works downward too. He descends along the wall, pausing at
   ledges, reporting until the 100-foot telepathy limit.
2. **Darkvision 60**: in darkness he sees as if in dim light, which imposes
   Disadvantage on sight-based Perception. Beyond 60 feet, darkness is
   darkness.
3. What he brings back decides the rope work: depth to the first landing,
   water or dry, occupied or empty.
4. Abort trigger: anything alive down there bigger than he is, he climbs
   out.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before anyone rigs the rope: Mr. Big goes down the shaft
wall, Climb 40, reporting the whole way. How deep, water, and whether
anything down there moves."

Verbally: *"The well swallows the little cow without a sound. A minute
later Kief nods slowly. 'Forty feet. Dry. Something's been down there, but
not today.'"*

### The rule underneath

Falling deals 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet (max 20d6), which is why the
climb-speed creature scouts shafts and everyone else uses rope. Telepathy's
100-foot radius is a built-in tether: past it, no reports. Think of radii in
dungeon terms: 100 feet is about ten stories.
