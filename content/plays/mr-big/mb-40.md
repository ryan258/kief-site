Helping on checks, where the 2024 rules actually allow it.

### When to use it

- A party Perception or Stealth check with real stakes.

### Mechanics

1. Help on an ability check requires the helper to be **proficient** in the
   skill. Mr. Big's skills: **Perception** and **Stealth**.
2. The ally's check then has Advantage.
3. Help is an action. In combat, that's his turn. Out of combat, the DM
   decides how it plays out.
4. He has to plausibly contribute: present, aware, and doing something
   helpful. Describe how.
5. Other skills need the DM's generosity. Don't lean on it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tam's searching the treeline for the scouts and Mr. Big is
beside him, watching the other half. He's proficient in Perception, so by
the Help rules, Tam's check has Advantage."

Verbally: *"Man and cow scan the treeline in comfortable silence, each
privately convinced he's carrying the other."*

### The rule underneath

Proficiency is binary: you add your proficiency bonus or you don't. A stat
block's skill list is its proficiency list. Before claiming any bonus, name
its source; "where does that come from?" is the most rules-literate
question at any table.
