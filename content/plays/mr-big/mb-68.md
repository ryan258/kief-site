De-escalation by cow: give the room something absurd to look at.

### When to use it

- Standoffs among people who are posturing, not yet fighting.
- **Never** against frenzy, undead, beasts, or anyone already swinging.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big walks slowly into the middle of the tension and sits down.
2. The pause is Kief's opening: Persuasion (+6), warm, offering everyone a
   way out.
3. Whether the moment works, and whether it prevents initiative, is the DM's
   call.
4. Risk: he's briefly next to armed, angry people with 2 HP.
5. It works once per audience.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Swords are half-drawn. Mr. Big trots into the space between
the two groups and sits. Into that gap: 'Gentlemen. Nobody bleeds in front of
the cow.' Persuasion, plus six."

Verbally: *"Eleven armed men discover that there is no dignified way to
start a fight across a small seated cow, and someone, fatally for the
fight's prospects, laughs."*

### The rule underneath

Combat starts when the DM calls for initiative, and whether it starts is
adjudication of the fiction. Before combat, one well-timed check can end
the conflict entirely. The best action economy is a fight that never gets
past zero rounds.
