The old guide leaned on a nose Mr. Big no longer has. Route those questions
elsewhere.

### When to use it

- Any question Mr. Big's current senses can't answer.

### Mechanics

1. **Smell and tracking**: ask a local dog through
   [Speak with Animals](../../kief/k-21/) ([play 30](../mb-30/)).
2. **Magic**: [Detect Magic](../../kief/k-34/), ritual when time allows.
3. **Lies and motives**: Kief's Insight +3, or the party's best reader
   ([Kief play 55](../../kief/k-55/)).
4. **Traps and mechanisms**: the party's best searcher with the Search or
   Study action. Not a 2-HP cat.
5. **Writing, maps, symbols**: Kief looks through Mr. Big's eyes and reads it
   himself.
6. **What's in the cup**: nobody at this table tastes it on a familiar's say-so.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big can't tell us if the wine's poisoned. Detect Magic
won't either unless it's magical. Does anyone have a better way, or do we
just not drink it?"

Verbally: *"Mr. Big regards the wine, then Kief, with the look of a colleague
who has been asked to work outside his department."*

### The rule underneath

A familiar is a set of specific senses and actions, not a universal
detector. Traits removed from a stat block take their tactics with them.
Every question has a best tool; asking the wrong one returns confident
nonsense.
