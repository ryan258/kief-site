Scouting without a light source, which is the only way to scout a dark room
quietly.

### When to use it

- Unlit cellars, caves, and night-time grounds, when a torch would announce
  the party.

### Mechanics

1. **Darkvision 60**: within 60 feet, darkness is dim light and dim light is
   bright, in shades of grey.
2. Dim light is Lightly Obscured: **Disadvantage on sight-based Perception**.
   His +3 is weaker in the dark than it looks.
3. No colors in darkness. Report shapes and movement, not banners and
   uniforms.
4. Kief also has Darkvision 60. Shared senses add vantage, not vision.
5. Beyond 60 feet, darkness is darkness for both of them. A distant lit
   window is visible; what's between isn't.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big goes into the dark cellar, no light. Darkvision 60,
so dim-light rules: he has Disadvantage on the Perception check. What
shapes does he see?"

Verbally: *"The cellar is black to everyone but a small cow, to whom it is
merely grey, and to whom grey has always been sufficient."*

### The rule underneath

Darkvision turns darkness into dim light within range, not into daylight.
Lightly Obscured areas impose Disadvantage on Perception checks that rely on
sight. That's the difference between "can see" and "sees well," and it's why
Darkvision scouts still miss things.
