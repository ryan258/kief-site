A man traveling with a well-kept tiny cow is a man people decide to trust.

### When to use it

- First impressions with landlords, clients, and checkpoints, where Kief's
  trustworthiness is being judged.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big's evident good health and good manners suggest a gentle owner. The
   DM decides how much that matters.
2. Context for Persuasion (+6). Describe the scene; let the DM price it.
3. It compounds with reputation: "the one with the little cow" is how towns
   remember Kief.
4. The mirror risk: the cow makes Kief **memorable**. When the party needs not
   to be remembered, pocket him ([play 85](../mb-85/)).

### At the table

Mechanically: "The landlady's deciding whether we're trouble. Mr. Big sits
up in the satchel and regards her politely. I mention he doesn't care for
loud lodgers either. Persuasion."

Verbally: *"The landlady has turned away sellswords and a duke's son this
season. She looks at the small cow looking politely back at her and hears
herself say the room at the top is quiet."*

### The rule underneath

2024 avoids stacking numeric circumstance bonuses: there's no +2 for a nice
cow. The currencies are attitude, DC, and Advantage, all adjudicated from
the fiction. Reputation and visible trust signals tilt many future checks at
once.
