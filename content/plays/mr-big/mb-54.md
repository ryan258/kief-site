Listen to the meeting before the meeting.

### When to use it

- A known time-and-place conversation the party can't attend.

### Mechanics

1. Place Mr. Big before the principals arrive: rafter (Climb 40), shelf, or
   clutter ([play 23](../mb-23/)).
2. **Live listening requires Kief within 100 feet**: next room, floor above,
   street outside, using a Bonus Action each round ([play 22](../mb-22/)).
   Kief understands the words.
3. Beyond 100 feet, he's a cat with a memory: tone, faces, maybe a
   repeated name. Not a transcript.
4. He leaves after the room empties, never during.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The meeting's at the Boar at midnight, private room. Mr. Big
is on the beam over the table since sundown. I'm in the taproom below, forty
feet away. Bonus Action each round they're talking."

Verbally: *"Six conspirators check the room and lock the door, alone at
last. Above the lamp, where the beam meets the wall, the seventh attendee
settles in to take the minutes."*

### The rule underneath

The rules have no memory system; what a witness retains is the DM's call,
scaled to Intelligence. Listening live through a familiar upgrades the
recorder to the caster's own understanding. Match the listener's mind to the
information's complexity.
