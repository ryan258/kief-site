Negotiation seating for two: the gnome, and the senior partner.

### When to use it

- Formal negotiations where a thinking pause and an unusual presence help
  Kief.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big sits beside Kief, facing the counterparty.
2. The [consultation](../mb-5/): before answering a hard term, Kief looks to
   the cow. The pause buys thinking time. What it does to the other side is
   the DM's call.
3. Kief looks through Mr. Big with a Bonus Action if aides whisper at a side
   table.
4. Etiquette: in some rooms an animal on the table ends the deal. Read the
   room (Insight +3, History +2) first; the sideboard works too.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Contract session: Mr. Big sits beside me. Before I answer
the exclusivity clause, I consult the cow. Long pause. Then my answer, and
Persuasion if it's needed."

Verbally: *"The Consortium's factor has negotiated against princes. He has
never negotiated against a gnome who checks every clause with a tiny cow."*

### The rule underneath

Social encounters have no initiative: pacing belongs to whoever structures
the scene. Pauses and presence are legal tools, and the DM tracks their
effect through attitude and Advantage. The rules don't referee etiquette;
learn the setting's customs first.
