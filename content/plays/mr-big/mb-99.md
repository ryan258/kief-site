Every escape has a passenger list. Write the cow on it before the crisis.

### When to use it

- Any day with retreat risk, and every time [Kief's retreat
  script](../../kief/k-70/) might run.

### Mechanics

1. **Misty Step**: Kief only. **Feather Fall**: up to five falling creatures
   within 60 feet, Mr. Big included. **Pocket dimension**: one cow, one Magic
   action.
2. Pocket him **early**, while the action is cheap. A dismissal during a
   collapse costs Kief the turn the retreat needed.
3. The failure mode to avoid: the party flees, the cow is left beyond 100 feet
   in whatever they fled, running on stale orders.
4. If it happens anyway: he's expendable by design ([play 52](../mb-52/)). The
   worst case costs incense and an hour.
5. Say the plan aloud once: "if we run, the cow goes in the pocket first."

### At the table

Mechanically: "Today has a bad-exit shape, so the checklist: Mr. Big does his
recon early, and the moment we commit, Magic action, he's in the pocket. If
we have to run, nobody's looking for a cow."

Verbally: *"The tower is coming down and the party is running, and nowhere in
the collapsing dark is there a small abandoned shape, because an hour earlier,
unhurried, Kief filed his oldest friend somewhere the tower couldn't reach."*

### The rule underneath

Movement and teleportation effects say exactly whom they move. Escape options
are capacity-limited transport, and party survival is seat assignment done in
advance. For every escape on the sheet, know its range, its passengers, and
its action cost.
