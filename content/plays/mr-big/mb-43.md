The repair cantrip, delivered where hands can't go.

### When to use it

- The broken thing is across a gap, behind bars, up a wall, or somewhere only
  a Tiny creature fits.

### Mechanics

1. *Mending*: touch, **1-minute** casting time. Kief casts within 100 feet;
   Mr. Big touches the break and delivers it with his Reaction. Ask the DM
   how Reaction delivery works with a 1-minute cast; most tables allow it
   out of combat.
2. One break or tear, no larger than 1 foot in any dimension.
3. Uses: a snapped chain link, a torn rope splice, a cracked lock plate.
4. Not a combat tool. Plan it into the timeline.
5. Kief needs the spell's material component: a component pouch or two
   lodestones. His Sorcerer focus does not cover Wizard Magic Initiate
   *Mending*. Confirm the gear before promising the repair.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We're through the grate. I cast Mending, one minute; Mr. Big
is on the other side touching the cut bar. Do I have my pouch or lodestones?
Is the cut small enough for one Mending?"

Verbally: *"Behind the departing party, a small cow touches the cut bar, and
the metal remembers being whole."*

### The rule underneath

Casting times longer than an action change a spell's role: a 1-minute
cantrip is a scene tool. Its effect text is precise (one break or tear, a
foot or less), so size up the damage before promising a repair.
