Climb 40 turns every wall into a patrol route.

### When to use it

- Mapping a compound, keep, or town block from above before the party
  chooses an approach.
- Ongoing overwatch during an operation below.

### Mechanics

1. **Climb Speed 40**: walls, drainpipes, ivy, and rooflines cost him no
   extra movement. Route him slowly at Stealth +4, pausing at vantage
   points.
2. Report by telepathy within 100 feet. For large compounds, plan loops that
   return inside range, or station Kief centrally.
3. Deliverables, in order: entrances, sentries and their sight lines, light
   sources, dogs ([play 28](../mb-28/)), and the unguarded roof access.
4. He sees and counts roughly. Kief's shared senses turn impressions into a
   plan.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big goes up the drainpipe, Climb 40, Stealth plus four,
and runs a circuit of the roofline. Entrances, sentries, lights, and dogs,
in that order."

Verbally: *"High above the courtyard, a tiny cow picks along the ridgeline
with the unhurried confidence of the only creature in the county that
cannot fall off anything."*

### The rule underneath

Speeds are separate movement budgets. A creature with a Climb Speed moves
on vertical surfaces without the extra cost everyone else pays (1 extra
foot per foot, and sometimes Athletics checks). Read the speed line first;
it tells you where a creature moves freely.
