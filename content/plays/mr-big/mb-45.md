The cow calls **when**. Kief chooses **where**.

### When to use it

- Kief can see the chokepoint but not the approach to it.

### Mechanics

1. Read the specific spell: not every area spell requires a visible point.
   A clear path to the target and range still matter. Shared-sense targeting
   remains a table ruling; Mr. Big does not become the spell’s origin.
2. Setup: Kief sights the gate arch. Mr. Big watches the road Kief can't
   see. Telepathy counts down: *coming, close, now*.
3. Works for any sighted effect: *Web* when the column bunches in the
   doorway, *Hypnotic Pattern* when the last one steps into the cube.
4. Kief waits with the **Ready** action if the moment comes on the enemy's
   turn. A readied spell uses the slot when readied and needs Concentration
   to hold, so Kief's current Concentration spell would end.
5. Or keep it simple: Mr. Big's warning lets the party act first on its own
   turns.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I can see the gate arch; I can't see the road. Mr. Big
watches the road. I ready Fireball on the arch: when he says the column is
in it, I release."

Verbally: *"The raiders never see the tower window. The window, via a small
associate on the wall, has been watching them for half a mile."*

### The rule underneath

Sight requirements are spell-specific. Familiar reports can inform your
aim and timing; they do not bypass total cover or relocate your casting. Readying a spell costs
your action, your Reaction when released, and Concentration while held,
and the slot is spent even if the trigger never comes.
