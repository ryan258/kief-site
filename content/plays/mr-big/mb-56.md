Get through the search clean, and still have the cow inside.

### When to use it

- Checkpoints, palace security, parley grounds: anywhere the party is
  searched and disarmed.

### Mechanics

1. **Clean option**: Kief dismisses Mr. Big to the pocket dimension before
   the search ([play 85](../mb-85/)), walks through with nothing to find, and
   recalls him to within 30 feet once inside. Both are Magic actions.
2. **Risky option**: carry him as a toy in the satchel. A warm, breathing
   animal is not a carved toy if anyone handles it. The DM decides how a
   search goes.
3. Inside, the party has a scout, a courier ([play 32](../mb-32/)), and a
   lookout nobody confiscated.
4. Kief without a focus: Subtle Spell removes unpriced material components,
   and some spells need none ([Kief play 38](../../kief/k-38/)).
5. The recall is visible magic if anyone watches. Do it somewhere private.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before the gate: Magic action, Mr. Big goes to the pocket
dimension. They search us and find nothing. In the guest room, Magic action,
he's back beside me."

Verbally: *"The guards search the gnome with great thoroughness and find a
satchel of tea, a patched robe, and no cow at all. Later, in a quiet room, a
cow is found to be present after all."*

### The rule underneath

A familiar is a creature, not equipment, and the pocket-dimension clause
lets it skip the search entirely. Searches have no codified rule; the DM
adjudicates what "thorough" finds. Know which capabilities live in your gear
and which live in your person.

**Cargo reminder:** Worn and carried items remain in Mr. Big’s space when he enters the pocket dimension or disappears at 0 HP. Retrieve them separately. See [shared table rulings](/rulings/).
