Get inside at closing time; be the only one inside by midnight.

### When to use it

- Shops, archives, and guildhalls that are public by day and locked at
  night.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big enters during open hours (with Kief, or unnoticed), finds a still
   corner ([play 9](../mb-9/)), and doesn't leave.
2. At night: a slow survey of the layout and what's guarded. With Kief
   outside within 100 feet, the survey streams live through shared senses.
3. He's poorly built for latches and handles; what he can manipulate is the
   DM's call. His product is information and position, not burglary.
4. Exit: the morning opening, a gap he found, or recall via pocket dismissal
   once Kief is close ([play 85](../mb-85/)).
5. Ethics and consequences: this is trespass. The party decides whether the
   target warrants it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We visit the archive at noon like honest people. When we
leave, we're one cow lighter. Tonight I stand in the alley, inside a hundred
feet, and look through him room by room."

Verbally: *"The archivist locks up with his usual care: door, window,
window, door. From the top of the map cabinet, the building's newest fixture
watches the keys turn with great interest."*

### The rule underneath

Creatures act within their anatomy, and the DM adjudicates what a body can
manipulate. Waiting has no cost in the rules, and passive Perception keeps
working the whole time. Infiltration by patience is free; infiltration by
force rolls dice at every step.
