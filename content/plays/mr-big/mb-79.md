Kief and Mr. Big can see in the dark. Some of the party can't.

### When to use it

- The party must move without light, and some members lack Darkvision.

### Mechanics

1. Mr. Big walks ahead in Darkvision 60; Kief, also with Darkvision 60,
   leads the party and relays what matters in a whisper.
2. Allies without Darkvision are effectively Blinded in darkness: they move
   carefully, holding a shoulder or a rope.
3. Both Darkvision users see darkness as dim light: Disadvantage on
   sight-based Perception.
4. Consider whether darkness is worth it. [Light](../../kief/k-9/) covered
   in a pouch can be uncovered instantly.

### At the table

Mechanically: "No light. Mr. Big goes ahead; I follow with Tam's hand on my
shoulder, and I whisper steps and turns. Anyone without Darkvision is
holding the rope."

Verbally: *"The party crosses the black hall in a slow chain, led by a gnome
who can see a little and a cow who can see a little more, whispering about
a step nobody else can see."*

### The rule underneath

Darkness is Heavily Obscured, which effectively Blinds anyone without
Darkvision: attacks against them have Advantage, and their own have
Disadvantage. Darkvision users see it as dim light. A party is only as
stealthy in the dark as its least-sighted member is careful.
