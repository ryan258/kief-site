Kief's Bonus Action turns the cow into a remote sensor.

### When to use it

- Mr. Big's telepathic report needs checking with Kief's own judgment.
- Watching a door, hallway, or prisoner in another room while Kief stays at
  the table.

### Mechanics

1. **Bonus Action** (Kief's), Mr. Big within 100 feet: Kief sees through Mr.
   Big's eyes and hears what he hears until the start of Kief's next turn,
   gaining any special senses Mr. Big has.
2. In combat it shares the turn with a full Action: look, then cast. Out of
   combat, renew it for a continuous feed.
3. The senses are the cat's: low vantage, Darkvision 60, Perception +3.
   Kief sees what Mr. Big sees, not what he understands.
4. The Bonus Action competes with Innate Sorcery, *Misty Step*, and Font of
   Magic in a fight. Look before the fight.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big's at the door, eighty feet from me. Bonus Action: I
look through his eyes until my next turn. What's in that room?"

Verbally: *"Kief's eyes go pleasantly unfocused, the look of a man
remembering good weather. Somewhere down the hall, a tiny cow is doing his
seeing for him."*

### The rule underneath

You get one Bonus Action per turn, and only when a rule grants one, like
*Find Familiar*'s sense-sharing clause. "Until the start of your next turn"
covers everyone else's turns but ends when yours begins, so renew it each
round. Kief's own Darkvision already equals Mr. Big's; the value is the
vantage, not the vision.
